# Season 1 — "Nobody's Parcel" (10 episodes, 30–40 min each)

Draft v1.0, showrunner. Guests and places are provisional until the character bank (rights)
and footage audit come back; any guest who fails rights is swapped for the next best fit.

**Season question:** what is in the parcel, and who is Jax?
**Season shape:** Moon → Mars → Venus → Sun → Belt → Jupiter → Saturn. Each hop costs more
than the last. Crew joins one at a time. The rat is planted in ep 2 and breaks in ep 10.

## 1. The Parcel Nobody Ordered
**Cold open:** the ship is repossessed while Jax is still flying it.
**A:** To clear the lien, Jax takes a sealed parcel without reading the address. On approach to
the lunar south pole the address is delisted in front of them. Customs seizes the port. A
ridiculous old man in a tricorn hat insists he is Baron Munchausen and that he used to be
taller. Nobody believes him — and his stories have stopped coming true.
**B:** Mira Sen, a struck-off cartographer working the customs desk, recognises the deletion
pattern and has to choose between her last job and being right. Lucía wants her wages.
**C:** A buyer offers more for the parcel than the ship is worth. Jax picks the old man over the money.
**Turns:** address vanishes · the Baron proves one impossible thing · buyer's offer · Jax gives
up the fee, they run the crater rim in permanent shadow.
**Tag:** the warrant lists the crew by name. Under "Captain" the registry says: *nobody*.
**Changes:** fugitives. Mira aboard. The Baron stows away. Voss knows the parcel has moved.

## 2. The Princess of the Far Side
**A:** Hiding where no signal reaches, the crew find the Moon's oldest resident: Kaguya, who
once wore a robe that made her forget everyone she loved on Earth. Enforcement jams the only
relay and settles in to wait them out.
**B:** A calm pilot named Seo talks her way aboard and saves the ship twice. Lucía doesn't trust
anyone that useful. Jax and Kaguya share an evening of two people who can't remember what they miss.
**C:** Kaguya recognises thinning in Jax and won't say what she sees.
**Turns:** relay dies · Seo's flying · Kaguya offers the robe as a way out of the siege — at a
price · escape through the L2 halo.
**Tag:** Seo files her first report to Auditor Pell. The audience is now ahead of the crew.
**Changes:** Seo aboard (the rat). First clue about Jax. Commander Arakawa is assigned the case.

## 3. The Knight of the Red Planet
**A:** The invoice points to Mars. La Mancha Station has been delisted and its knight has gone
sane: Don Quixote sees windmills, files tax returns, and is dying of it. Voss surveyors arrive
to buy the wreck. To get the next coordinate the crew must make an old man mad again — across
four thousand kilometres of canyon, with dust devils he once called giants.
**B:** Idris al-Rahhal loses to Jax at cards and is won as a navigator. He and Sancho compete
over who serves the bigger fool. Mira hates that the cure is a lie.
**C:** A stray dog attaches itself to Jax and will not be left behind.
**Turns:** sane Quixote · Voss's offer to Sancho · the charge at the dust devil · Olympus Mons at dawn.
**Tag:** Ilse Voss watches the footage. "He chose the old man again. Find out why."
**Changes:** Idris aboard. The dog. First legend un-thinned — proof it can be reversed.

## 4. The Phobos Exception
**A:** Veda Rao, the last unedited memory of the Atlas, is held on Phobos — which by an ancient
clerical accident is exempt from every law, including the one that lets you leave. The only man
who ever beat the system is Nasreddin, who has lived in the waiting room for eleven years out of spite.
**B:** PIP meets a bureaucracy more literal than he is and falls in something like love.
Seo is ordered to make sure Veda does not get out.
**C:** Pell and Arakawa, in person, being reasonable.
**Turns:** form requires its own approval · Nasreddin's lesson · Seo's order · PIP files the one
report nobody can refuse.
**Tag:** Veda looks at Jax for a long time. "I have memorised every name in the Atlas. Yours is missing."
**Changes:** Veda aboard. Seo disobeys for the first time, in a way she can still explain.

## 5. A Gentleman on Venus
**A:** The next fragment is in Soren Voss's private collection, shown once a year at a gala in
the Venus cloud tops. They need a thief. The best one alive is already there to steal the
same thing. Scheherazade owns the room and is keeping a dangerous guest listening.
**B:** Jax and Mira dance as cover and forget it is cover. Idris and Seo lie to each other all
night and enjoy it far too much.
**C:** Jax meets Voss. Voss is delightful. Voss already knows what Jax takes in his tea.
**Turns:** the plan · Lupin's counter-plan · the double-cross is itself a double · fifty
kilometres up with nowhere to stand.
**Tag:** Voss lets them go. "Follow the parcel. He'll take it home for us."
**Changes:** fragment won, Lupin owed a favour, Voss now steering them. Jax–Mira is real.

## 6. Too Close to the Sun
**A:** A sabotaged gate drops the ship sunward of Mercury with dead engines. In the same decaying
orbit: a tender run by two old flyers who once went too high — Icarus, who did it for joy, and
Sampati, who burned his wings shielding his brother. One of them wants to try again.
**B:** Lucía and Mira, engineer and scientist, fix the unfixable and finally talk. Veda tells
the story that keeps everyone awake.
**C:** Someone on board sabotaged the gate. Someone on board also sent the distress call that
saves them. Both were Seo.
**Turns:** engines die · the heat clock · the old flyers' quarrel · the slingshot.
**Tag:** Seo deletes the log of her own rescue signal.
**Changes:** the crew knows there is a saboteur. Suspicion lands on Idris.

## 7. The Spider's Share
**A:** Home for Lucía: Ceres, where the Voss water company owns every drop and her mother's
debt. Anansi, who owns all the stories and has mislaid a few, offers a con big enough to drown
the company in its own contracts. Fast rise, faster fall.
**B:** Idris, under suspicion, works twice as hard to be trusted and it looks twice as guilty.
Mattias Voss, the unhappy younger son, makes contact: he wants out and will pay in secrets.
**C:** The crew now has its own rat. Neither rat knows about the other.
**Turns:** the web · the money rolls in · Anansi's real game · Lucía chooses the town over the score.
**Tag:** Pell to Seo: "They have someone inside the family. Find out who."
**Changes:** Ceres free of one debt and in for worse. Two moles hunting each other.

## 8. Havoc Under the Mountain
**A:** Mattias's first secret: Voss keeps his most dangerous acquisition under a volcano on Io.
Sun Wukong signed for a grand title with no power in it, and the small print put a mountain on
him. Breaking him out means crossing Jupiter's radiation belts with Arakawa's whole fleet behind.
**B:** Seo flies the impossible run and has to decide how well. Jax and the Monkey recognise
something in each other and neither likes it.
**C:** Arakawa corners them — and lets a lifeboat go. She has started reading the file she was told not to.
**Turns:** the contract · the breakout · the Red Spot chase · the cost of the escape.
**Tag:** Wukong, leaving: "You've forgotten more than a name, Captain. Ask the archivist what a blank looks like."
**Changes:** Voss's vault is open. Arakawa is in doubt. A crew member is badly hurt.

## 9. Murder on the Nautilus
**A:** They shelter under Europa's ice with Captain Nemo, who has hidden from empires for a
century. A Directorate auditor aboard is found dead in a sealed cabin. Nemo is the obvious
killer. The only other guest is a consulting detective who is thinning and knows it.
**B:** Everyone has a secret and Holmes takes them apart one by one: Idris's debt, Mira's
doubt, Veda's missing name. The crew sees each other properly for the first time.
**C:** Holmes solves the murder and, privately, the rat. He tells only Seo. "I know. The
interesting question is whether you do."
**Turns:** the body · Nemo accused · Holmes falters, Mira lends him her method · the solution nobody wanted.
**Tag:** Mattias has gone silent. Soren Voss sends an invitation to dinner.
**Changes:** the crew is whole just in time to be broken. Seo has one episode left to choose.

## 10. A Seat at the Table
**A:** Saturn. The Voss family at home. Dinner is perfect. Every debt of the season is on the
table: Lucía's mother, Idris's marker, Veda's memory, Mattias in the next room. Voss wants one
thing — the parcel delivered by the only courier who could find the address.
**B:** Seo is exposed. Then she chooses. It costs her everything she believed in.
**C:** The parcel is opened: a living piece of olive wood. The label, restored: *Nobody, Ithaca.*
The audience understands who has been narrating. Jax understands only that Voss owns the
rights to whoever he used to be.
**Turns:** the offer · the betrayal · the cavalry (a Baron, on a cannonball, as promised in ep 1) · the price.
**Tag:** a woman at a loom, far away, stops weaving and looks up.
**Changes:** the crew is a family and a target. Season 2: steal back a man's own story.
