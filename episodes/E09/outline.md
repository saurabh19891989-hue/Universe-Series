## 1. Logline and why you keep watching

**Logline:** Hiding under the ice of Europa aboard Captain Nemo's submarine, the crew wake to
find a Directorate auditor dead in a cabin sealed from the inside, with one word written on the
glass, "NOBODY", which is the Latin for their host's name and the word on their captain's warrant;
and the only detective aboard is Sherlock Holmes, who has started to miss things, and knows it.

**Why you keep watching:** A sealed room in a sealed ship under a sealed sea, with nineteen hours
on the clock before the tide opens the ice and Nemo dives for good with everyone aboard. Three
people on the ship are called Nobody. The great detective walks into the room and solves a
different one, in front of everybody. Every member of the crew lied about last night, and each lie
turns out to be the kindest or the most frightened thing about them. The ship's literal-minded
computer gives the correct solution at minute three, and nobody listens for nineteen hours. And
the audience watches the pilot hand the victim a small grey tin, and has to wait to find out what
it was.

**The solution (showrunner and critic: this is the whole design).** Nobody aboard killed her.
Auditor Thandiwe Maseko had audited the Atlas and found that forty-one "withdrawals at the
owner's request" were all entered at one desk, Auditor Pell's, each three days after a Voss
contract. She asked him about it, as an honest auditor does. Pell's reply was to send her to find
Nemo (who never lets a finder leave), and then, when Seo reported that the crew were heading for
the same harbour, to order Seo to hand the Auditor her own sealed wallet: a **Form Nine**, the
sealed orders every undercover officer carries for "an officer in unlisted custody". In the
service it is called **a Courageous**. Its instructions: seal the room, cast away the cover, read
alone, burn. The paper is the weapon; burned in a sealed cabin with the vent shut, it fouls the
air in seconds. Maseko, who never met a regulation she couldn't follow, followed it exactly. In
her last ten seconds she understood, laughed, said the last word of every Directorate order
("courageous"), and, knowing that a vessel on which an officer dies in custody **lapses** to the
Directorate (and that Voss already had the prospectus printed), spent her one word not on her
murderer but on the people he meant to hang: NOBODY A[BOARD]. Seo did not know what she was
carrying. She has carried her own death in her jacket since Episode 2.
**Who benefits:** Pell (his auditor is silenced) and Voss (the Nautilus lapses, and Nemo is
relisted as a jolly European eccentric with no family and no cause: the English translation).
**What she was carrying:** about Pell, the desk column; about Jax, the one withdrawal with no
desk, no order and no fee ("ask the singer"); about Nemo, his own file with every deletion marked
in her margins; about the parcel, a question for PIP about one working day, unasked.

## 5. Act breaks (each makes the next act a different story)

- **Cold open out:** a body, a word, a clock. Act 1 is a hunt for a detective and a room.
- **Act 1 out (6):** "Four of yours are false." The crew arrived as witnesses; Act 2 takes them apart.
- **Act 2 out (13):** the audience learns what Seo carried; Holmes is certain, and wrong. Act 3 is the fall of a legend and what a scientist does about it.
- **Act 3 out (19):** the missing cover, issued to a name that is nobody's and one person's. Act 4 is a solution with no murderer in the room.
- **Tag (28):** the villain who was never aboard has already filed; and the other one sends a dinner invitation.

## 6. What it costs

- **A woman is dead**, and she was the honest one. Her findings can be filed nowhere.
- **Nemo** loses the only peace an empire ever gave him: for a century he was forgotten; tonight he is wanted for murder, and the Nautilus has "lapsed" on paper. The truth does not stop the filing.
- **Jax** loses the story he told himself: nobody did it to him. He handed in his own name.
- **Mira** is caught, by the method she lives by, having refused evidence; her lie to Jax is out.
- **Idris** loses the secret that let him be kind without being watched; and reads the tab.
- **Seo** learns her service kills its own with a form, and that she carried hers for seven episodes. She was the hand.
- **Holmes** is still thinning. He solved it with help, and he stays below, where there is little to notice.
- **PIP** loses the only colleague he has had, and the question she meant to ask him.

## 8. Running gags, escalated

1. **PIP's reports and who reads them.** Under kilometres of ice nothing sends: for the first time
   every report is QUEUED — NO SIGNAL, and PIP is writing to nobody. His first report of the day
   (#1,266: "the deceased states that nobody did it") is the correct solution of the murder, and
   the world's greatest detective tells him the dying don't write riddles. Nineteen hours later
   Holmes orders it recorded "that Sherlock Holmes took nineteen hours to agree with a lamp", and
   PIP opens a new category: Vindication (#1,267). On the surface the queue sends and is READ in
   1.3 seconds as always, including #1,268, the true cause of death: "nobody read me, and one man
   believed me. I preferred it." New this episode: PIP meets a host who has logged every door for
   a hundred years. "Are you hiring?"
2. **Nobody can remember Chichikov.** He is not in the story at all, which is fair play. In the tag

## 9. The scientist and the detective (the episode's spine)

Holmes's side: certainty is a gift, and for forty years he was right, so he called it method.
Thinning takes the gift and leaves the habit: he walks into a room and recites the most famous
room he ever solved. Mira's side: evidence first, the list before the story, write down what is
absent, say what would prove you wrong; it is slower and nobody claps. She gives him back his own
printed rule (a capital mistake to theorise before the data) and adds hers. He gives her what
she lacks: she "will not say 'certainly' about a sheet of ice" and yet was sure enough of a man
to throw his history into a cloud. She has no number for that, and by 23 she has stopped waiting
for one. He ends by refusing, in front of everyone, to say what he cannot prove ("a desk, not a
hand"), which is her method used to keep a secret. That is how the spy stays private.
