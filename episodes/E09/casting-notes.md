# E09 "Murder on the Nautilus" — casting notes for the art department

Style of `canon/CHARACTER-LOCKS.md`: one locked description per new face, set and prop, to be
used verbatim in prompts with the house style line from `production/IMAGE-PIPELINE.md`.
Everything is photoreal, bright and readable. **This episode's rule: luminous, never murky.** No
sunlight reaches the ocean, so every frame is lit by the ship's own lamps (brass, with green and
amber glass), by floodlamps on the ice, or by a drifting blue-green glow in the water that nobody
explains. Faces are always clearly lit. Status of every row: PROPOSED (no master yet; owner's
casting approval needed for the three faces).

## 2. The Nautilus (exterior)

| Id | What | Locked description |
|---|---|---|
| nautilus | The Nautilus, exterior | A very long spindle-shaped submarine, a cylinder tapering to a point at each end, hull of overlapping steel plates with brass bands and fittings, weathered and immaculate; a long glass gallery along the top of the hull glowing warm from within; rows of round lit ports; a flat deck aft with brass clamps, on which the small patched courier ship *The Unscheduled* sits clamped like something it has caught; four floodlamps throwing light up onto an ice ceiling. Original design after Verne's text (an elongated cylinder with conical ends) |

**Never** the 1954 film's submarine (no saw-toothed dorsal ridge, no "eye" ports, no ram teeth,
no fish or monster silhouette) and no other screen Nautilus. No letter N on anything (the
monogram is the most copied design element; we leave it off).

## 5. Real footage and its gaps

Europa is THIN (`research/locations/12-europa-ganymede.md`). The episode uses real plates in 27,
the tag window and "What was real" only.

- **Logged now in `assets/candidates.json`:** Voyager 1's Europa (A79-7021, NASA); Juno's view of Io and Europa (PIA25014, NASA/JPL-Caltech/SwRI/MSSS, image processing credit required on that item); Hubble rotations of Jupiter (heic1914c, heic2017a; NASA, ESA, Hubble; CC BY 4.0, full credit line); the Hubble three-moon transit (heic1504a) if a moon crossing Jupiter is wanted.
- **Not yet logged, and needed:** NASA/JPL Galileo close-ups of Europa's ridges and of the Conamara Chaos region (the "rafts" of ice). Log them, with source URL, exact credit and licence, before any shot in 27 is built on them. Until then 27 can be cut from the Voyager and Juno stills.
- **Do not use:** the Clipper artist's concepts and the plume animations as if they were observed; citizen-processed JunoCam images whose pages state no licence.
- **Composites:** Jupiter is inserted by us into a pure-black sky (the model redraws planets); never generate Jupiter or Europa's surface texture.
- **No NASA or ESA logo** on the Nautilus, the armour or anything fictional.

## 6. Sound and music notes for the composer (not for pictures)

Solo violin for Holmes: one long note that breaks off (0, 1); three notes and a stop (15).
Pipe organ for Nemo, and the ice's groan is the organ's lowest pipe played by nobody (18, 19).
Sonar pings as percussion and as scene punctuation. The two instruments are never in the same
key until the hatch closes in 26. Oyuna's song in 22 is sung, low, in the epic voice, composed
with the E04 song and checked by a performer of the tradition; no organ or violin under it.
Agents cannot hear any of this: send the owner a listening reel.
