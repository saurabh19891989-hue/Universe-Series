# E09 "Murder on the Nautilus" — sources

Seven sources from five traditions (British, French with an Indian hero, Greek, Indian, Korean),
plus one piece of history. Everything is taken from the books and old tellings as read in the
Story Library (`research/library/sources/`), never from a film, series, game or musical. **Nothing
is retold scene by scene: the plot (an auditor killed by her own sealed orders, the word on the
glass, the tin on the ice ceiling) is ours.** Our dialogue is our own throughout; nobody speaks a
line of Doyle's or Verne's.

**How to read "Checked".** *Card* = taken from the library card, whose writer read the named
edition. *Memory* = the passage is not on a library card; I have used it from memory of the text
and **it must be checked against the edition named before lock**. Nothing here was re-read from
the primary text in this session.

## 8. History (not a literary source)

Nemo's line that "when a prince died without an heir the Company approved, his kingdom lapsed to
the Company. Jhansi went so" refers to the East India Company's doctrine of lapse under
Dalhousie, under which Jhansi (in Bundelkhand) was annexed in 1853–54. It is the model for our
invented Directorate rule that a vessel "lapses" when an officer dies aboard, and for the season's
"delist, buy the wreck". **From general knowledge: verify the wording before lock.** 1857 is
called "the rising" by Nemo and "a mutiny" only when he quotes the English edition; it remains
contested history and is not argued here.

## What is ours

The Directorate, Form Nine ("a Courageous"), the lapse rule, the drum and the air glass, the
Auditor and her ledger, the tin on the ice ceiling, the testimony montage, Mira's method, the
lamps in the ice, everything said by anybody, the crew and their secrets, and the solution.

## Considered and not used

- The Speckled Band's method as the true solution (it is the false one).
- Verne's mechanism of Nemo feigning not to understand his guests' languages, as a way for him to
  have overheard the handover: it would have told him who the spy is.
- Moriarty and "the spider at the centre of the web" (card 13): held for the showrunner; Pell is
  written as an office, not a mastermind.
- Any living thing in the ocean.
