label-one prop fixed by ruling, the parcel, Ilse's launch, goggles outdoors, bigger lantern-globes,

photoreal, bright, rich colour, film-star faces dressed for their job, never sexualised, no logos,

below. State ages firmly (the model ages women 5–10 years). Status of everything here: PROPOSED.

## The regulars at the gala (costume lines to add to each master's prompt)
- **Jax:** black evening suit, the jacket lined in his burnt-orange, a white rose, a brass
  pocket-watch on a chain across the waistcoat (PIP's travelling lens is in the watch face); gold
  bauta mask, often pushed up. Same face, curls, stubble.
- **Mira:** fitted indigo silk evening dress with long sleeves, her dark braid pinned up, her brass
  loupe worn as a pendant on its chain; lapis bauta mask. Elegant, not revealing.
- **Lucía:** a dark red silk dress she plainly hates, worn with her work boots underneath, the red
  ribbon on her wrist; from scene 9 cook's whites tied over it; by 27 the hem torn to the knee.
  Ivory bauta mask.
- **Idris:** his own threadbare embroidered coat, cleaned and pressed, sapphire with gold thread,
  over a white shirt; trimmed beard; gold mask.
- **Seo:** a plain grey silk suit cut like a flight jacket over a grey shirt, cropped hair, no
  jewellery; she carries her mask like a helmet. **v3:** in 14 the grey silk jacket is off and laid
  over PIP's console lens; in 15 it is over her arm and there is one smear of black grease on her
  shirt cuff; in 26–29 she is back in her pilot's jacket and the silk one lies folded on the
  navigator's seat.
- **Oyuna:** a formal Mongolian deel of deep green silk with a gold-thread sash and long cuffs, hair
  in a low grey-streaked knot; an embroidered bag instead of her satchel. Accurate, dignified, not
  costume.
- **Outside (all):** light hooded wraps in pale gold acid-proof cloth, **close-fitting goggles (v3:
  acid haze on bare eyes)** and a slim breathing mask over nose and mouth; no pressure suits. The
  wraps hang almost still: the city rides the wind.
- **Indoors:** a gala mask pushed up shows the mouth, so masks go up only for silent beats (the
  four seconds at the end of 17: two faces, no words).

## Props
- **The Reliquary:** a brass-and-enamel cage-sphere about three metres across, on one cable,
  heat-shimmer pouring off it and acid mist fuming where the gallery's air touches the hot metal
  (**v3:** hot metal does not steam); a lock that opens like a mouth; a drawer.
- **Item seven, label one (v3, fixed by showrunner ruling; write nothing else on it, ever):** a torn
  strip of old brown parcel paper, soft at the edges. The surviving half shows exactly three
  things: (1) **PIP's small square audit stamp with one chipped corner**; (2) **half a round date
  mark** carrying an office number and a date twenty years ago, **no letters**; (3) at the torn
  edge, **the last letter of the address line only, a slanted "…A" in blue ink**. No other letter
  of any name or place is legible, and the missing half is never shown. The stamp, date mark and
  "A" are in-house INSERT art, not generated text. ("…ERES" from draft 2 is withdrawn: PIP reads
  Ceres from the office number.) Two crisp identical **copies** in two glass cases show the same
  three things. From the end of 29 the real one sits on the bridge console under PIP's stamp arm.
- **The GATE DRIVE panel (v3, new; needed again in Episode 6):** a brass panel low on the bridge's
  centre console, between the two front seats, under a hinged brass cover. On the cover an
  engraved plate reading **GATE DRIVE** (in-house text, legible in every shot) and a small window
  that shows the setting (VENUS GATE — SET: THE BELT in 29). Under the cover: a dial, a needle
  gauge (sinking out of the red on arrival) and a bundle of fibre lines, one of which takes **one
  clean square cut** in 14, matching the fuel-line cut in Episode 2. States to make: closed with
  the dog against it (1, 26, end of 29); open with the cutter and the cut (14); Idris's hand
  turning the dial (29).
- **The parcel (v3):** seen once, in the hold (cold open): the existing parcel prop with
  **thirty-two labels** layered like scales, the newest in a big knightly hand (Episode 3), and
  low among them one pale, clean rectangle where label one used to be. Oyuna's Phobos delivery
  slip (Episode 4) is still on it. Check the count against the season continuity pass before
  the image is made.
- **Three radar charts (v3, REAL FOOTAGE, composited, never generated):** PIP's console chart, the
  Magellan global view PIA00478 (NASA/JPL), full frame; the chart on Scheherazade's desk, the
  southern-hemisphere view PIA00008 (NASA/JPL/USGS), with her inky hand at its edge; the chart on
  the watch face in 25, the Alpha Regio radar image PIA00147 (NASA/JPL). No logos on or near them.
- **Ilse's launch (v3):** a small grey Voss boat at the station dock, grey guards climbing in (24),
  idling and never sent (30). A shape behind her, never a hero prop.
