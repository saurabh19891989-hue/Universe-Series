## E. What Episode 6 must assume

**The gate.** The Venus Gate is a ring of brass and white light in space above the cloud. At the
last frame of 29 the ship is climbing toward it and has **not** gone through. The gate drive is
**set: THE BELT** (Ceres). Under the closed brass cover marked GATE DRIVE, low on the centre
console between the two front seats, **one fibre line has a clean square cut through it**, the
same cut as the fuel line in Episode 2. The dog's place is against that panel.

**Who did what to the panel.** Seo cut it at about 12:34 am by the Hesperus clock, alone, with the
ship docked under the Night House, and was back in the salon at 12:45. Idris shut the cover on
arrival ("Nobody touch it till we leave") and, at 1:40 am, lifted it, turned the dial and shut it
again in front of the whole crew. **He is the last and only person the crew has seen touch it.** Seo
did not touch it and did not speak; her "Checked" is cut.

**PIP's eleven dark minutes.** Seo laid her grey silk jacket over PIP's bridge lens before she
cut. His bridge record has nothing from about 12:34 to 12:45: he will know **when**, not who. He
has not looked at that stretch yet; nothing was wrong until the gate. His watch lens and Lucía's
wrist comm were both at Hesperus the whole time and saw nothing of the ship.

**Who knows what.**
- *Nobody in the crew* knows about the cut, Pell's order ("See to the gate tonight. No courage
  this time. — P.") or Pell's "Done? — P.", which Seo has **not answered**.
- *Idris* knows Seo's papers are false (Hong Gildong) and has promised not to report it; he knows
  she left the salon for eleven minutes and lied about why. *Scheherazade* also saw the chair
  empty. *Oyuna* was in the room and did not remark on it. *The dog* watched the cut and growls
  at Seo's hands.
- *Seo* has reported to Pell that Idris owes the storyteller, amount unknown.
- *The whole crew* knows: label one is recovered; its date mark is the Ceres office, twenty years
  ago; one letter of the address survives, the last, "A"; the audit stamp on it is PIP's own; the

  to the day. Jax has said: "He wants me to follow it. Then he can try to keep up."
- *Mira alone* (and Lupin) knows there was an envelope marked VALE, J. — BEFORE and that she let it

## F. Checked on the web during this pass

- **The white rose is not in "The Arrest of Arsène Lupin".** It is in story IX, "Sherlock Holmes
  Arrives Too Late": Lupin tells Nelly she carried a white rose on the steamer, that he found it
  and kept it, and at the end he finds another on the box beside the bamboo. Checked in the Morehead
  translation at Standard Ebooks (the same text as Gutenberg #6133; gutenberg.org itself answered
  503 today): https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/maurice-leblanc/the-extraordinary-adventures-of-arsene-lupin-gentleman-burglar/george-morehead/text/chapter-9
  and, for the first story (no rose; "blonde hair, wound right forearm"; the camera let fall
  "between the vessel and the pier"), .../text/chapter-1. `sources.md` is corrected. The library
  card (`research/library/sources/07-arsene-lupin.md`) lists the rose under images without saying
  which story; I have not edited it.
- **"In under a week."** The Vega balloons floated at about 54 km and went about a third of the way
  round the planet in 46 hours at roughly 70 m/s, which is about six days for a full circuit.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vega_program ; https://ipnpr.jpl.nasa.gov/progress_report/42-85/85Q.html
- **1.3 light-seconds** is 389,700 km (my arithmetic); the Moon's mean distance is 384,400 km. "Four
  hundred thousand kilometres" and "the distance from Earth to the Moon" are both fair.
- Everything else in the science table rests on the critic's own checks and URLs (review, section
  6), which I did not repeat.
