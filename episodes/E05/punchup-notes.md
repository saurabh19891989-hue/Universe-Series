## What the story still gets wrong (for the critic and the showrunner)
- **READ timing is inconsistent across the season.** E01–E02 use 1.3 s at the Moon, which is the
  one-way Earth–Moon light time; E03–E04 treat READ as a round trip (22:04 at Mars). This episode
  uses the round-trip reading: 1.3 s at Venus puts the reader within about 195,000 km. That
  contradicts an Earth reader at the Moon (it should have been about 2.6 s) and suggests the reader
  was on Earth during E03–E04 but is near Venus now. Either the reader travels with them, or E01's
  number needs a note. Showrunner ruling needed before ep 10.
- **Voss's "once a year, and on Venus that is more often than once a day"** is true for the sidereal
  day (243 Earth days) against the Venus year (225), not for sunrise to sunrise (about 117). Kept
  because "What was real" states it correctly; science critic to confirm.
- **Why the brake has a lock, and why Mattias helps,** is unexplained (fine as an ep 7 plant, but
  the critic may call it convenient).
- **Seo's eleven minutes** assumes the ship is docked at the Night House belly hatch, a short walk
  from the salon. Not stated.
- **The gondola geography**: the car was about thirty metres from the Night House station when the
  far clamp opened (23), and hangs on thirty metres of cable (25). Consistent, but nobody has drawn it.
- **Who raised the alarm at 1:04** is shown only as ALARM — RELIQUARY on Ilse's wrist (21). Assumed
  automatic when the box arrives and the drawer is found opened.
- The 26-day gap between E04 and E05 is not mentioned on screen.
- "What was real" now runs 0:49 (rulebook guide 0:30; E01 used 0:40).
- I ran one read-only `git status` on the E05 folder by mistake while checking that `script.md` was
  untouched. Nothing was staged or committed.
