# SECRET UNIVERSE
## Episode 5 — "A Gentleman on Venus"
### Shooting script, draft 2 (comedy and dialogue punch-up of the writer's draft 1, with the showrunner's rulings; for the independent critic)

Running time: about 35:44 honest (scene targets add up to 35:34 including "What was real" at 0:49, plus the 0:10 title; model: dialogue at 155 wpm, 0.4 s per speaker change, plus each scene's stated **Holds** (silences, held wides and insert-only action, 4:45 in all), counted by script). Cold open 1:35 · Act 1 7:42 · Act 2 9:22 · Act 3 7:46 · Act 4 8:00 · tag 0:20. 4,286 spoken words (dialogue lines only, including "What was real"; cues, parentheticals and action excluded); 477 speaker changes; narrator V.O. 243 words (5.7%). 31 units (cold open, 29 scenes, tag), plus "What was real". Longest scene 2:21 (8, tea). Time inside *The Unscheduled*: about 4:10 (12%). Places: 8 (7 off the ship). Unit numbers are kept from draft 1 so that the outline's plant references still work.
Visual style: photoreal characters and sets composited with real NASA footage where it exists,
staged mostly as cinematic stills with camera moves and limited motion. Lip movement is scarce or
absent. Venus is THIN on real footage, so the city, the cloud sea and every interior are
generated; real Magellan radar and Parker views appear on screens and in "What was real".

**How to read the headers.** Each unit lists its number, setting, thread (A = the heist and the
legends · B = the crew changing toward each other: Jax and Mira, Idris and Seo, Lucía and PIP ·
C = the spy, Voss, Jax's past, PIP's reader), honest target seconds, the stated silences and holds
inside that target (**Holds**), visual class (REAL FOOTAGE WIDE / GENERATED WIDE / PHOTOREAL SET
STILL / PHOTOREAL CLOSE-UP / INSERT / MONTAGE / DATA VISUAL), and a STAGING note saying how it
plays without lip-sync.

**Standing staging rules (they apply everywhere unless a scene says otherwise)**
- **This episode is narrated by its guest.** NARRATOR (V.O.) is Arsène Lupin's own voice, light,
  amused, French. He lies; **the pictures contradict him** (5, 14 four times, 28). On screen he
  spends Acts 1–2 disguised as INSPECTOR GANIMARD, the detective Voss has hired: the same voice,
  aged and slowed. An attentive viewer can catch it. The crew cannot. Jax has no V.O. this episode.
- **Ganimard's heavy grey moustache covers his mouth completely.** Play him on eyes and eyebrows,
  like the Baron. When the moustache comes off (13) it is one silhouette INSERT.
- **The masked gala is the lip-sync plan.** Indoors at Hesperus every guest wears a house mask: a
  full-face lacquered mask after the Venetian *bauta* (gold, ivory or lapis), whose jutting jaw lets
  the wearer talk, eat and drink unseen. The crew push their masks up only for silent beats.
- **Outside, everyone wears a breathing mask.** The air is carbon dioxide with acid haze, but the
  pressure and warmth near fifty kilometres are close to Earth's, so there are no pressure suits:
  light hooded wraps and a mask over nose and mouth. All outdoor dialogue is behind masks.
- **The light.** Outside, the sky is a ceiling of even gold-white light, edge to edge, with no
  visible Sun and soft shadows; below, a sea of cloud to every horizon. Bright by default. Each
  interior has its own colour world (see casting-notes.md).
- **Jax never looks down.** Near any edge he is framed facing away from the drop, or looking up.
  No one explains it.
- **Scheherazade's lines play on her hands** (writing, turning pages), on the rings of listeners,
  on the dome and the lamp; her face is held in silence. Dignity always: a library and an
  observatory, never a harem, never dancers, never lounging. The threat she lives under is never
  played for a laugh.
- **Ilse Voss never speaks.** Her silver knife lies on the arm of her chair while she listens.
- **Chichikov is never in sharp focus.**
- **PIP travels as a lens in a brass pocket-watch case** on Jax's waistcoat chain, and through
  Lucía's wrist comm; aboard ship he is his own console. The lens brightens when he talks. **Every
  report gets an INSERT of its number** (#1,240 in 4, #1,241 in 29; E04 ends at #1,231).
- **Label one** (the fragment) is always the same prop: a torn strip of brown paper; an address
  half in blue ink, in a slanted hand, torn away before the name; half a postmark, ...ERES, and a
  date twenty years ago; a small square audit stamp with **one chipped corner**. Never show the
  missing half.
- **Lucía** wears the red dress she hates, her boots under it, the ribbon on her wrist; from 9,
  kitchen whites over it. Anything she keeps goes in her boot.
- **Mira wears her brass loupe as a pendant.** Reflections are for silent beats only.
- **When someone important speaks, hold on the listener.**
- **Music:** a swing band with an oud and a qanun in the front row, all original. Do not quote
  Rimsky-Korsakov.

**Visible-mouth speaking shots: 5 (maximum 6).** Marked **[MOUTH 1]** to **[MOUTH 5]** in the text:
1. Voss, 8, "Honey, not sugar."
2. Lupin, 23, "A double-cross is only a favour done rudely."
3. Lucía, 23, "My mother's is water."
4. Scheherazade, 28, "One of them came back."
5. Mira, 29, "I write things down."

**Voice and TTS notes**
- Lupin: one voice for NARRATOR and LUPIN. GANIMARD is that voice pitched down and slowed about
  10%, with a cough of age. In 13 the line "And your plan is very bad" starts as Ganimard and
  finishes as Lupin.
- Scheherazade: low, unhurried, warm and exact. Never a comic accent. Voss: soft, Scandinavian
  vowels, never louder than tea. PIP: as E01; in 19 slower and quieter than we have ever heard
  him, with no processing change. Seo: flat and quick. Idris: rich, enjoying himself.
- Radio colours: breathing masks are close and slightly muffled; Lucía's wrist comm is narrow.
- Say it: Arsène Lupin "ar-SEN loo-PAN" · Ganimard "gah-nee-MAR" · Scheherazade
  "sheh-HEH-reh-ZAH-deh" · Hesperus "HESS-per-us" · Dunyazad "doon-yah-ZAHD" · qanun "ka-NOON" ·
  Hong Gildong "hong GIL-dong" · "A.L." and "H.G." as letters.
