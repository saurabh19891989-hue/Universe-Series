## 1. Logline and why you keep watching

**Logline:** To win back the oldest label off their mystery parcel, a broke courier crew crash
the richest party in the sky, a masked gala on a balloon city fifty kilometres above Venus, and
find that the best thief alive has announced he is stealing the same thing, the host already
knows how their captain takes his tea, and the hostess of the salon next door is telling a story
to keep a woman with a knife from cutting off her air.

**Why you keep watching:** Three hundred men at the party are dressed as the thief. The narrator
is a guest who won't give his name, and the detective has his voice. The romance is hidden as
cover, and the cover keeps failing because they keep forgetting to turn at the pillar. Every
plan is out-planned by someone cleverer, until the cleverest person in the room turns out to be
the villain, who let it all happen. And under the city there is nothing to stand on for fifty
kilometres.

## 5. Act breaks (each makes the next act a different story)
- **Act 1 out (7):** "Honey, not sugar." The crew thought they'd slipped in; the host has been
  expecting them. Act 2 is a game played inside the villain's house with his permission.
- **Act 2 out (13):** the detective is the thief, and the rival is now the partner. Act 3 is a
  robbery run to someone else's plan.
- **Act 3 out (21):** robbed by their partner while being searched, the story over, Ilse loose with
  the knife. Act 4 is an escape across a cable.
- **Act 4 (27–29):** the double revealed, the cable cut, the climb, the cost counted.
- **Tag (30):** the villain let everything happen, and still holds the original.

## 6. What it costs
- **Mira** gives up the one piece of evidence of Jax's past, unread, and then lies to him for the
  first time ("He took a lot of things"). The scientist chooses not to know. The audience alone sees it.
- **Lucía** gives up the reward (her mother's water, again) and keeps Voss's card in her boot.
- **Jax** wins the label the only way Voss wanted him to, and knows it ("He wanted you to have it").
  Voss is now steering.
- **Scheherazade** wins her air, but her sister stays under Heritage contract.
- **Idris** pays one night and is reminded that he owes more.
- The gondola, which was not theirs.

## 8. Running gags, escalated
1. **PIP's reports and who reads them.** E01: READ in 1.3 s (audience only). E02: PIP sees the
   READs. E04: he writes *to* the reader. **E05:** at Venus, 1.3 s is impossible for anyone on
   Earth, so the reader is not on Earth (4); and a thief files the only correct form PIP has ever
   received (6). The tag of the gag (29): READ, 1.3 s, again. PIP also refuses, for once, to file
   ("I am not filing a report on this").
2. **Nobody can remember Chichikov.** E01 "man, average"; E02 silent on Arakawa's bridge. **E05:** the
   world's greatest observer, the narrator, can't describe him (5); Voss himself blanks on his name
   (8). He carries the original envelope in the tag and is still out of focus.
3. **A legend correcting Earth's version.** Scheherazade: "I told them so the next girl wouldn't
   have to" (2). Lupin: "My first story ends with my arrest" (13), and the whole ballroom dressed in
   Earth's version of him (5). Escalation: Scheherazade corrects *Lupin's* version of their past
   ("half a story and stole my bookmark", 28).
4. **Lucía's wage count.** Voss knows it to the day and beats PIP ("I was rounding down. He was
   not.", 9); a job card in her boot; "eleven months, one fortune, and now a flower" (20); a second
   fortune given up (23); twenty days by the end (29).

## 9. Real-place properties used (rulebook: the place fights back)
- **Nowhere to stand for fifty kilometres:** the docking line (0), the plank (18), the climb (27).
- **Breathing air is a lifting gas in CO₂:** the city, the Night House sinking and rising (2, 25,
  27), Lupin's exit (28).
- **Heat increases with depth; the surface melts lead:** the Reliquary lives in the heat (0, 12);
  the sinking house (25).
- **CO₂ and acid outside, near-Earth pressure:** breathing masks without suits (13, 21, 27).
- **Super-rotation, about four days:** the city moves with the wind (1).
- **Retrograde, a day longer than a year:** Voss's toast (5); "What was real".
