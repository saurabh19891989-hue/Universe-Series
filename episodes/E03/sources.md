# Episode 3 — Sources

Episode 3 mixes **six sources from five cultures**: Spanish (Cervantes), Arabic (the *Thousand and
One Nights*; al-Mutanabbi), Greek (Homer), English-German (the Munchausen *Sequel*) and Russian
(Gogol, as Chichikov's method). From each we take a mechanism, an image or a trait. **The plot is
ours, and nothing is retold scene by scene.** No film, stage musical (*Man of La Mancha*, 1965),
opera, ballet or game version was used, and no modern translation was copied. Picasso's 1955
Quixote sketch is in copyright and is not a reference.

"Opened" means I read or searched the text in this session. "Card" means it is recorded as read on
the library card in `research/library/sources/`. "Known" means I worked from knowledge of the
book, and the passage should be checked against the public-domain edition named before lock.
