# Episode 3 — Casting notes and art list

For the art department. Format follows `canon/CHARACTER-LOCKS.md`: one locked description per new
character or place, to be used verbatim in prompts with the style line from
`production/IMAGE-PIPELINE.md` §2 (bright, rich vivid colour, photoreal, no logos, no readable text).
Every new face is **PROPOSED** until the owner approves a master. Once approved, every later image
passes the master with `-i`. Sets get pure-black windows and skies where real footage goes in; we
composite NASA plates ourselves (§3a). Say ages firmly: the model ages women 5–10 years.

**Never reference:** any film, TV, stage or musical design of Quixote or Sancho (Pabst 1933, Kozintsev
1957, Welles, *Man of La Mancha*, Gilliam 2018, any animation), Picasso's 1955 sketch (in copyright),
or any screen Sindbad. Gustave Doré's 1863 Quixote engravings are public domain and may be consulted
**only** for period armour and Rocinante's leanness; the faces are ours. Never name a real actor in a
prompt.
