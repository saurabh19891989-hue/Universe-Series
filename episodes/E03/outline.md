first legend un-thinned, and the proof is a sworn statement, which ep 10 can abuse or honour.

## 1. Logline and why you keep watching

**Logline:** The parcel's next address is locked in the head of Don Quixote, who has gone sane,
files tax returns, and is fading to the colour of the dust. With a Voss sale due at dawn on top of
Olympus Mons, a courier, a squire and a cheating poet drive him four thousand kilometres across Mars
to make an old man mad again, while the one honest woman aboard keeps telling him exactly what
they are up to.

**Why you keep watching:** The cure is a lie and the scientist won't tell it, so the plan has to
find a truth big enough to work. The squire has been offered the island he was promised for thirty
years, and we don't know until the dust devil whether he will take it. The episode's spectacle (an
old man on a skeleton rover charging a giant of dust that is also the only thing that can save
their power) is both madness and engineering. And the one person the Atlas cannot find at the end is
the captain.

## 5. Running gags, escalated

1. **PIP's reports and who reads them.** E01: filed, and READ in 1.3 s, seen only by the audience. E02:
   UNSENT behind the Moon, then "Someone reads them." **E03:** on Mars a READ takes at least twenty-two
   minutes ("I have never waited twenty-two minutes to be ignored", 4). Then, for the first time,
   someone reads them *aloud*, with relish: the knight calls PIP his chronicler, has #1,204 read to
   him, and asks for the dog in every chapter (24). A report becomes the legal record that relists a
   legend (26). And the READ on Mira's oath comes back at 22:04: "The four seconds were theirs" (29).
   The reader's identity is untouched; ep 4 has PIP start writing to them.
2. **Nobody can remember Chichikov.** E01 "man, average", "Chichester". E02 silent on Arakawa's bridge.
   **E03:** Idris worked for him three weeks and "couldn't pick him out of a crowd of one"; Sancho
   calls him Chicharrón (pork crackling); PIP: "He has not said a word since the Moon." "Hasn't he?"
   "No one has noticed. I am filing a report on no one noticing." He signs everything "P. I. Ch.";
   he never speaks; he is handed back an unread letter.
3. **A legend correcting Earth's version.** E01: the Baron corrects Earth. E02: Kaguya corrects the
   Baron. **E03, three ways:** Sancho corrects Earth ("Earth says fat and stupid." … "I'm fat."); the
   sane Quixano *agrees* with Earth, which is how Sancho knows he's ill (10); restored, Quixote corrects
   Cervantes ("I mistook nothing. They were both."), and **Sancho corrects Quixote's correction** ("He
   mistook them. I was there. I picked him up.") (23). Idris corrects the Nights on Sindbad (13).
4. **Lucía's wage count.** E01 nine; E02 ten. **E03:** "On Mars I'm switching to sols. It'll sound
   worse." PIP: 296, "fewer, longer days." "Switching back." (6). In 29: "ten months and one week …
   In sols." "Fewer." "In days, then." Idris: "Then I'll be owed eleven. I like to be at the top of a
   list."

## 6. Mira's argument, both sides (the episode's spine)

- **Mira:** he is the sanest man on Mars; he asked for the truth and thanked her for it; consent
  matters; "Voss wants a tidy version of him. You want an untidy one. No one has asked which one he
  is." She tells him about the con, against the crew.
- **Jax, Sancho, Idris:** he is literally going transparent; sanity is the illness, here as in
  Cervantes; and he didn't choose it, Voss beat him into it with a white moon and a clipboard; "A man
  is the story he tells about himself."
- **How it resolves:** neither wins. Trickery fails because Mira was honest (17). The cure comes from
  something real: a real dust devil, a real need (power), Sancho's inverted lie *plus* Mira's truth
  that it is only wind. He chooses to charge, to prove them wrong, and sees both. Then the law
  demands a witness, and the only listed person present is the woman who never lies. What she swears
  is her own reading of the truth, and it costs her: it is on the Directorate's file by the tag.

## 7. What it costs
Mira's oath on the permanent record, now in Pell's hands; Sancho's island (his life's wish), returned
unread; the free address Jax turned down, and with it three sols, a full house and a fall; Seo is
ordered to keep them on Mars and is on the record helping them anyway; Ilse Voss now wants to know
*why* Jax does what he does; and the Atlas has, in front of two crew members, failed to find the
captain at all.
