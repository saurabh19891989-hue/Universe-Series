## What the story still gets wrong, or needs a ruling on
- **READ arithmetic.** E02's 1.3 s is the one-way Moon–Earth time, so I made the stamp count
  SEND to read: 00:11:00 for ordinary reports on Mars, 00:11:04 for Mira's oath (draft 1 said
  22:04, which counts the trip back). PIP still learns it twenty-two minutes after sending.
  Confirm this is the convention for E04+.
- **The 22-minute wait on the summit.** "LATER" in 28 covers it; nobody says why the ship sits
  for twenty minutes after the goodbye. Fine for most viewers; the critic may want one line.
- **Pell now has a stake in Jax not knowing** ("He is not to know"), and Seo hid from Pell that
  she cleared the screen. The E04 writer needs both. Voss does not learn NO SUCH PERSON in E03.
- **Science check, unchanged lines:** Lucía's "Up here it [the Sun] rises before it does
  anywhere else" is too strong as written (the summit sees sunrise earlier than the plains round
  it, not earlier than all of Mars); suggest "before it does down there" if the science critic
  agrees. Spirit's 2005 power recoveries were cleaning events credited to wind and dust devils;
  "a dust devil went over a rover called Spirit" should be checked for wording. Al-Mutanabbi
  rendering still unverified (sources.md).
- **Mira's cost is visible but still private.** Only the audience sees the shaking hand and
  Pell's CREDIBILITY folder. If the showrunner wants a crew member to see it, Lucía is the
  natural witness (E06 is her and Mira's episode).

## Lines I am not sure are in good taste
- **"Nobody's."** (1, Sancho about the dog). Deliberate: the dog belongs to Nobody. It is never
  said about Jax and the audience cannot decode it before ep 10, but it is close to the secret.
  Cut to "No one's." if the showrunner wants zero risk.
- **"Then pirates caught him and sold him." / "That's couriers."** Sindbad's enslavement is in
  the source; the joke is on the courier trade, not the slavery. Keep an ear on the read.
- **"He hadn't got a Bible. He used what he had."** A sacred text named in a light line about
  a ledger used for a knighting (in Cervantes too). Mild; owner may prefer "a book of rites".
- **"Chickpea."** Idris mangling Chichikov's name. The joke is that no one can hold his name,
  not that it is Russian; if it reads as mocking a language, cut Idris's word and keep the rest.
- **"Earth says fat and stupid." / "I'm fat."** Kept from draft 1: Sancho owns it and corrects
  Earth. Flagging only because it is body humour.
