# UNIVERSE UNDELIVERED
## Episode 8 — "Havoc Under the Mountain"
### Shooting script, draft 1 (writer's draft, for punch-up and the independent critic)

Running time: about __RUNTIME__ honest (scene targets add up to __SUM__ including "What was real" at 0:40, plus the 0:10 title; model: dialogue at 155 wpm, 0.4 s per speaker change, plus each scene's stated silences, holds and insert-only action). Cold open __CO__ · Act 1 __A1__ · Act 2 __A2__ · Act 3 __A3__ · Act 4 __A4__ · tag __TAG__. __WORDS__ spoken words (counted by script: dialogue lines only, including "What was real"; cues, parentheticals and action excluded); Jax V.O. __VO__ words. 28 units (cold open, 26 scenes, tag), plus "What was real". Time inside *The Unscheduled*: about __SHIP__.
Visual style: photoreal characters and sets composited with real NASA and ESA footage, staged
mostly as cinematic stills with camera moves and limited motion. Lip movement is scarce or absent.
Jupiter is ADEQUATE on real footage (Hubble and Webb rotations and aurora in 4K, Juno stills), so
Jupiter is always the real planet: in windows, in the sky of Io, and under the chase. Io is THIN
(three verified stills, no video), so Io from orbit is a real still and everything on and under its
surface is a generated set.

**How to read the headers.** Each unit lists its number, setting, thread (A = the job and the
legend: getting Sun Wukong out · B = the crew changing toward each other: Jax and the Monkey,
Lucía and the Monkey, Seo and Idris, Seo's decision · C = the spy, Pell and Arakawa, Voss, Jax's
past), honest target seconds, visual class (REAL FOOTAGE WIDE / GENERATED WIDE / PHOTOREAL SET
STILL / PHOTOREAL CLOSE-UP / INSERT / MONTAGE / DATA VISUAL), and a STAGING note saying how it
plays without lip-sync.

**Standing staging rules (they apply everywhere unless a scene says otherwise)**
- **Sun Wukong is the hero of this episode** (showrunner ruling). He is formidable, proud, quick
  and funny on his own terms. He is never the butt of a joke, never does "monkey business", never
  screeches, scratches or capers. His power reads as **stillness**: he sits, he looks, and the
  mountain moves. The comedy is aimed at the contract and at the officials. No Buddha, bodhisattva
  or deity appears, is named or is joked about; the court that once made him keeper of its horses
  is "a great court" and nothing more, and the first mountain is "not your business".
- **Wukong wears a half-mask of carved grey stone** over nose and mouth, cut by himself from the
  mountain (he was born from a stone). He can talk all day on a still face. Play him on **his
  eyes** (gold, lit from inside, steady as pilot lights), **his hands** (long, furred russet-gold)
  and **the needle**. Outdoors and on the barge he wears the Protector's gilded ceremonial helmet,
  which he despises; through its gold visor his eyes are two steady points of fire. Design from the
  novel's text only (see casting-notes.md): no circlet, no tiger skin, no opera costume, nothing
  from any film, series, cartoon or game.
- **Under the mountain everyone wears a sulphur filter mask.** The Residence's air stinks of
  sulphur dioxide, so staff and guests wear a silk half-mask with a small brass filter (peacock
  blue for guests, plain for ushers, gold filigree for the Chamberlain). That is the lip-sync plan
  for every interior on Io.
- **Exteriors are in helmets**, with dialogue on suit radio. On Io there is no air and no sound.
  **In Jupiter's cloud tops there is wind**: sound comes back, and the barge's banners, stiff in
  vacuum, finally fly.
- **PIP is in the box.** Aboard ship he is an amber glow through the slot of a titanium strongbox,
  slightly muffled. On Jax's wrist he is a lens that **dies in the radiation belts and wakes under
  rock or under the belts** (INSERT: lens dark / lens amber). Every report gets an INSERT of its
  number: #1,262 in the cold open, #1,263 in 21, #1,264 in 26 (renumber to follow E07).
- **Dose badges.** Every suit carries a strip that reads green, amber, red, black. They are always
  INSERTS. Lucía's is turned face-in from 15 and not seen again until 23.
- **The board and the clock talk on screen.** The gate's flip-letter board (2) and the Year Clock
  (4, 21, tag) are INSERTS; neither has a voice.
- **Arakawa is a profile** against real Jupiter, lit by her console, as in E02. Her face is seen
  speaking once, in 24, and that is the point of it.
- **Chichikov is soft, grey and silent** at the back of the *Steadfast*'s bridge. He has not
  spoken since the Moon.
- **Seo is a flight helmet and a pair of gloved hands.** Her hands are the hero insert of the
  episode: we read both passes on them.
- **Lucía**: the ribbon on her wrist outside her suit glove; from 15 she is a figure at two brass
  wheels in the open stern, seen from behind.
- **Mira**: the loupe, the back of her head. Reflections are for silent beats only.
- **Action is vehicles, wide real footage and held poses.** No fluid hand-to-hand fighting
  anywhere. Wukong never strikes anyone.
- **Default cut: hold on the listener.**
- **Places and light (rulebook, Places).** Eight bright places, each with its own colour world:
  **the sulphur plains** (yellow, white frost, black sky, Jupiter striped across half of it), **the
  Chancery of Dignities** (peacock blue and gold, a jewel box), **the Hall of the Seat** (lava
  orange on black rock, one figure in patched silk), **the Hall of Deference** (emerald and gilt,
  wax directors under glass), **the plume** (blue-white sulphur snow falling upward into sunlight),
  **the State Barge** (gilt and peacock, absurd and magnificent), **Jupiter's cloud tops and the
  eye of the Great Red Spot** (cream, salmon, brick red, real), and **the *Steadfast*** (steel
  blue). Darkness is not used at all: even under the mountain the light is lava and lamps.
- **Music:** Peking-opera percussion (clapper, small gong, large gong, cymbals) for the officials
  and the houses, an erhu for Wukong, big drums for the Year Clock and the chase. All original; no
  opera aria and no screen theme is quoted.

**Visible-mouth speaking shots: 4 (maximum 5).** Marked **[MOUTH 1]** to **[MOUTH 4]** in the text:
1. Seo, 16, "I know where Lucía is."
2. Mira, 22, "It isn't crossed out. It's gone."
3. Arakawa, 24, "Stand my grapples down."
4. Lucía, 26, "Don't you dare pay me now."

**Voice and TTS notes**
- WUKONG: a quick, light baritone, bright and amused, with sudden complete stillness. Precise
  English with a light Mandarin colour. **No comic accent, no animal noises, ever.** He is the
  fastest talker in the episode until 22, where he is the slowest.
- CHAMBERLAIN LEHNER: warm, rounded, Viennese courtesy; a man who is genuinely pleased to see you.
  No comic accent. He never shouts until 12.
- ARAKAWA: as E02: formal, exact, a little slower than anyone else.
- PIP in the box: the same voice with the top rolled off, as if through a letterbox.
- Radio colours: suit radio narrow; the barge in the belt has heavy static that clears in 19;
  in the cloud tops add wind under everything.
- Say it: Sun Wukong "swun woo-KONG" (PIP says "Mr Sun") · Io "EYE-oh" · Loki Patera "LOH-kee
  PAT-er-ah" · Lehner "LAY-ner" · Juno "JOO-noh" · Arakawa "ah-RAH-kah-wah" · #1,262 read
  "twelve-sixty-two" · "P." as a letter.
