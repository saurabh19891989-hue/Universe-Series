# Episode 8 — casting notes, sets and props

For the art department. Style as `canon/CHARACTER-LOCKS.md` and `production/IMAGE-PIPELINE.md`:
photoreal, bright, rich colour, film-star faces dressed for their job, never sexualised, no logos,
no readable text in generated frames (all text is a DATA VISUAL or INSERT made in-house). New
characters get one master image each for the owner's casting approval; every later shot passes
that master with `-i`. Real Jupiter and the real Io stills are **composited by us** into pure-black
skies and windows, never drawn by the model. State ages firmly. Status of everything here:
PROPOSED.

## The guest: read this first

**Sun Wukong is honoured in some temples. He is the hero of this episode** (showrunner ruling,
`canon/season-01.md`). Every picture of him must read as formidable, self-possessed and
dignified. The lock below is built from the novel's text (a stone-born monkey, king of a mountain
of fruit, eyes that see through disguise, an iron staff the size of a needle) and from one real
animal, and from nothing else.

**Never, in any image:** crouching, squatting, scratching, capering, hanging by a hand or tail,
going on all fours, baring teeth, grinning, eating fruit, a comic pose of any kind. No gold
circlet or headband. No tiger-skin kilt. No red or yellow neck scarf. No golden chain-mail,
phoenix-feather cap or cloud boots. No Peking-opera costume or painted opera face. No pink
heart-shaped face. No resemblance to the 1986 television series, the 1978 Japanese series, any
animated film, any comic or cartoon hero derived from him, *Black Myth: Wukong* or any other
game, or any film ape. No halo, lotus, temple or religious setting. He is never shown fighting.
