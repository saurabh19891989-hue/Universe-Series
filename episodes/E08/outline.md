# UNIVERSE UNDELIVERED — Episode 8: "Havoc Under the Mountain"

**Outline v1, writer.** Written with the full script in one pass (owner's instruction: no stop for
a ruling); punch-up writer and independent critic follow. Genre: prison break and action, the
season's biggest spectacle. Music world: Peking-opera percussion (clapper, small gong, large gong,
cymbals), an erhu for the guest, big drums for the Year Clock and the chase. All original.
Running time: about 36:00 honest (cold open 1:30 · title 0:10 · Act 1 7:55 · Act 2 9:15 · Act 3
7:05 · Act 4 8:05 · tag 1:15 · "What was real" 0:45). 4,018 spoken words. 28 units.

**Showrunner ruling honoured.** Sun Wukong is the hero of the episode: formidable, proud, quick,
funny on his own terms, never the butt of a joke. The comedy is aimed at the contract and at the
officials who caged him with a title. No Buddha, bodhisattva or deity appears, is named or is
joked about. The court that once made him keeper of its horses is "a great court" and nothing
more; the first mountain is "not your business". Design from the novel's text, never a screen,
stage or game look.

**Picks up from E07 (on disk).** The crew's unnamed friend inside the Voss family (Mattias; only
Lucía knows the name) paid his first secret: "Io. Ask what he keeps under the mountain." Pell has
told Seo to find out who the friend is. Lucía was owed twelve months and ten days leaving Ceres;
PIP's last report was #1,251 and his reader is "never further away than 1.3 seconds". The crew
believe one of them sabotaged the Venus gate (E06; its stop-note was checked) and suspect Idris; it was Seo.
**Hands on to E09 (draft seen in progress):** Lucía cannot walk; the count is thirteen months and
four days; reports end at #1,265; the ship runs for a harbour under Europa's ice; Wukong's line.

**The rule in one breath, for viewers with no interest in contracts:** *He signed for one year
as Grand Protector of Jupiter. It was one year of Jupiter, which is twelve of ours. The title has
no rank, so he cannot resign; its "dignity" sits on him with the weight of the mountain; it renews
tonight if he is in his seat when the clock strikes. For twelve years they have laid a pen in
front of him: he may strike his own name out whenever he likes, and be nobody.*

## 1. Logline and why you keep watching

**Logline:** To open Voss's vault under a volcano on Io, a courier crew flies into Jupiter's
radiation belts with a whole fleet closing the door behind them, and finds not a thing but a
person: Sun Wukong, twelve years in a stone chair with a grand title and no rank. They cannot
break him out, so they deliver him, chair and all, in a gilded parade barge; and when the
contract follows them down into the eye of the Great Red Spot, the only way out is the one thing
he has refused for twelve years, and the one thing their own captain once did and cannot
remember.

**Why you keep watching:** A clock with one hand that ticks once in twelve years is about to
tick. The strongest being in the system cannot stand up, and says so with perfect manners. Every
door in the prison asks your rank, and the crew have none, which turns out to be a rank. The
audience knows the pilot has been ordered to fly "adequately" while the engineer is running an
engine by hand in the open; we watch her hands. And a commander who has never once been late
finally catches them, with the file she was told not to read open on her knee.

## 3. Scene by scene

Key: **A** = the job and the legend · **B** = crew changing toward each other · **C** = the spy,
Pell and Arakawa, Voss, Jax's past. Seconds are honest targets.

### Cold open

**0. Bridge — into the belt** · A/C · 90 s
PIP has to be put in a titanium box: Jupiter's belts cook computers. The box holds everything
aboard worth money: the lamp, the parcel, the dog, the coffee machine. Behind them Arakawa's
fleet closes the way in. The friend's line: the most dangerous thing Voss owns, under a mountain
on Io; out tonight or not for twelve years.
**Turns:** the way back is shut; the vault has a clock. **Line:** PIP: "I notice the crew is
outside it." / JAX: "We're not worth money. We've checked." **Place:** the radiation belts fry
electronics; Juno's titanium vault.

### Act One — "The Position Before Any Rank"

**1. Io — the sulphur plains** · A/B · 80 s
Jax leaves Idris with the ship and Seo to watch him (the wrong way round). Dose badges: green,
amber, red, black. PIP's wrist lens dies in four minutes. Frost that is sulphur dioxide; a plume
on the horizon ("a small one"); Jupiter fixed in the sky. The vault has a front door and a bell.
**Turns:** it is not a vault, it is an address. **Line:** MIRA: "From here it never rises and
never sets." / OYUNA: "Like a debt." **Place:** Io keeps one face to Jupiter; volcanic frost;
plumes hundreds of kilometres high; lethal radiation at the surface.

**2. The Gate of Rank** · A · 55 s
First house. A flip-board asks their rank; they have none; "the unknown are not admitted". PIP
finds the lowest grade on its table, "the position before any rank at all", holders: one, and
gets four more admitted to it.
**Turns:** they are in; and the prisoner is a person with a grade. **Line:** JAX: "Did you just
get us promoted?" / PIP: "To nothing. It was the only vacancy." **Place:** rock shields: PIP
wakes under the mountain.

**3. Cockpit** · B/C · 55 s
Idris, a suspect since Venus, plays the part badly; Seo, who did it, tells him so. Pell's order
arrives forty-one minutes old: bring them out to the Commander; do not be brilliant; fly
adequately. "Do you think I cut that gate?" "No." "That was very fast."
**Turns:** Seo has her order. **Line:** IDRIS: "I've never been as rich as when I didn't know."
**Place:** light delay to Earth, over half an hour.

**4. The Chancery of Dignities** · A/B · 115 s
Chamberlain Lehner is delighted: visitors, at last. Four hundred dignities, each held by somebody
who used to be difficult. The Bow of Office nobody can string. The Year Clock with one hand,
which strikes tonight. Oyuna reads the first line of the Register: the Grand Protector of
Jupiter is Sun Wukong.
**Turns:** the "acquisition" is the Monkey King. **Line:** CHAMBERLAIN: "A title is the only
gift that costs nothing and that people will guard with their lives." / LUCÍA: "What's it pay?"
… "I've got that job." **Place:** sulphur in the air (filter masks).

**5. The *Steadfast*** · C · 30 s
Arakawa holds the belt. She has asked the Auditor what is under that mountain; the answer will
take over an hour and will say "nothing", beautifully. She breaks the seal on Annex C. Page one
is PIP's report #1,204. She reads "Take it" five times, level. Chichikov's teacup stops.
**Turns:** she is reading the file. **Line:** "The unit counted." **Place:** light delay.

**6. The Hall of the Seat** · A/B/C · 140 s
A cavern over the lava; a small figure sewing sashes into a jacket with a needle. He looks at
Jax: "These eyes see through any disguise. Yours is very good. Even you believe it." Jax looks
back: he sells for a living and knows a man who has bought. Neither enjoys it. The contract: one
year, *local*; no rank, so no resignation; "the dignity shall rest upon the holder". He pushes
on the arms of the Seat and the mountain moves a finger's width. The clock strikes in two hours
and nine minutes.
**Turns (act break):** he cannot be lifted out, cannot quit, and renews tonight: "Twelve more."
**Line:** "I asked how long. I did not ask whose." **Place:** Jupiter's year is nearly twelve of
ours (the Year Star); Loki Patera's lava.

### Act Two — "A Chair Is Freight"

**7. The plan** · A/B/C · 130 s
The witness's signature slides off the eye; even Wukong cannot recall the grey man's face.
Oyuna: an entry can be struck by its owner. He refuses: he has struck a name before, his and
every monkey's, from a book that only counted his days; this book tells strangers who he is. "I
have never been no one. Ask your captain how it suits him." Then Jax: he can't leave the seat;
does it say the seat can't leave? A chair is freight. Wukong accepts carriage: front door, facing
forward. Lucía lifts king and chair. Under the Seat, a round black hole nobody looks at.
**Turns:** the rescue becomes a delivery. **Line:** "Voss owns the paper. A name belongs to
whoever turns round when it is called." **Place:** Io's gravity, about the Moon's.

**8. Cockpit** · C/B · 35 s
Arakawa on the open channel: she can wait; the belts won't let them. "Pilot. I have been reading
about you." … "Yours is the best written."
**Turns:** Arakawa knows what Seo is. **Line:** IDRIS: "Should I be jealous?" "Of a file?" "Of
anyone who has read you." **Place:** nobody can loiter in the belts.

**9. The Hall of Deference** · A/B · 55 s
Second house. ALL RANKS WILL BOW TO THE BOARD; the floor opens when you do. Jax, a salesman, bows
by reflex and freezes bent double. Grade Zero is not a rank; the instruction does not apply. The
Board is wax. Wukong asks his porter what she is paid.
**Turns:** Lucía and the Monkey recognise each other: both unpaid. **Line:** "Then you are paid
in the wrong coin, not unpaid. I was paid in titles." / "Doesn't buy water." / "No. Neither did
mine."

**10. The Chancery — the Register attends** · A/C · 80 s
On Renewal night the Chamberlain carries the Register down himself and lays it open at the
Protector's line with the pen beside it. Twelve times. He has never looked at the pen. "Any
owner can. It is the oldest rule in the book, and the only one we don't print." Oyuna finds line
17: clean paper. "We do not discuss seventeen." And he is always the senior officer present.
**Turns:** the cage door has been open for twelve years; the price is himself. **Line:** MIRA:
"That's a cage." / "It is a page, Doctor. You would be amazed what people will sit still for, to
stay on one."

**11. The causeway over the lake** · A/B · 90 s
Third house. Wukong knows the lake's four moods and has named them after directors. The Seat
meets the Renewal procession in the middle. "For twelve years I have asked you my rank. Tonight
I am asking about the precedence." The Protector takes precedence in any procession; when he
proceeds, the Chamberlain precedes, carrying the Register, facing the way he is going. "I am
going out." The whole procession turns round and leads the escape.
**Turns:** the officials escort the breakout. **Line:** "This is highly irregular." / "It is the
most regular thing that has ever happened to me." **Place:** a lava lake whose crust founders
and re-forms.

**12. The Residence doors — havoc** · A · 55 s
Outside, the ground shudders. The Chamberlain: it was never a chair, it was a cork; the weight
has been holding the mountain shut. The title had one duty; they left it out because he would
have asked to be paid. The mountain opens; the plume goes up. The Chamberlain thrusts the
Register at Oyuna and runs back for his clock.
**Turns:** the book is in the crew's hands; Io is erupting behind them. **Line:** "I would. I'll
invoice." / LUCÍA: "They don't pay." / "I have not finished asking." **Place:** Io's volcanism.

**13. Montage — under the plume** · A · 35 s
Big drums. Sulphur snow in sunlight; Lucía bounding with a king on her shoulders; lava
fountains; the ship lifting with a yellow scar burned down its port side. Jax (V.O.): "We left
quietly." Seo cannot land near that. Round the mountain's shoulder: a boat.
**Turns:** they cannot reach the ship. **Place:** plume fallout; low gravity.

**14. The Protector's pier — the State Barge** · A/C · 75 s
A gilded parade barge with a horse for a figurehead and a throne mount, registered as a lifeboat
and a float. It lifts off Io. "We're out." Arakawa: "All ships. Close the belt." Then the
Register's small print: "The Residence: Jupiter, and all that Jupiter holds", everything inside
its magnetic field. Days to cross; the clock strikes in fifty minutes; the dignity will come
back at full weight, on a boat.
**Turns (act break):** out of the mountain is not out of the contract. **Line:** "The mountain
that followed, I earned, and whose it was is not your business. This one I was sold." **Place:**
Jupiter's magnetic field, the biggest thing any planet owns.

### Act Three — "All That Jupiter Holds"

**15. The barge — the back step** · A/B · 65 s
The helm cooks; the engine has to be run by hand in the open stern. Lucía goes. The others
shelter in the lee of the stone Seat. Her badge darkens; she turns it face-in. Jax, on a thing
with a figurehead, looks sick.
**Turns:** Lucía is in the open, and the dose clock is hers. **Line:** "I am the strongest thing
this side of the Sun, and I am furniture." / "Then be good furniture." **Place:** radiation fries
electronics; mass shields.

**16. Cockpit — the first pass** · C/B · 65 s
One pass and the barge drops into the hold, and Lucía is off the back step. Seo's hands move a
hair late. The barge falls past the open hold and is gone. PIP: "That is the first approach I
would describe as adequate." Lucía, cheerful on the radio: "Go round! I'll hold her!"
**Turns:** Seo obeys Pell; the barge will fall the whole belt with Lucía in the open. **Line:**
IDRIS: "I know that hand. I've played it. The one where you're better than the cards you show."
/ SEO: "I know where Lucía is."

**17. The *Steadfast*** · C · 30 s
"The first approach was the Auditor's." She reads "one count of enjoying it". The Auditor is
over half an hour away; by the time he forbids it, it will have been done. She takes the
flagship down herself.
**Turns:** Arakawa stops waiting. **Line:** "That is the one mercy of this planet." **Place:**
light delay; Juno's road, fast and under.

**18. The barge — the fall** · B/A · 70 s
Juno's trick: the quietest place for a very long way is right on top of the planet. The contract
is airtight. Lucía's badge is "a warm colour". Jax gets up to go to her; a gold hand on his
chest. "Sit, and hate it. That is what the chair is for."
**Turns:** Jax learns what twelve years in the Seat was. **Line:** MIRA: "We're in an open boat
in space, Jax. I'd like some things to be airtight." **Place:** the gap between the belts and the
cloud tops.

**19. Jupiter — the cloud tops** · A/B · 60 s
Under the belts the badges stop. There is wind, so there is sound, and the barge's banners fly
for the first time. Everything weighs more than double. The *Steadfast* is behind. Ahead: the
Great Red Spot, whose outer lane is the fast part; the middle is slow.
**Turns:** they choose the eye. **Line:** WUKONG: "At last. Somewhere with clouds." **Place:**
no surface; a storm wider than Earth; winds fastest at its edge.

**20. The eye — the bow** · A/C · 65 s
The ship cannot come lower; the barge cannot climb; the line gun is cooked. The Bow of Office,
which three wardens could not string. "I don't carry bows." "Nobody asked you to carry it." He
strings it like a man doing up a shoe, plucks it by his ear, and it sings one note like a bird.
Sixty metres straight up, in a wind, at double weight: the arrow goes through the eye of the
winch hook.
**Turns:** the catch is on; and the Monkey has seen what Jax's hands know. **Line:** "Your hands
are better liars than you are. They don't bother." **Place:** Jupiter's gravity.

**21. The eye — the stroke** · A/B/C · 70 s
On Io the clock strikes, with the Chamberlain witnessing. On the barge the Seat takes the weight
of a mountain. Boat and ship sink together. Idris's hand is near the line release; Seo: "Move
your hand." Everything forward: the flying she was told not to do. The *Steadfast* slides over
the eye. Oyuna turns the Register toward the Seat.
**Turns (act break):** cornered above, sinking below; he looks at the pen. **Line:** PIP: "The
barge has declared a change of mass. My display has run out of zeroes." **Place:** no ground to
sink to: hotter and tighter until the hull stops arguing.

### Act Four — "What a Blank Looks Like"

**22. The barge — the Register** · A/B/C · 110 s
"Don't sell to me, Captain. I was bought by better." So Jax stops selling: he does not know what
it costs; his record starts seven years ago; the warrant has him as "nobody", small n; he is
still here. It is the worst pitch Wukong has ever heard. He looks aft at a porter burning on the
back step of his boat while he guards a line of ink, and asks for the pen. Struck by another
hand, a name keeps a line through it and can still be read. Struck by its owner: the stroke
fades, the name fades, clean paper. "There is an end of the registration. I am no longer under
your control." He stands up.
**Turns:** the crew learn that a listing can be removed by its own owner, and what that looks
like; Mira looks at Jax; Jax does not see it. **Line:** "You are a bad liar when you tell the
truth. It suits you." / MIRA: "It isn't crossed out. It's gone."

**23. The eye — nine ships** · A/B/C · 80 s
The *Steadfast*'s grapples close on the barge. Wukong drops the gilt helmet over the side, takes
the needle from behind his ear, and it is an iron staff. He lifts Lucía off the wheels and takes
the engine in one hand. Her badge is black. A pinch of gold hairs into the wind: eight more
*Unscheduleds*, each with the same sulphur scar. Jax gives his first order: cut the line and go.
**Turns:** the ship is free; the boat is not; we see what it cost. **Line:** SEO: "Eight copies,
and every one with the scar. I know this story." / IDRIS: "I thought you might."

**24. The *Steadfast* — the lifeboat** · C/A/B · 125 s
Nine contacts, nine scars. Pell, forty-four minutes ago: recover the asset; to let anything go
would be courageous. The asset's entry is blank: "Your warrant is for a title. There is nobody
in it." A medical beacon. Jax has no pitch left: "Commander. Please." She has read sixty pages
tonight; most were written to persuade her; PIP's were not. "Are you the one point three
seconds?" "No. I read slowly." The pilot flew two approaches: she knows which she would want on
her record. Full face, for the first time: she does not seize lifeboats and does not follow
them home.
**Turns:** Arakawa lets the lifeboat go, and enters it under the word "courageous". **Line:** "I
do not arrest people on a book I am halfway through. When I have read the end, I will find you.
By then I should like to know which of us I have come for."

**25. Cargo hold** · B/C · 45 s
The dog has left its place by the drain and lies with its chin on Lucía's boot. PIP reads the
badges and, at hers, does not say the number. Jax, kindly, to Seo: "Nobody makes that catch
first time." "I do." "Not today." Idris, passing: the second pass was the best flying he has
ever seen. "It was late." "Yes."
**Turns:** PIP chooses ambiguity; Seo is forgiven for the wrong thing. **Line:** "Miss Ortega's
badge reads more than I should like." / "That's not a number." / "No. I am aware."

**26. The strongbox, then the bridge** · B/A/C · 125 s
The coffee machine is out on the deck; Lucía is in the box. "It's where we put what we can't
replace." Jax orders her paid in full. "Don't you dare pay me now. If you pay me now, I'll know
how bad it is." … "Then keep owing me. People don't die owed. It's rude." On the bridge: she
needs rock over her, or ice. Oyuna: a harbour under Europa's ice came off the Atlas a hundred
years ago at the request of its owner; the entry says No One. PIP files a report on his reader;
it comes back read in 1.3 seconds: "So it was not her."
**Turns:** the cost is counted; course for Europa. **Line:** "And four days. Don't make it
fourteen." / "I'm going to make it fourteen." / "Good." **Place:** ice and rock as shielding.

### Tag

**27. The Chancery / the *Steadfast* / Seo's bunk / the open ramp** · C/A · 75 s
The Year Clock is one tick past the top, and the Chamberlain, the senior officer present, cannot
get out of his chair to look at it. Arakawa turns to page 61: VALE, J. — BEFORE: SEE VOSS
HOLDING; behind her a grey glove writes ARAKAWA, T. Seo types "ENGINEER HURT. MY FIRST PASS—",
and the cursor eats it: UNSENT. On the open ramp over a planet of cloud, Wukong: he is going
where there are no strangers; he owes Lucía and will come when she calls, complaining. "Earth
says I ride a cloud. One. Nobody has ever offered me this many." Then:
**Line:** "You've forgotten more than a name, Captain. Ask the archivist what a blank looks
like." He steps off. Jax puts his hands in his pockets.

## 6. Running gags, escalated

1. **PIP's reports and who reads them.** He goes into a titanium box and his first report comes
   back UNSENT ("Titanium. I did not think this through"). His reports turn out to be Annex C of
   the sealed file: a Directorate commander reads #1,204 aloud, level, and then all of them. For
   the first time PIP meets a reader, asks her straight out whether she is the 1.3 seconds, and
   is told "No. I read slowly." Then the old receipt comes back anyway: "So it was not her."
2. **Nobody can remember Chichikov.** The being whose eyes see through any disguise cannot draw
   his face: "He was not wearing one. There was nothing to see through." He witnessed the
   contract as "P. I. Ch—". He is still silent, still soft, and writes one more name.
3. **A legend correcting Earth's version.** "Earth says I made havoc because I was wild. I made
   havoc over a job description." Earth leaves out the horses. Earth says he struck out only his
   own name: "Earth thinks I am vainer than I am, which takes effort." Earth says he rides a
   cloud: one.
4. **Lucía's wage count.** Thirteen months, rounded up as ever. The guest asks what she is paid
   and tells her she is paid in the wrong coin. The escalation is the reversal: Jax finally
   orders her paid in full, and she forbids it, because being paid would mean she is dying.
5. **"Courageous."** Pell sends it across forty-four minutes to stop Arakawa. She has it entered
   in the log under that word, as a description, and asks for it to be spelled correctly.
6. **New this episode: every door asks your rank,** and the crew's is nothing, which is a grade.

## 7. Science used (for the critic; items marked * are not in the location files and need the
science check)

- Io is the most volcanically active world known, heated by Jupiter's tides; lava lakes (Loki
  Patera); sulphur plains; plumes hundreds of kilometres high (location file 11; brief).
- * Volcanic sulphur dioxide falls back as frost. * Io is tidally locked, so Jupiter hangs fixed
  in its sky. Io orbits in under two days (brief). * Io's surface gravity is close to the Moon's.
  * Loki Patera's lava-lake crust founders and re-forms (used only as "moods").
- Jupiter's radiation belts are lethal and damage electronics; Juno keeps its electronics in a
  titanium vault and dives in on long orbits (brief). * Juno's close passes go under the main
  belts, between them and the cloud tops: used as "the quietest place is right on top of the
  planet". * Mass shields: rock, a stone seat, a hull, ice.
- The Great Red Spot is a storm larger than Earth; winds in its outermost lane are the fastest
  and are speeding up (location file 10). * Its centre is comparatively slow.
- Jupiter has no surface (brief): pressure and heat rise without a floor.
- * Gravity at Jupiter's cloud tops is about two and a half times Earth's ("more than double").
- * Jupiter's year is about 11.9 Earth years; * Chinese astronomy calls Jupiter the Year Star
  (Suixing), whose roughly twelve-year circuit lies behind the twelve-year cycle.
- * Jupiter's magnetosphere is the largest structure belonging to any planet.
- Light time to Earth is over half an hour (brief): Pell's messages are 41–44 minutes old on
  arrival; Arakawa's question takes over an hour to be answered.
- No numbers are given for radiation dose, plume height beyond "hundreds of kilometres", storm
  size beyond "wider than Earth", or distances. Dose badges and their colours are fiction.
- Fiction, plainly: the mountain, the Residence, the contract, the eruption caused by lifting the
  Seat, a barge crossing from Io to Jupiter in fifty minutes, hovering in the eye.
