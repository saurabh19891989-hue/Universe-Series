# UNIVERSE UNDELIVERED
## Episode 6 — "Too Close to the Sun"
### Shooting script, draft 1 (writer's draft, for punch-up and the independent critic)

Running time: about 36:00 honest (scene targets add up to 35:50 including "What was real" at 0:40, plus the 0:10 title; model: dialogue at 155 wpm, 0.4 s per speaker change, plus each scene's stated silences, holds, slow sung lines and insert-only action, about 6½ minutes in all, because this episode's music world includes silence; the model's own total is 35:55). Cold open 1:20 · Act 1 7:10 · Act 2 9:00 · Act 3 7:50 · Act 4 9:10 · tag 0:40. 4,082 spoken words (counted by script: dialogue lines only, including the sung lines and "What was real"; cues, parentheticals, action, screens and lamp text excluded); 447 speaker changes; Jax V.O. 96 words (2%). 28 units (cold open, 26 scenes, tag), plus "What was real". Longest scene 2:00 (7 and 18). Time inside *The Unscheduled*: about 6:15 (17%).
Visual style: photoreal characters and sets composited with real footage, staged mostly as
cinematic stills with camera moves and limited motion. Lip movement is scarce or absent. The Sun
is RICH in real footage and carries the episode; Mercury is THIN (MESSENGER stills only), so the
crater is a generated set and the planet itself appears small, on screens and in one slow push.

**How to read the headers.** Each unit lists its number, setting, thread (A = the fall, the kettle
and the two old flyers · B = Lucía and Mira, the wage count, Jax and Mira · C = the saboteur: Seo,
Idris under suspicion, Jax's past), honest target seconds, visual class (REAL FOOTAGE WIDE / REAL
STILL / GENERATED WIDE / PHOTOREAL SET STILL / PHOTOREAL CLOSE-UP / INSERT / MONTAGE / DATA
VISUAL), the real property of the place that the scene uses, and a STAGING note saying how it
plays without lip-sync.

**Standing staging rules (they apply everywhere unless a scene says otherwise)**
- **The Sun is always real, and we composite it ourselves.** Every view of the Sun (the bridge
  window, the sun-deck dome, the wides) is real footage: SDO flares and loops, the ESO 4K surface,
  and the 2019 La Silla eclipse for the corona seen round the edge of the shield. Never generate
  the Sun. Sets are generated with pure-black windows and domes. The dome's colours (gold, deep
  red, teal) are the real colours of SDO's filters; Sampati changes them with a brass wheel.
- **The shadow rule.** Behind the shield the Sun's disc is hidden, the corona streams round the
  rim, and it is cool, blue-dark and starry. One step outside the shadow is white. Faces in the
  shadow are still clearly lit (suit lamps, bounce).
- **Outside, everyone is in a helmet with a gold sun visor down**, and all dialogue is suit radio.
- **Sampati is played from behind**: a very tall old man between two long brass levers, arms laid
  out along them like open wings, against the Sun. His grey mantle covers his shoulders until 25.
  When he turns, we see his eyes. He has one visible-mouth line. He is never comic: nobody jokes
  about his burning or his hunger, and his one dry line (25) is his own.
- **Icarus works in a tile-setter's dust mask and smoked goggles**, so he can talk all day. His
  signature INSERT is his thumb softening a bead of yellow wax. In 25 one hand is bandaged.
- **Three clocks, all pictures.** The row of wax beads on Icarus's bench (firm in 3, slumping
  from 13, pooled flat by 21). The ice in the cold hold (none in 7, stacked to the roof in 20,
  running by 22). The DATA VISUAL of the loop with its dot.
- **From 17 on, everyone indoors wears a wet cloth tied over nose and mouth** against the heat,
  until 26, when the cloths hang round their necks.
- **Seo is played on her hands.** The dog watches her hands. The crew read that as affection.
- **PIP** is the lens on Jax's wrist and a brass relay lamp carried aboard the tender. **Every
  report gets an INSERT of its number** (#1,242 in the cold open, #1,243 in 6, #1,244 in 20). From
  22 to 26 his lens is out.
- **In Act 4 the two ships talk through brass speaking tubes** (no electrics). Tube voices are
  hollow and close. Icarus, outside, speaks down a taut wire: thinner still.
- **Jax keeps away from the meltwater.** No one explains it.
- **Lucía's ribbon** is tied over her suit cuff outside. **Mira's loupe** is on its chain;
  reflections are for silent beats only. **Idris** is played on his hands: a small brass
  astrolabe, a pencil.
- **When someone important speaks, hold on the listener.**
- **Music:** a bansuri (Sampati), a reed pan-pipe (Icarus), low strings, Oyuna's two-stringed
  lute and voice, and real silence. The two flutes never play together until 23. All original.

**Visible-mouth speaking shots: 5 (the maximum).** Marked **[MOUTH 1]** to **[MOUTH 5]**:
1. Idris, 7, "No."
2. Sampati, 12, "I spread them."
3. Oyuna, 21, singing.
4. Idris, 24, "Now."
5. Seo, 27, "I know."

**Voice and TTS notes**
- Sampati: deep, slow, exact, slightly old-fashioned English; no comic accent; he never raises
  his voice. Icarus: light, quick, a boy's delight in an old throat. PIP as E01. Seo flat and
  quick. Idris rich; in 7 and 24 his one-word lines are dry, with no music in them at all.
- Radio colours: suit radio is narrow; the speaking tubes are hollow, with breath in them; the
  wire to Icarus is thin and far. Lucía on the comm in the cold open is dry.
- Oyuna's song (21, 23) is sung, not spoken: a low epic voice over a two-stringed lute
  (topshuur). Have it composed and, if possible, sung or checked by a performer from the
  Mongolian epic (tuuli) tradition. No traditional text is quoted. Allow about 55 s for its sung
  lines.
- Say it: Sampati "sum-PAA-tee" · Jatayu "juh-TAA-yoo" · Vindhya "VIN-dhyuh" · Icarus
  "IK-uh-rus" · topshuur "TOP-shoor" · al-Rahhal "al-ra-HAAL" · "S.V." and "H.G." as letters ·
  #1,242 read "twelve forty-two" only if spoken (it is not).
