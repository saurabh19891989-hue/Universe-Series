# Episode 6 — casting notes, sets and props

For the art department. Style as `canon/CHARACTER-LOCKS.md` and `production/IMAGE-PIPELINE.md`:
photoreal, bright, rich colour, film-star faces dressed for their job, never sexualised, no
logos, no readable text in generated frames (all text is a DATA VISUAL or INSERT made in-house).
New characters get one master image each for the owner's casting approval; every later shot
passes that master with `-i`. Regulars use their existing masters plus the costume lines below.
State ages firmly. Status of everything here: PROPOSED.

**The one rule that matters most this episode: never generate the Sun.** Every dome, window and
wide is generated with a pure-black opening and the real plate is composited by us (SDO, ESO).
The same goes for Mercury seen from space (MESSENGER stills, small).

## Props
- **Feather tiles:** white fired-ceramic plates shaped like long flight feathers, a hand to an arm long.
- **Wax:** beeswax-yellow beads the size of a walnut; one always in Icarus's fingers.
- **Reed pan-pipes** (seven reeds bound with thread and wax), worn at the belt; played only in the score.
- **Seo's brass hand key:** a small old telegraph-style key on a wooden base no longer than a finger, no markings (0, 18, 27).
- **The neat cutter** (from E02 and E05) on a hook by the pilot's seat (6).
- **The cut:** one copper line inside the gate-plot panel, cut square and bright (6); Mira's brass loupe.
- **Sampati's cup:** a small plain brass cup (20).
- **The feather** (25, 27): as above; and the lining of Seo's scuffed flight helmet with the creased photograph of an older woman in a rescue pilot's helmet (E02 prop).
- **Idris's astrolabe:** small, brass, plain, with a sighting arm; no readable engraving.
- **Oyuna's topshuur:** two strings, small wooden body, carved head (E04 prop).
- **The kettle:** a coil of copper pipe round the engine bell, a feed hopper for ice, an iron valve wheel; white steam jet (24).
- **Speaking tubes:** brass pipes with flared mouths and hinged caps, in every set of both ships from 18.
- **Ice blocks, a sled, a pry bar, a snapped blade, a sheet of gold foil, a bucket with one loaf-sized lump.**
- **Wet cloths and a bowl; a measured tin cup of water** (17, 26).
- **Voss's job card** (E05 prop), seen at the top of a boot.
- **The parcel** (existing lock), unchanged; the dog's chin on it.
- **PIP's brass relay lamp:** a small copy of his lens on a hand-lamp base, carried onto the tender.
