**Outline v1, writer.** Written in one go with the script (owner's instruction: no stop for a

Genre: survival drama; the season's most emotional episode, with humour as courage. Music:

**Picks up from E05 as written on disk** (`episodes/E05/script.md`, scenes 26 and 29). Pell's

Belt; Seo put her hand on the same panel after him and said "Checked." Her small neat cutter

PIP, Lucía, Seo, Idris, Oyuna, the dog, the parcel. Next address: Ceres.

**The rules of this episode in one breath, for a viewer who hates physics:** *We are falling
round the Sun in a long loop. The bottom of the loop is fire. An old man's shield can cover us
for one more pass, so we must never come round again. Our only engine is a kettle that boils
Mercury ice in sunlight, and it only works close to the Sun. Too high and it won't boil; too low
and the shield dies. There is a line between, as wide as the ship.*

## 1. Logline and why you keep watching

**Logline:** A sabotaged gate drops a courier crew sunward of Mercury with dead engines, into the
orbit of two old flyers who once went too high: one who did it for joy and wants to do it again,
and one who burned his wings covering his brother and has waited a century to be useful. To get
out they must boil Mercury's ice in the Sun's own light and fly a line as wide as the ship round
the bottom of the fire, by hand, with one of them known to be a traitor.

**Why you keep watching:** The audience knows from minute one who cut the wire, and has to watch
the best liar aboard tell the plain truth and not be believed, while the guilty pilot flies
honestly and the dog stares at her hands. A man who can see the rings of Saturn saw her do it,
and tells only her. An epic singer starts a song that, by the rules of her art, cannot be left
unfinished, and admits she doesn't know the ending. A countdown of unpaid wages turns into the
count that fires the engine. And the help that arrives was called by the person who broke the
ship, four minutes after she broke it.

## 3. Places (9; each with its own colour)

1. **The wreck field**: a dead gate ring with bites cut from it, white modules, turning gold foil, ink-black shadows, the real Sun behind (1, 2).
2. **The eclipse**: the tender's fan-shaped shield sliding across the Sun; real corona round its rim; white light to blue shadow in one cut (2).
3. **The *Vindhya*'s long gallery**: honey lamplight, racks of white ceramic "feathers" like pan-pipes, copper, wax (3, 5, 8, 13, 14, 15, 24).
4. **The sun-deck**: a dark-glass dome full of the real Sun, in gold, red and teal; two brass levers; a great mirror (4, 12, 18, 19, 20, 23, 25).
5. **The cold hold**: bare white and frost (7); then stacked to the roof with blue ice (20–22).
6. **Mercury, a polar crater**: a rim of white fire, black sky, the Sun three times its Earth width, blue ice under dark crust (10, 11). Mercury itself from MESSENGER stills (5, 9).
7. **The shield's shadow**: black ribs like a cathedral, the corona round the rim, stars (16).
8. **The rim of the shield at the bottom of the pass**: a tiny figure at the edge of the black fan, the real corona beyond (23).
9. **The Unscheduled**: bridge (0, 6, 26, 27), hold (17), engine room (24).
Every act goes somewhere new: Act 1 the wreck field and the tender; Act 2 Mercury; Act 3 the shadow; Act 4 the bottom of the Sun.

## 5. Act breaks (each makes the next act a different story)
- **Cold open:** delivered to the Sun, everything dead.
- **Act 1 out (6):** the wire was cut. A survival story becomes a whodunit with nowhere to go.
- **Act 2 out (13):** the orbit is being lowered on purpose by one of the rescuers; this pass is too deep for the shield. The helpers are the danger.
- **Act 3 out (19):** the storm will arrive at the bottom. No electronics, no PIP: the plan must be flown by eye, hand and voice.
- **Act 4 (24–26):** the slingshot; saved by a call nobody admits to.
- **Tag (27):** Seo erases her own rescue.

## 6. What it costs (changes that stay changed)
- **The crew know one of them did it**, and that the same person called for help. Nobody is
  cleared. Idris has been told to his face that his captain could not answer quickly enough.
- **Sampati** loses his shield (two thirds of its feathers), gives up the mirror he has watched
  through for a century, and gives away the first feather he has grown since he fell.
- **Icarus** has had his last low pass, and has seen what he was costing.
- **Seo** knows she is seen, suspects she was lied to, and deletes the evidence that she tried to
  save them. She owes an old man the truth.
- **Mira** has said aloud that she chose not to know who Jax was. **Lucía** has said aloud that
  she keeps Voss's offer. Each now holds the other's secret; Idris holds Lucía's.
- **PIP** has two days and one line missing, and a reader who needs no radio.
- The crew owe the old flyers "a pair of wings", and owe the Guild for a tow.

## 9. Real-place properties used (rulebook: the place fights back)
- **Sunlight several times Earth's** near Mercury's orbit: the hull cannot shed it (0, 1, 25, 26).
- **A heat shield's shadow** (Parker Solar Probe's method): cool behind, glowing in front; step
  out and you are gone (2, 16, 23).
- **Mercury:** 430 °C day side and −180 °C cold [file]; ice in permanently shadowed polar
  craters; a third of a g; a day two years long; the Sun that stops and goes back; ice that
  sublimes rather than melts (5, 9, 10, 11).
- **Flybys change speed for nothing**: in front of a planet you lose speed, behind it you gain
  (Parker at Venus, seven times); all bodies fall alike (13).
- **Where a push counts:** at the bottom of the loop to raise the top, at the top to raise the
  bottom; fastest at the bottom (14, 24).
- **Flares and ejected clouds:** light first (eight minutes to Earth), the cloud hours later;
  radio and electronics fail (19, 22). Brass tubes, a taut wire and a signal lamp do not.
- **The corona:** millions of degrees and almost nothing in it; a spacecraft has flown through
  it [file] (16, 23).

## 10. Science notes for the critic (what is real, what is licence)
**From the location file:** 430 °C and −180 °C; Sun about 1.4 million km across, core about 15
million °C; Parker Solar Probe has flown through the corona.
**General knowledge, please confirm (NASA Mercury and Parker pages):** sunlight at Mercury up to
about seven times Earth's and the Sun more than three times as wide; 3:2 spin-orbit resonance;
one solar day of 176 Earth days, two Mercury years; the double sunrise at some longitudes; water
ice in permanently shadowed polar craters (radar and MESSENGER); surface gravity 0.38 g; lead
melts at 327 °C; Parker's shield (about 11 cm of carbon composite, roughly 1,400 °C in front and
about 30 °C behind: on screen only "warm as a kitchen" and "glowing"); seven Venus flybys to
lower its orbit; the corona's high temperature and very low density; flare light reaching Earth
in about 8.3 minutes and coronal mass ejections taking hours to days; ice subliming in vacuum
sunlight; a taut wire carrying sound without air.
**Licence, flagged:** a small boat landing on Mercury and catching its mother ship again during
a flyby needs a very large change of speed (treated as ordinary ship capability, as elsewhere in
the series); a block of ice becoming a plume in seconds (true in kind, fast for the camera); how
quickly Venus haze dulls copper (invented); a storm stopping *every* circuit (exaggerated); the
size of the push from forty-one seconds of steam (in reality modest; the rule that it must be
made at the bottom, and that the bottom of the loop is then lifted from the top, is correct);
the terminator on Mercury is not shown moving (a day lasts months; the shadow edge in 11 is
fixed). The fictional orbit: about eight days from Mercury's distance to the bottom, a loop of
about seven weeks, the bottom near where Parker flies.
