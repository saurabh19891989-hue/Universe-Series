## Props
- **Catchers**: grey Company issue; painted town ones; gold (Anansi); Guild yellow; Lucía's.
- **Salt tallies**: white pressed-salt tiles the size of a playing card, each with a stamped
  number, slightly translucent at the edge; in nets of rope. One on its own thread: 4471-C.
- **Anansi's knotted counting cord**, about a metre, dark, with knots of several sizes.
- **Brass goldweights** (Akan figurative weights): birds, fish, a ladder, a drum, a stool; one new
  one: a small figure with both arms up, throwing.
- **Paper**: turnstile slips; the account screen; the Guild's box of counterfoils and the one
  slip (the clerk's slanting hand); the torn label with its salt tide mark (from E05); Carmen's
  vault receipt (line: GUARANTOR); Mattias's answer cards; yellow Guild dispatch slips; Voss's
  notice; Voss's cream card (matching the E05 job card); Mattias's recall slip; Oyuna's three
  slates. All made in-house.
- **Carmen's drawer**: shaved brass meter wheels, tiles with the numbers filed off, a notebook.
- **The tap**: brass padlock (on, then lying on the table), meter, one glass.
- **The oware board**: carved wood, two rows of six cups, seeds, a game in progress.
- **Mattias's brass key** on its watch chain (E05); **the paper bag and pastry**.
- **Lucía's steel key** on the ribbon; **Seo's neat steel cutter** (E02); **Wen's stopwatch**.
- **The strongbox** with a bar handle wide enough for two boots; **Vesta bonds**.
