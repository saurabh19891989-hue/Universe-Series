# UNIVERSE UNDELIVERED — Episode 7: "The Spider's Share"

**Outline v1, writer.** Written in one go with the script (owner's instruction: no stop for a
ruling between outline and script). Punch-up and the independent critic follow.
Genre: con / sting, the season's crime-film episode: fast narration, day cards, small favours
becoming big debts, one montage of the money rolling in, a fast rise and a faster fall. Music:
cumbia for Lucía's home; highlife guitars and an Akan talking drum for Anansi; the two lock
together while the con runs. Narrator: Jax (226 words of V.O., 5%).
Running time: about 35:55 honest (cold open 1:25 · title 0:10 · Act 1 8:30 · Act 2 7:55 · Act 3
7:10 · Act 4 9:30 · tag 0:35 · "What was real" 0:40). 4,137 spoken words. 29 units. Speaking
roles: 10. Places: 9 (8 off the ship). Inside the ship: about 5:45 (16%).

**Works from the season plan as contract.** Episode 6 was being written in parallel and is not on
disk. State assumed at the start: the sabotaged gate dropped the ship sunward (it was Seo; the
crew does not know); the crew knows there is a saboteur and suspicion sits on Idris, who set the
gate first (E05, 29); Oyuna told the story that kept everyone awake; the parcel's oldest label
(won in E05) reads *Ceres, twenty years ago* and carries an audit stamp PIP "knows"; Mattias Voss
saw the ship's call sign on Lucía's wrist (E05, 18) and shared a pastry with her (E05, 9); Voss's
job card is in Lucía's boot (E05); the crew owe Kaguya one impossible task and are owed one
favour by Lupin; Seo signs her reports "H.G." and Pell answers in text (E04). Lucía's count is
written as **twelve months and two days** on arrival (E05 ended at eleven months, twenty days)
and PIP's reports as **#1,250–#1,251**; both to be adjusted to Episode 6.

**Two choices made here that the showrunner should know about (also in WHERE-I-STOPPED).**
1. **Oyuna has no voice this episode** (resting it after Episode 6's all-night story; three chalk
   slates). This is how seven crew, Anansi, Carmen, Wen and Mattias fit into ten speaking roles.
2. **Bennu is not in the asteroid belt** and the script says so. It is a near-Earth asteroid that
   is thought to have come from the belt; in the story that is why Anansi banks there ("born in
   the Belt; it left"), it is reached by gate, and "What was real" states it plainly.

## 1. Logline

Home on Ceres, where the Voss water company owns every drop and her mother's debt, Lucía joins
the spider Anansi in a con that makes the Company lend its own money against its own dead debts;
for one afternoon she is paid in full, and then she learns the con was her mother's design, that
Anansi is selling the whole town to Soren Voss to buy back the stories he has mislaid, and that
the only way to free the town is to throw eleven thousand debts, and her own score, off an
asteroid by hand.

## 2. Why you keep watching

- **0:05** A turnstile bills the crew for the water inside their own bodies.
- **0:55** The clock: account 4471-C, supply ends in nine days. Jax's V.O. promises a fortune and
  its loss ("my engineer threw the money into space. By hand").
- **Act 1** Who dripped seawater on a post counter in the asteroid belt twenty years ago, and
  what did they say to PIP that made an incorruptible machine stamp a wet parcel and then lose
  the day?
- **Act 2** The rise. Will anyone look? And the Company's own Resident is helping them. Why?
- **Act 3** Every trap is in Carmen's handwriting. One thread of the web runs to Saturn.
- **Act 4** Two doors, both bad. What does a wrestler stand on to throw a town's debt away?
- **Tag** The spy is told to find the crew's informant. Neither knows the other.

## 6. Act breaks (each makes the next act a different story)
- **Act 1 out (6):** a debt nobody can pay becomes a bank they are standing in. Act 2 is the rise.
- **Act 2 out (13):** the mark asks for the whole town, and one thread runs to Saturn. Act 3 is
  finding out whose con this is.
- **Act 3 out (19):** the con is Carmen's, the customer is Voss, and the score is offered clean
  for doing nothing. Act 4 is a choice with a physics problem.
- **Act 4 (23–27):** the throw, the bill for it, and the kitchen.
- **Tag:** two informers, each now hunting the other without knowing it.

## 7. What it costs
- **Lucía** throws away the only wages she has ever been paid, and with them the crew's whole
  score, and knowingly lands the House's entire default on her mother.
- **Carmen** goes from a small water debt she controlled to a fortune owed to Soren Voss in
  person, and loses a trap two years in the drafting.
- **Ceres** is free of the water debt and has no credit and, from the first of the month, no
  pumps: a free town on bitter brine.
- **Jax** is a man who has not paid his engineer, again; and he chose her wrist over the box.
- **Anansi** loses the fourth delivery; his stories stay with Voss.
- **Mattias** is recalled home to explain himself, which is the worst place and the useful one.
- **The crew** now keep a secret from each other on purpose (the informant's name).

## 9. Running gags, escalated
1. **PIP's reports and who reads them.** For the first time a report *works*: the dog's deposit
   is refunded "per report #1,250", so the reader has reach. And the READ is 1.3 seconds again:
   whoever reads him is never further away than that. He files on himself: missing, one day.
2. **Nobody can remember Chichikov.** The Resident who employs him cannot ("I can never—"
   "Nobody can"), and the owner of every story ever told has not got one about him ("I find it
   rude"). He never speaks and is never sharp; he takes two small notes.
3. **A legend correcting Earth's version.** A leopard, not a tiger; four prices, not three, and
   his mother; "Earth says I learned humility. I was furious." The one thing he does not correct
   until the end is the one that flatters him: his wife planned every trap.
4. **Lucía's wage count.** Twelve months and two days; then, for the first time in the series,
   *nothing*; then twelve months and ten days. "Nine and a half. I had it for an afternoon. I'm
   counting that."
5. **"Courageous."** Wen defines it ("what an office says when it's afraid of you"); Carmen uses
   it as plain praise and Lucía flinches ("That word means no." "Not in this kitchen."); Pell,
   for the first time, says it and then orders the thing anyway.

## 10. Science notes for the critic
- **Bennu's place.** A near-Earth asteroid, not a main-belt one; thought to derive from the belt
  [gen]. Said in dialogue (5) and in "What was real". The location file lists it under "small
  bodies" only.
- **Fourteen minutes, Ceres to Vesta.** The file gives no figure. Both orbit in the main belt and
  their separation varies from well under 1 AU to over 5 AU, so one-way light time runs from a
  few minutes to about three quarters of an hour; 14 minutes (about 1.7 AU) is a plausible
  reading "this week" [gen]. The tag uses 26 minutes each way to Pell, inside the Ceres–Earth
  range of roughly a quarter to half an hour [gen]. Change either freely.
- **The throw.** Bennu's escape speed is of the order of 20 cm/s [gen], so a braced leg-drive
  sends even a massive net off for good, and by Newton's third law drives the thrower and what
  she stands on the other way. The script does not give a number.
- **The pad.** OSIRIS-REx's sampling arm met almost no resistance and sank in [brief; gen]. The
  script claims only that the machine "sank in at the touch".
- **Occator's salts.** The script says only "salts the brine left behind" and does not name a
  mineral, because the file (magnesium sulphate) and later Dawn results (sodium carbonate and
  chloride salts) differ. The clue therefore rests on smell and a salt stain, not on chemistry.
- **Water in a person, and in breath**: mostly water; about a cupful a day breathed out [gen].
