FIX 1 — TAG (unit 28), about line 3018: when label one fell off
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Now:
    JAX
    It fell off. Years ago.

Change to:
    JAX
    It fell off. In the spring.

Why: Episode 1 says "Thirty-two. One fell off in the spring", and Episode 5's cold open repeats it
("It fell off in the spring"). The ruling: label one fell off "in the spring"; "years ago" is the
error. Nothing else in the exchange needs to change: Oyuna's "It was sold. A strip of brown paper
with half a postmark..." still answers it. (In Episode 5 the prop is fixed as a torn strip showing
PIP's audit stamp, half a round date mark and one letter "…A"; "half a postmark" is consistent
with that. Do not add any legible place name to it.)

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FIX 2 — UNIT 27 (Deimos dock / bridge) and the standing rule: the READ stamp is one way
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The rule (Episode 3's, now law for the season): a READ stamp counts from SEND to the moment the
report is read, one way, on the reader's clock. PIP waits the full round trip to learn it. At
Mars, Phobos and Deimos that is 00:11:00, then 00:11:04, and PIP waits twenty-two minutes for the
receipt. Episode 4 currently prints the round trip. Four places:

(a) Standing staging rules, about lines 62–63. Now:
    "Earth is eleven minutes away each way, so any READ takes at least twenty-two minutes."
  Change to something like:
    "Earth is eleven minutes away each way. A READ stamp counts one way, so it says 00:11:04;
    PIP waits twenty-two minutes to see it."

(b) Unit 27 header, about line 2849. Now:
    "Place: light delay (READ in twenty-two minutes and four seconds)"
  Change to:
    "Place: light delay (READ — 00:11:04; the receipt reaches PIP twenty-two minutes after SEND)"

(c) Unit 27 insert, about line 2917. Now:
    "...a small grey line: READ — 00:22:04."
  Change to:
    "...a small grey line: READ — 00:11:04."

(d) Unit 27, PIP's speech, about lines 2919–2923. Now:
    PIP (CONT'D)
    Every one of them, Captain. Read. Even the thirteen. Twenty-two minutes and four seconds.
    They always take the four seconds.
  The last sentence has to change as well as the number. "Always" contradicts the Moon, where the
  stamp was a flat 1.3 seconds with nothing added (Episodes 1–2), and it contradicts Episode 3,
  where the first Mars stamp was 00:11:00 flat and PIP says of 00:11:04 that "they have never
  taken four seconds over anything of mine". Suggested replacement (33 words against 20,
  about four seconds longer):
    PIP (CONT'D)
    Every one of them, Captain. Read. Even the thirteen. Eleven minutes and four seconds each. I
    waited twenty-two to be told. They take the four seconds now. They never did on the Moon.
  Any wording is fine so long as: the stamp is eleven minutes and four seconds; the twenty-two
  minutes is PIP's wait, not the stamp; and the four seconds is something that began at Mars,
  not something "they always" do.

Not affected, leave as they are: unit 25's title and V.O. ("Twenty-two minutes. Eleven for the word
to reach Earth, and eleven for Earth to say so") describe a message and its answer, which is a
round trip and is correct; "Report twelve twenty-two" (about line 1188) is a report number.

Why it matters downstream: Episode 5, scene 4, now turns on the comparison. PIP: "At Mars it took
eleven minutes. Tonight, one point three seconds. Captain, the reader has moved." If Episode 4
still shows 00:22:04, that line is wrong on its face.
