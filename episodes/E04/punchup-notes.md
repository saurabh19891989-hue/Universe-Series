## What the story may still get wrong (for the critic)
- **Occultation timing.** Earth sets behind Mars as seen from Deimos 1:38 after they arrive, and
  the reply arrives at Phobos instead. That Phobos can see Earth at that moment is asserted, not
  computed. Earth is hidden from Deimos for roughly 80 minutes an orbit and from Phobos for
  roughly 50; the science critic should confirm that both can be true, or the board line in 25
  should go.
- **The song names Deimos at discovery** ("the first is found, and named for Dread"). Madan
  proposed the names after the discovery, not on the night. The song is poetic compression;
  "What was real" makes no claim about it. Untouched by rule.
- **"Bought at auction"** (28) for the first label and E05's "item seven" agree; how a label
  that "fell off in the spring" (E01) reached an auction is not explained. E05 may want a line.
- **The music box** now strikes the hour (52,596 hours in six years). If the art department has
  already planned a loop, the line in 26 needs the new arithmetic.
- **Can Arakawa's officer board at the edge?** Yes: the edge is outside the Exception. A viewer
  may wonder why she didn't board on the pad; the cold open says she can't come in.
- **Seo's order** was "make sure Miss Tseren stays on Phobos". She did: Oyuna was still on Phobos
  when the law changed. Her disobedience is the intercept, which she can explain as rescuing a
  ship's computer. Check that the critic reads this as explicable on paper, as the season plan
  requires.
- **Wen's line count.** She has seven slips. If the critic thinks that is too much reading on
  Deimos, cut "You're late." first, never the postscripts.

## Lines I'm unsure are in good taste
- **Pell to Idris: "You're not listed anywhere. I can promise you'll stay that way." / "It is from
  me."** It is a threat to keep a man stateless on paper, and Idris is the Arab member of the crew.
  It is written as Pell's menace and lands on Pell, not on Idris, and it matches E03's canon (Idris
  "is not listed anywhere"). Flag for the owner; if in doubt, give the bid to Jax instead.
- **"A hundred and eight say, 'Mother asks after the donkey.'"** The mother's death is told by
  arithmetic. I think it is tender, not glib; keep the TTS plain and slow, no music under it.
- **"They don't want to start with a donkey."** Aimed at the Office's dignity, not the animal or
  Nasreddin. Fine, I think.
- **Nasreddin** has no religious material anywhere, no title spoken, and no nationality; his three
  new turns (the grey beard, the ladder, "the very question I was about to ask myself") are all
  from Borrow's secular tales on card 06.
- **Oyuna** is never the joke; the only laugh near the song (Idris's "recited") is her correcting
  him. The song still needs a tuuli performer's check before lock.
- **Lucía's "In my family that's a wedding"** (10, draft 1) jokes about her family and kicked
  machines, not about Mexico. Kept.
