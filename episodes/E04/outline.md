# SECRET UNIVERSE — Episode 4: "The Phobos Exception"

**Outline v1, head writer.** Written with the full script in one pass (owner's instruction: no
stop for a ruling); punch-up writer and independent critic follow. Genre: farce, dry British
institutional comedy at speed. Music: clarinet, darbuka, and a very small march (an 1877 brass
music box in the waiting room that never plays its last bar until the end).
Running time: about 36:25 honest (cold open 1:30 · title 0:10 · Act 1 7:25 · Act 2 8:50 · Act 3
8:10 · Act 4 9:00 · tag 0:40 · "What was real" 0:40). 4,314 spoken words. 29 units.

**Picks up from E03 (on disk) and E02 (outline).** The parcel left Mars relabelled by Don Quixote
"c/o She Who Remembers Every Name — Phobos", so this is a delivery. Idris and the old dog are
aboard. Lucía is owed ten months and one week. Mira swore before the Atlas that she saw a giant;
Pell keeps a copy. Seo alone saw the Mars kiosk answer NO SUCH PERSON for Jax and has told no one.
Pell's last message to Seo: "Arakawa reaches Phobos in two sols." PIP's last report was #1,219;
E05 starts at #1,240. Earth is eleven minutes away each way; READ comes back at 22:04. The Baron
stayed on the Moon; the crew owe Kaguya one impossible task; PIP lost the parcel's oldest label
from memory; Seo reports to Pell as "H.G."; Arakawa has the case.

**The rule in one breath, for viewers with no interest in law:** *On Phobos no law applies, so you
can't be arrested, and no law lets you leave. You could leave on a bicycle. Arakawa waits at the
edge for anyone who does.*

## 1. Logline and why you keep watching

**Logline:** A courier crew lands on Phobos to deliver a parcel to the woman who memorised the
whole Atlas, and finds a moon exempt from every law, including the one that lets you leave. With
a Directorate auditor taking a ticket in the same waiting room, the only man who ever beat the
system teaches them to ask the rule where it came from, and the answer is a typo from 1877.

**Why you keep watching:** Everyone on Fear and Dread talks on paper: a ticket machine that can
only print forms, a commander who won't speak where nothing binds, a dispatcher who only counts
what's logged. PIP falls in love with the machine, and the plan to free the woman requires him to
post himself away from her and to become the report that closes her office. The audience knows
the pilot has been ordered to keep the prisoner in; the crew do not.

## 6. Running gags, escalated
1. **PIP's reports and who reads them.** On Phobos they bounce (RETURNED — NO LAWFUL ORIGIN). He
   writes "Dear Reader" for the first time. The Registrar stamps even the returned one: the first
   reader he can see. He *becomes* a report (#1,231) to get out. Then every backlog report comes
   back READ at 22:04: "They always take the four seconds." And someone files on *him*: FINDINGS: NONE.
2. **Nobody can remember Chichikov.** Still silent since the Moon. Arakawa, the most observant
   officer in the Directorate, writes "WHO —" and can't finish. And then the escalation: Nasreddin
   remembers him perfectly, by name and patronymic, and Chichikov's pen crosses out "THE OLD MAN
   REMEMBERED ME".
3. **A legend correcting Earth's version.** Nasreddin: the donkey backwards is a division of labour;
   "a thousand stories with my name on them, I was in about forty". Oyuna corrects Earth about
   her own tradition ("not recited"), and the episode's whole plot is the Atlas's own version
   corrected by the singer's.
4. **Lucía's wage count.** "Ten months, one week and two days" is worth nothing on Phobos (no law),
   is offered in full by Pell, is traded for a postbox by Jax, and on Deimos, where law resumes,
   "Here it's eleven."
5. **New this episode: everyone on Fear and Dread talks on paper** (the Registrar, Arakawa, Wen).

## 7. Science used (for the critic)
Phobos ~27 × 22 × 18 km; surface gravity ~0.0057 m/s² (less than a thousandth of Earth's; a 75 kg
man weighs ~44 g there); escape speed ~11 m/s; a card dropped from 1.5 m falls for ~23 s; a 4 m/s
jump lasts ~23 min ignoring rotation. Orbit 7 h 39 min; tidally locked, so Mars hangs fixed in its
sky, about 42° across (some 80 times the full Moon seen from Earth); from Mars it rises in the west
(used in E03). Phobos passes through Mars's shadow on most orbits, for up to about an hour.
Stickney ~9 km, named after Angeline Stickney Hall. Craters Flimnap, Reldresal, Skyresh, Gulliver
and others from *Gulliver's Travels*; Deimos has craters Swift and Voltaire. Kepler misread
Galileo's 1610 anagram as "children of Mars", twins; Swift gave Mars two moons in 1726. Hall found
Deimos on 12 Aug 1877 and Phobos six nights later. Names from *Iliad* XV, proposed by Henry Madan
of Eton. Phobos is spiralling inward (~2 m per century) and will break up or hit Mars in tens of
millions of years. Earth–Mars light time 3–22 min each way (11 min used, as in E03). Mars blocks
Earth from an orbiter for part of each orbit. Phobos–Deimos minimum-energy transfer ~9 hours.
