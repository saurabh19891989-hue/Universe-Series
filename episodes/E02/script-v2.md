# SECRET UNIVERSE
## Episode 2 — "The Princess of the Far Side"
### Shooting script, draft 2 (comedy and dialogue punch-up of draft 1)

Spoken words: **WORDCOUNT** (counted by script: dialogue lines only, including "What was real";
cues, parentheticals and action excluded; same counter as draft 1, which counts 4,337 by it). Units:
32 (cold open, 30 scenes, tag), plus "What was real". Jax narrates three times (8, and twice in 30);
his other V.O. cues in 25 are his voice heard over the radio on the *Steadfast*.

Runtime: the scene targets below are honest (dialogue at 155 wpm, plus 0.4 s per speaker change,
plus each scene's stated holds and insert-only beats, rounded up to 5 s). They add up to **RUNTIME**
including the 0:10 title and the 0:45 "What was real". ACTSPLIT. No scene is over 2:30. About 18%
of the running time is aboard *The Unscheduled* (unchanged from draft 1).

Visual style: photoreal characters and sets composited with real NASA footage, staged mostly
as cinematic stills with camera moves and limited motion. Lip movement is scarce or absent.

**How to read the headers.** Each unit lists its number, setting, thread (A = Kaguya and the
siege · B = Mira and Jax; Lucía and Seo · C = Seo the rat, Voss and Chichikov, PIP's reader,
Jax's past), honest target seconds, visual class (REAL FOOTAGE WIDE / PHOTOREAL SET STILL /
PHOTOREAL CLOSE-UP / INSERT / MONTAGE / DATA VISUAL), and a STAGING note saying how it plays
without lip-sync.

**Standing staging rules (they apply everywhere unless a scene says otherwise)**
- **Mouths are hidden or off-screen whenever someone speaks.** The episode has **four** marked
  exceptions, each tagged **[VISIBLE MOUTH n/4]**. For those, paste only the mouth region onto
  the still (IMAGE-PIPELINE §3a).
- **Kaguya speaks from behind a bamboo blind**, as Heian ladies received visitors. Her one great
  gesture is to lower her fan and show her eyes, and that is always a silent beat. She writes with
  a brush (INSERT). Outside, she wears a white suit with a smoked visor.
- **Astolfo keeps his visor down**, an old knight's helm over a pressure seal. He lifts it once, in
  22, for a silent beat.
- **Seo is in a flight helmet or seen from behind in the pilot's seat.** Her gloved hands on the
  controls are her signature insert.
- **Arakawa is radio only to the crew.** On her own bridge she is a profile against the far side
  from orbit (real footage), lit by the console. Her face is never seen speaking.
- **The Baron's moustache covers his mouth completely.** He is bareheaded and coatless (his coat
  and tricorn are on the horse's head at Rim Port). His colour, about 60% at the start, **comes
  back** in the Valley: 70% in Act 2, full by 22. Never make him look younger.
- **Chichikov is in soft focus and never speaks.** No line all episode; nobody remarks on it.
- **PIP speaks with his amber lens.** Every report gets an INSERT of its number and status. They
  continue from #1,207: #1,208 (3, the watch), #1,209 (4, the lost-property office), #1,210 (10,
  the dead letters) and #1,211 (23, on no one) come back UNSENT until 25.
- **Lucía**: welding visor, back to camera, half inside machines; the ribbon on her wrist.
- **Mira**: the loupe, her notebook, the back of her head. Reflections are for silent beats only.
- **Exteriors are always in helmets**, with dialogue on suit radio over real footage.
- **Default cut: hold on the listener.**
- **Places and light (rulebook, Places).** About a fifth of the running time is aboard the ship
  (0, 1, half of 5 and 8, 25, 28, 30, part of the tag). Everything else plays in six bright
  places, each with its own colour world: **the Valley** (hard white sun, black sky, the glint of
  silver and gold heaps), **the Keeping House** (warm amber lamplight, red lacquer, honey-coloured
  bamboo), **the Lost Workshop** (forge orange on a wall of every tool ever dropped),
  **the dead-letter vault** (a rainbow mountain of envelopes and stamps under paper lanterns),
  **Seo's wreck** (a red-and-white shuttle in a crater of blinding sun), and **the *Steadfast***
  (steel blue, the sunlit far side filling the window). **The far side is not dark.** Darkness is
  used twice, as a story event: the sunset run (27–28) and the shelf at night (tag).

**Voice and TTS notes**
- KAGUYA: low, unhurried, dry, a thousand years tired and amused by it. She never raises her voice.
  Her English is precise with a light Japanese colour. **No comic accent.**
- ASTOLFO: bright, hearty English, an old-fashioned knight who is having a lovely time.
- SEO: calm, low, economical; Korean-born, idiomatic English. She never hurries a sentence, except
  once (23, "Stop.").
- ARAKAWA: formal, exact, slightly slower than anyone else, with a Japanese colour. Radio is
  narrow-band with the faint delay of distance (L2 is close, so no comic lag).
- PIP in 23: the same voice, but warm, slow and pleasant, like a stranger being kind. No slurring,
  no glitch effects. The fright is that he sounds happy.
- Radio colours as in E01. The Valley's Keeping House is pressurised and has a small, stony reverb.
- Say it: Kaguya "KAH-goo-yah" · Astolfo "ah-STOL-foh" · Han Seo-yeon "HAHN suh-YAWN" · Hong
  Gildong "HONG GIL-dong" · Arakawa "ah-RAH-kah-wah" · Tomoe "TOH-moh-eh" · Smyrna "SMUR-nah" ·
  #1,208 read "twelve-oh-eight".

```

INSERT: in the dark of the trunk, a great white moustache. Two

                         LUCÍA
          ...Hello.

          Señorita. You carried me up the ramp.

                         LUCÍA
          I did?

                         BARON
          Beautifully. I have never been lifted

          You said you were staying on the Moon.

          I am on the Moon. I was very precise.

                         BARON
          And I meant every word. I simply

          My hat.

                         LUCÍA
          You left it on the horse.
```

### 4 · THE KEEPING HOUSE — FIRST MEETING WITH KAGUYA
