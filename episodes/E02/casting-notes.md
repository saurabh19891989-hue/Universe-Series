# Episode 2 — Casting and design notes for the art department (script draft 3, locked)

Style: as `canon/CHARACTER-LOCKS.md`. Photoreal, consistent from shot to shot, and graded to
match the NASA plate. Generate sets with pure-black windows and composite the real footage
ourselves (IMAGE-PIPELINE §3a). Every description below is **PROPOSED** until the owner approves a
master. Once approved, use the description verbatim in prompts with the master as reference.
Design from the source texts and real period dress only: **never from any film, anime or game**
(no Ghibli Kaguya, no anime Astolfo).

**What changed in draft 3 (read this first).**
1. **Every view from the far-side surface is an illustrated plate**, not real footage (none exists).
   That is about eleven exterior set-ups; build **one hero wide of the Valley** and crop it.
2. **Geography is fixed** (below). The ship is on a rim ledge until scene 8, then under the rim
   wall for the rest of the episode, **in sunlight**.
3. **Two scenes left the bamboo blind for the open Valley**: 19 (under the ship: the Voss drone on
   the hull, Lucía's leap) and 22 (the silver heap). New images are listed in section 5.
4. **Two clocks** are in shot all episode: SUNSET (a countdown) and AIR (a percentage), on Jax's
   wrist screen, on the bridge and on Arakawa's console. Screen text is added in code, not generated.
5. The Baron wears a **bare helmet** outdoors (5, 15, 22, 26). Seo's photograph lives in **her own
   flight helmet** (13, tag). PIP's **wrist relay** is introduced in 3.
6. Status column below uses `assets/LIBRARY-INDEX.md` as of 27 Nov. I have not looked at any image.

## 1. Geography (the same in every shot)

- The Valley is a crater just beyond the Moon's **west limb**. Black sky, hard low sun.
- **Earth is never in the sky.** It is just below the horizon in one direction (toward the near
  side). In the cold open and in 8 only, a hair of blue shows above a ridge in that direction.
- **The Sun is low on the opposite side** and gets lower all episode: long shadows by 21 and 22,
  the line of night crossing the Valley in 26. Shadows point toward the Earthward wall.
- **The Earthward rim wall is the sunlit one.** High on it is **the ledge** where the ship stands in
  0–7. In 8 the ship drops to the **foot of that wall** and stays there (15, 18's drone image, 19,
  26): hidden from Earth by the wall, but in full sun.
- **The Keeping House** is cut into the same sunlit wall, further along. Its steps look across a bay
  of heaps to the ship. Kaguya on the steps is a small white figure seen from under the ship (19) and
  from the silver heap (22).
- **Seo's wreck** is in a small crater three hundred metres along, with the ledge and the ship in
  the background of its wide (6–7).
- **The Valley's edge** (21) is the crater's lip, looking toward where Earth would be: the Sun is
  behind the two figures, the sky ahead is black and starry with no Earth.
- **The *Steadfast*** is at L2, far beyond the Moon: its window shows the **whole far side, sunlit**
  (5, 18, 25), and dark in the tag.
- **Earthrise (29)** and **Earth in the window (30)**: Earth is **nearly full**, about 2° across (a
  bright marble, not a window full of Earth). The ground under it is in night, tinted blue-grey.
