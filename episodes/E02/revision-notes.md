critic's review (`critic-review.md`, READY AFTER FIXES) and the showrunner's eight rulings on it.

**Size.** **4,072 spoken words** by script (dialogue lines only, including "What was real"; the
same count that gives draft 2 its 3,966), 479 cues. Scene targets add up to **35:40** with the 0:10
title and "What was real". Cold open 1:10 · Act 1 7:25 · Act 2 10:05 · Act 3 7:30 · Act 4 8:10 ·

**How the runtime was estimated.** Draft 2 assumed 155 words a minute; the Episode 1 table read
measured **184**. So the model is now: speech at 184 wpm, plus 0.97 s per speaker cue (the Episode 1
shot plan allowed 0.5 s between lines and 0.6 s of breath per dialogue shot: 477 cues, 372 dialogue
shots), plus 1.5 s for each stand-alone INSERT and each stated beat, silence or hold, plus
picture-only time listed by hand (the montage at 40 s, the sunset and Earthrise plates at 20 s
each, the held lens stills in 23, the readouts in 25, the text the viewer must read in the tag).
Check: the same formula on Episode 1's `script-v3.md` gives 35:27; its shot plan, built from real
table-read line lengths, came to 35:34. For this script it gives speech 22:08, cue gaps 7:45,
picture-only 5:52, title 0:10 = 35:55 before rounding each scene to 5 s (35:40 after). Expect the
shot plan to land within about a minute either way. The voices do not need slowing.

**Not checked:** any audio (agents cannot hear it); the picture assets; the Earth phase in the
Earthrise plate (must be checked by eye, see 29); the Japanese and Italian originals (library cards

## 6. Declined, adapted, or needing the showrunner

1. **Bible (SHOULD-FIX 25): not edited.** `canon/SERIES-BIBLE.md` still lists the Baron as a
   recurring stowaway. The ruling says he stays on the Moon. Showrunner to align.
2. **155 wpm (part of SHOULD-FIX 22): declined.** Planned at the measured 184 wpm, as briefed.
3. **A Heian musical colour (NICE-TO-HAVE 5): not applied.** Owner's call; canon names the instruments.
4. **"The Valley's shadow" is a shadow from Earth, not from the Sun.** With the Valley beyond the
   west limb and the Sun setting, the wall that hides the ship from Earth is the sunlit one. The
   script says "tight in under the rim wall, until the wall itself stands between it and the
   horizon" and the header tells the art department to keep the ship in sunlight. If you want the
   ship literally in darkness, the Sun has to be on the other side, and that breaks the nearly
   full Earthrise; tell me which you prefer.
5. **18 sits after 15, not after 13** (reason in MUST-FIX 6). Easy to move back if you prefer.
6. **Three hundred metres**, not the critic's five hundred (SHOULD-FIX 6).
7. **The cutter's two-hour descent** from L2 is our number (about 60,000 km; fast, not absurd for
   an Enforcement cutter). It is what makes the slip true and the cost visible. It is not a real fact
   and is not in "What was real".
8. **READ stamps now say 1.7 s** (the path through the relay). E05's last scene holds a READ
   receipt "1.3 seconds" at Venus; whoever revises E05 should look at that.
9. **PIP's reports** are #1,208 (watch), #1,209 (dead letters), #1,210 (the crow, new), #1,211
   (on no one). The lost-property-office report was cut with its exchange in 4. E03 starts at #1,215.
10. **Role table:** Lucía is now "lean and athletic", to match CHARACTER-LOCKS (draft 2 said "broad
    shoulders"). Arakawa is still "about 50" here and "40s" in CHARACTER-LOCKS: still open.
11. **Temi's "Engine release takes five working days"** is new. It explains why paying the bank
    stops the air falling at once but leaves one engine for the run. Nothing in E03 contradicts it.
12. **Not addressed:** the critic's wish for a moment where Mira's method fails and costs her.
    That is a bigger change than a revision pass should make; the word "cartographer" is the small
    version.
