**Standing rules for picture and performance**
- **Reaction faces (engine 8), one per regular, always the same pose, held 1.5 s on a clean background:**
  **JAX — THE SALESMAN'S FREEZE** (cold open) · **BARON — THE DROOP** (1) · **LUCÍA — THE ARM-FOLD** (5) ·

  **PIP — THE DOT** (10). Each is a TAKE line below.
- **Three drawn styles of our own, each for one teller:**
  - **Kaguya's world — woodblock style.** Flat colour as if printed from carved blocks, a bold black
    keyline, colour that fades in soft bands across sky and water, horizontal bands of stylised mist,
    a faint wood-grain texture, a small palette (indigo, vermilion, pale green, gold). Inspired by
    public-domain Japanese woodblock prints in general; never traced from any print; never the look of
    any film, anime or game. Her images print themselves *onto her lit blind* (4) and onto the black sky
    at the edge (14).
  - **The Baron — engraving style** as E01: black cross-hatched line on warm cream, after the 1862 Doré
    plates in spirit, never traced. **Thinning rule:** he arrives at 60% colour (moustache the colour of
    old paper) and recovers in the Valley, where nothing fades: 70% by 4, 80% by 8, full in Part Two. In 13
    his engraved tales draw themselves in full colour and, for the first time, **do not break**. Never
    make him younger.
  - **Jax's lie — "salesman's poster" style** (7 only): thick wobbly outline, too-bright poster paint,
    speed lines, Jax a head taller with a bigger chin. Lucía's invoice erases it item by item into the
    plain truth.
- **Kaguya is seen face to face only once (14).** Indoors she speaks only from behind a bamboo blind **lit from
  behind, so she is a sharp black silhouette on it**: the silhouette acts with her whole body (the fan,
  the sleeves, the hair pooled on the floor, the shoulders when she laughs) and needs no lip-sync. Her one
  great gesture is a silent beat: the fan lowers and **two slats part a finger's width**, and through the
  gap we see her real eyes. Outdoors she wears a clear helmet with **a sheer travelling veil hung outside the
  dome from a brim ring, to the shoulders**, in the Heian manner (in vacuum it hangs still and moves only
  when she does), and speaks on suit radio. **The one partial reveal** is in 14, on "If I remembered how,
  I'd ask you to stay.": her glove lifts one side of the veil and we see her face in three-quarter through
  the dome, lit by his helmet light, until the veil falls on the HOLD. Draw it from the locked master only
  (plum-and-cream court robes, very long straight black hair), never toward any screen Kaguya.
- **Respect (CLAUDE.md, bible rule 7).** Kaguya is the princess of the old folktale, not a deity. **The
  Keeping House is a Heian palace, not a shrine:** unpainted cypress pillars, white plaster, black lacquer
  trim, bamboo blinds; **no vermilion pillars or doors** (the locked set's red pillars are repainted) and no
  shrine or temple fittings (no torii, no altars, no offerings, no bells or incense); no halos, no Buddhist
  imagery, no heavenly procession or cloud-borne host for the robe. Jax's poster serpent must not read as an
  East Asian dragon (no horns, whiskers or pearl). Of the tale's
  five tasks we use only the branch and the dragon's jewel (the Buddha's begging bowl is never mentioned).
- **Arakawa** (a woman in her 40s, per the locked master) is mostly a voice and, in 16, a growing light.
  On her own bridge she is seen from behind her shoulder against the window, or as her hands on the
  console, **until 16, where we see her face for the first time, speaking the hillside line** (ruling 2).
  To the crew she is a courteous voice on the band, counting down to sunset (3, 6, 9, 14, 15, 16), never

- **Chichikov** is drawn by the faint rule from E01 (unfinished, broken line; colour washed toward grey;

  speaks. He takes Arakawa's teacup.
- **PIP.** On the bridge, the brass-and-cream desk machine (locked prop): amber lens, stamp arm. **New in

- **Seo** wears her scuffed flight helmet indoors with the visor up (a pilot's habit; her face reads),

**Geography (keep it the same in every shot).** The Valley lies just beyond the Moon's limb, on the far
side. Earth is just below the horizon toward the near side and peeps over it when the Moon wobbles. The
Sun is low in the opposite direction, so the Valley's Earthward wall faces the Sun and is sunlit. The cold
open sets the ship on **a ledge high on the Valley's rim**; in 5 Seo moves it **down under the Earthward
wall**, which then stands between the ship and Earth, in full sunlight. The Keeping House is cut into the
same wall; its steps look across a bay of heaps to the ship. The *Steadfast* and the relay ("the Lantern")
hang far beyond the Moon at L2, seeing the whole sunlit far side; the cutter needs two hours to come down.
**From the Valley, L2 is low over the western rim, near the Sun** (5, 16). **The siege rules, as the
viewer hears them:** no signal reaches the far side but through the relay, and the bank is first in its
queue at sunset (3); if the ship lifts, Arakawa sees it (3); she will not come down before sunset (3); so
the crew go at sunset, in the dark (10, 11), and what goes wrong is the fin still in sunlight (16).
Sunset here falls near new Moon, so the Earth that rises in 17 is **nearly full** and the near side under
it is in night, lit blue-grey by earthlight.
