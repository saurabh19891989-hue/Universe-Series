# Episode 2 — Sources

Ep 2 mixes **eight sources from seven cultures**: Japanese, Italian, Korean, Chinese, Mongolian,
German and Russian, with the Greek plants carried over from E01. Mechanisms, images and traits
are taken; **the plot is ours, and nothing is retold scene by scene.** The editions are the ones
the library cards record as actually read (in `research/library/sources/`), unless marked
otherwise. No film, anime or game version was used.

---

## 7. Nikolai Gogol, *Dead Souls* (Russia, 1842)
**Edition:** as in E01 sources.md (check against a public-domain translation before lock).

## 8. Homer, *The Odyssey* (Greece): plants only
**Edition:** as in E01 sources.md (Butler 1900 / Butcher and Lang 1879; not opened). "You've worn

Nobody, Book 9; Calypso the Concealer, Book 5). Nothing is stated, and no scene is retold.

## Wholly ours
The siege's terms (the Lantern relay, the sunset expiry, the Moon's two faces); Arakawa,
the *Steadfast*, and her honour over the payment; Seo's cut fuel line, her "never lie in the air"
and her mother's photo; the coffee-machine branch and Lucía's invoice; sighs as air; the
dead-letter mountain and the parcel's lost invoice to Mars; PIP choosing the robe and losing one
label; the forgotten 1889 deposit; the debt of one impossible task; the Baron staying; the flask
labelled NOBODY; every line of dialogue.
