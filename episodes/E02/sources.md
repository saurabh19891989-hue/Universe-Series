# Episode 2 — Sources (script draft 1)

Ep 2 mixes **the Bamboo Cutter (Japanese), Orlando Furioso (Italian), Hong Gildong (Korean, as a
plant), Munchausen (German) and the Odyssey (Greek, hidden)**. It also uses one structural idea from
the Mongolian Geser epic, and carries Gogol's Chichikov (Russian) over from E01. We take mechanisms,
images and traits; **the plot is ours, and nothing is retold scene by scene.** Editions are those
the library cards record as actually read (`research/library/sources/`), unless marked otherwise.
No film, anime or game version was used.

---

Internet Archive. Public domain. Dickins omits the fifth quest (the swallow's cowry), so it is not
used. Avoid the look of the 2013 Ghibli film.

## 5. Homer, *The Odyssey* (Greece): hidden
**Edition:** as in E01 sources.md (Butler 1900 / Butcher and Lang 1879; not opened). Two plants
only, with nothing stated and no scene retold: "You've worn one before" (16), pointing to the
bible's §6 (Calypso the Concealer, Book 5), and the flask labelled **NOBODY** (tag; the name given
to the Cyclops, Book 9). "Someone who's very good at leaving" (21) and "Things do [come back], to
you" (22) are our own echoes.

## 6. *Gessar Khan* (Mongolian Geser epic): one structural idea

| Taken | Kind | Scene | What is ours |
|---|---|---|---|
| A second kind of forgetting, brought on by comfort after victory | Mechanism | 22: "Comfort is the other robe, Hieronymus" | Applying it to the Baron's staying. Geser is venerated, so only the structure is used and the hero does not appear |

## 7. Gogol, *Dead Souls* (Russia, 1842): carried over
Chichikov's forgettable ordinariness, escalated here to having no lines at all, plus his notebook
(18, 25: FAR SIDE · VALLEY; CREDITOR — THE PRINCESS). All of his action is ours.

## Considered, not used
- **Pu Songling, *Strange Stories from a Chinese Studio*** (Giles, 1880; card 19): the moon-lady's
  song, "Too lonely and cold is my home in the moon", was proposed as a distant neighbour's song.
  **Cut by showrunner ruling.** The figure is worshipped in living practice, so identifying her is an
  owner decision. The script keeps only a wordless, unexplained distant woman's voice on the
  receiver (21), which Kaguya switches off without comment. That voice is ours and is not attributed
  to any source.

## Wholly ours
The siege's terms (the Lantern relay, the sunset expiry, the Moon's two faces); Arakawa, the
*Steadfast*, her promise not to come down before sunset, kept at a cost, and her honour over the
payment; Seo's cut fuel line, her mother's photograph and "never lie in the air"; the coffee-machine
branch and Lucía's invoice; sighs as air; the Lost Workshop; the dead-letter vault and the parcel's
lost invoice (next forwarding office: La Mancha Station, Mars); PIP choosing the robe and losing
label one; the forgotten 1889 deposit; the debt of one impossible task; the Baron staying; the
flask labelled NOBODY; every line of dialogue.
