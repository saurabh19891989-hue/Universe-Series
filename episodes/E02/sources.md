# Episode 2 — Sources (script draft 3, locked)

Ep 2 mixes **the Bamboo Cutter (Japanese), Orlando Furioso (Italian), Hong Gildong (Korean, as a
plant), Munchausen (German) and the Odyssey (Greek, hidden)**. It also uses one structural idea from
the Mongolian Geser epic, and carries Gogol's Chichikov (Russian) over from E01. We take mechanisms,
images and traits; **the plot is ours, and nothing is retold scene by scene.** Editions are those
the library cards record as actually read (`research/library/sources/`), unless marked otherwise.
No film, anime or game version was used. Scene numbers are those of `script-v3.md` (draft 2's
numbers, kept; 5 plays before 4, 6 is merged into 7, 18 plays after 15).

Changed in draft 3: scene references brought up to date; two stale quotations removed (Astolfo's
flask lines); the dragon's-jewel wording noted; the relay row corrected; the light-time row added;
"wholly ours" extended (the watch, the Yokohama deposit, the drone on the hull, the two readouts).

Internet Archive. Public domain. Dickins omits the fifth quest (the swallow's cowry), so it is not
used. Avoid the look of the 2013 Ghibli film.

## 5. Homer, *The Odyssey* (Greece): hidden
**Edition:** as in E01 sources.md (Butler 1900 / Butcher and Lang 1879; not opened). Two plants
only, with nothing stated and no scene retold: "You've worn one before" (16), pointing to the
bible's §6 (Calypso the Concealer, Book 5), and the flask labelled **NOBODY** (tag; the name given
to the Cyclops, Book 9). "Someone who's very good at leaving" (21) and "Things do [come back], to
you" (22) are our own echoes. Draft 3 removes two lines that leaned on the clue ("Does that sound
familiar, Captain?" and "SEVEN YEARS" on Mira's page).

## 7. Gogol, *Dead Souls* (Russia, 1842): carried over
Chichikov's forgettable ordinariness, escalated here to having no lines at all, plus his notebook
(18, 25: FAR SIDE · VALLEY; CREDITOR — THE PRINCESS). All of his action is ours.

## Considered, not used
- **Pu Songling, *Strange Stories from a Chinese Studio*** (Giles, 1880; card 19): the moon-lady's
  song, "Too lonely and cold is my home in the moon", was proposed as a distant neighbour's song.
  **Cut by showrunner ruling.** The figure is worshipped in living practice, so identifying her is an
  owner decision. The script keeps only a wordless, unexplained distant woman's voice on the
  receiver (21), which Kaguya switches off without comment. That voice is ours and is not attributed
  to any source. Cue title: "Distant voice", nothing more.
- **Lewis Carroll** (public domain): draft 2's act title "Five Impossible Things" echoed "six
  impossible things before breakfast". The act is now "The Tasks". The Baron's "Once a day, believe
  something you can't prove" (26) stays; it is aimed at Mira, not at Carroll.
- "Papers, please" (a stock phrase and a 2013 game title) is gone from 7.

"What was real" names only the first. Our fictional relay ("the Lantern") sits at L2. **Footage.** Real footage and stills are used only for views **from space**: the far side in the
*Steadfast*'s window (5, 18, 25, tag), the line of night (27), the Earthrise (29), Earth in the window
(30), and "What was real". Candidates, all in `assets/candidates.json` (location 2) or
`assets/e01-plates.json`, licence "NASA media: generally not subject to copyright in the US":
- Earthrise (29): "Earthrise after Orion Executes Outbound Powered Flyby" (ART-CMA1_2022_325_1250…,
  457 s, NASA, Orion camera, Artemis I, 21 Nov 2022) or "Earth Rise as Seen from Orion Spacecraft"
  (ART-SAW4_2022_325_1305…, 142 s). **Check by eye that Earth is nearly full.** Not "Flight Day 20:
  Crescent Earthrise" and not the Artemis II crescent frames: wrong phase for this story.
- Far side from space (5, 18, 25, 27): "The Far Side of the Moon — And All the Way Around"
  (GSFC_20171208_Archive_e001939, NASA/Goddard/Arizona State University; an LRO mosaic, so credit it
  as a visualisation); Artemis II crew photographs of the far side (for example art002e012702
  "Poynting and Keeler Craters on the Lunar Far Side", art002e009281 "Shadows at the Edge of Lunar
  Day", art002e021296 "Hertzsprung in Light and Shadow"; credit "NASA").
- Possible plate for "What was real": art002e021283 "Taking a Peek at Earth from the Far Side of the
  Moon" (NASA). Confirm its credit line before use.
- **No ground-level far-side footage exists in the library** (location file 02 says so). Every view
  from the surface is an illustrated plate, built on LRO/LOLA terrain where possible, and "What was
  real" says so in one plain sentence.
Strip original audio; no NASA or other agency logos on fictional things; no implied endorsement.

## Wholly ours
The siege's terms (the Lantern relay, the sunset expiry, the Moon's two faces); Arakawa, the
*Steadfast*, her radio countdown, her promise not to come down before sunset, and its cost shown as
two readouts drifting apart; Seo's cut fuel line, her mother's photograph and "never lie in the
air"; the coffee-machine branch and Lucía's invoice; sighs as air; the Lost Workshop; the
dead-letter vault and the parcel's lost invoice (next forwarding office: La Mancha Station, Mars);
the Voss drone on the hull and Lucía taking it off; PIP choosing the robe and losing the record of
the first posting; the gold watch (a grain merchant of Smyrna, 1851); the forgotten 1889 deposit (a
merchant in Yokohama); Temi's messages; the debt of one impossible task; the Baron staying; the
flask labelled NOBODY; every line of dialogue.
