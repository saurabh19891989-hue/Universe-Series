## 3. What was cut, merged, added, and why

**Why the cuts are deep.** Draft 3 was 35:40 on the old model; the ruled window is 23:30–24:15 with the
allowances. That is a third of the episode. I followed the audit's trims first (cut 17; stop explaining
the plan in 24; merge 3 into the walk; trim 1, 11, 15, 19, 21, 22, 26, 30), then merged scenes that
share a place or a turn (4 + 9; 6–7 + 8; 11 + 12 + 10; 19 + 20 + 16; 23 + 24; 25 + 18), then took lines.
**Every plant and every line a later script quotes was searched for and kept** (section 5). Jokes went
only where the same beat already had a laugh (see section 8, "lost").

**Restaged for animation.** All "no visible mouth" staging is gone. Faces act inside clear domes. Three
rules are kept because they are character, not pipeline: Kaguya never face to face (silhouette indoors,
veil outdoors), Arakawa never seen speaking, Chichikov silent and faint. Reaction faces are written by
name for every regular, and Seo gets a new one (THE BLINK). Physical business by name: the watch set
down "as if it might tell on him", the screwdriver arc, the tape, the leap, the lope with PIP under one
arm, the Baron sinking into the chaff, Lucía with the ribbon in her teeth.

**Three drawn styles of our own** (rulings: tall tales drawn; thinning holds): Kaguya's woodblock style
(her tasks printed on her blind, 4; the bamboo grove lifted off the sky, 14), inspired by public-domain
Japanese woodblock prints in general and never by any film or anime; the Baron's engraving style,
thinning from 60% to full and, in 13, holding for the first time; Jax's "salesman's poster" for the lie
(7), which the invoice erases. Lucía's invoice is the folktale's own turn: in the Bamboo Cutter the
forged branch is exposed when the unpaid craftsmen arrive at the palace.

**Logic and science fixes (the faults the E01 critic catches):**
- *Earth "rising" in the cold open:* libration is too slow to see, so draft 3 had a rising Earth that
  "does not visibly move". Now the ship is in low orbit, where Earth really does climb over the limb in
  seconds; Jax dives to the surface to put the horizon back between them.
- *"Since Tuesday":* E01 ends hours earlier, so the Baron cannot have been in the trunk for days. Now:
  "It's still Tuesday." / "It has been a very long Tuesday."
- *Seo's wreck:* draft 3 had her down "two days" before a crew who had only just arrived. Now she comes
  down after them, which is why Lucía distrusts it; "since I came down" is implied, not stated.
- *Seo's photograph:* the flight helmet she wears in 10 cannot also lie on the shuttle seat in 12; she
  comes back for a photo left behind the panel and moves it into the helmet's lining (where E03 and the
  tag have it).
- *Stars in sunlight (draft 3's "licence"):* now they sit in a boulder's shadow and the stars appear as
  their eyes adapt.
- *The escape:* the plan is never explained; what goes wrong is real physics. After sunset the ground is
  dark but anything above it stays sunlit for a while; the shadow's depth grows with distance from the
  terminator (about a hundred metres some twenty kilometres in). The fin glints, Arakawa sees it from
  L2, they get lower, the fin goes dark, she loses them. "What was real" now states the fact.
- *READ:* 1.7 s through the relay, shown as each READ line appearing 3.4 s after its SENT (PIP waits
  there and back), as E01's drone hold does. No "same as always"; the reader is not named.
- *Vacuum:* the bladders roar in silence; the leap, the run and the Earthrise are silent outside.
- *Why they wait for sunset* is shown, not said: Arakawa's word (3), the engine needs two hours (11), and
  her two readouts drifting apart (11) explain why she arrives two hours late.

**Romance through work (Jax and Mira).** The tape in the montage (6); the reputation, "With the right word
in it", the gloves touching (8); "Why? / It's the one I've got" in the vote (9); "Keep him counting" (10);
"She's right. / I know." at the goodbye (15); Jax relaying Mira's numbers in the run ("Twenty metres." /
"I know. I'm telling you anyway.", 16); "Thanks for the shadow. / I didn't do it for you. / No? / Not
only." (18). Longest gap without a Jax–Mira exchange: 12:35–19:40 is covered only by the robe (10) and
"She's right" (15) — see open question 6.
