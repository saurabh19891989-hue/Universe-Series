# UNIVERSE UNDELIVERED
## Episode 1 — "The Parcel Nobody Ordered"
### Shooting script, draft 4 — the animation format (22–26 min, two halves). Awaiting independent critic and showrunner read.

*(Working title in older files: Secret Universe. Draft 3, `script-v3.md`, is untouched.)*

**Format (showrunner rulings, 28 Nov).** The cast are 2D animated characters who lip-sync, walk,
gesture and do takes, composited over real NASA video. The old no-lip-sync staging (backs of heads,
insert-only lines, hidden mouths, soft focus) is gone: leads act their big lines on their faces. Suits
have clear helmets. Inserts are kept only where the object is the point (the fading signature, the
READ receipt, the board, the labels, the report numbers).

**Size.** **3,066 spoken words** by script (dialogue lines only, including "What was real"; cues,
performance notes and action excluded — the same counter gives draft 3 its 4,443), **335 speaker cues**.
**22 units:** cold open, 20 scenes, tag; plus "What was real". Scenes are renumbered for draft 4; the map
from draft 3's numbers is in `revision-notes-v4.md`.

**Runtime arithmetic** (the Episode 2 model, as ruled): speech at 184 words a minute, plus 0.97 s per
speaker cue, plus 1.5 s for each stand-alone INSERT, CUTAWAY, TAKE, HOLD or BEAT line, plus the
picture-only time listed in each scene header (held wides, the drawn tales that play without words,
the march, the throw). Cutaways marked "(UNDER)" play under dialogue and add nothing.

| Part | Seconds |
|---|---|
| Speech: 3,066 words ÷ 184 × 60 | 999.8 |
| Cue gaps: 335 × 0.97 | 324.9 |
| Inserts, cutaways, takes, holds, beats: 60 × 1.5 | 90.0 |
| Picture-only, listed per scene | 76.0 |
| Main title | 10.0 |
| **Total** | **1,500.7 = 25:01** |

Scene targets below are the model rounded to 5 s (they add up to 24:55 with the title). The window is
22:30–25:30. If the acting voices play slower than 184 wpm (each 10 wpm slower adds about 55 s), the
first cuts are listed in `revision-notes-v4.md` (about 60 s, no plant touched).

**Shape.** PART ONE 12:10 — cold open 1:26 · title 0:10 · Act 1 (1–5) 5:19 · Act 2 (6–10) 5:15, ending on
the midpoint. PART TWO 12:51 — Act 3 (11–14) 5:09 · Act 4 (15–20) 6:26 · tag 0:46 · "What was real" 0:30.
Longest scenes: 4 (1:52) and 13 (1:50); nothing over 2:00. Aboard *The Unscheduled*: about 3:45 (15%).
Places: the bridge in orbit, the hold, the real south pole from above, the customs hall (desk, cage, long
window, office, console), the rim road, the inn (parlour, cold store), the outer rim under Earth, the inner
wall in the dark, the apron.

**SHORT test (15).** A stranger understands it in 3 seconds (a little car chased by police cars on the
Moon, in silence); one escalation (PULL OVER → PLEASE → the jump → the speech with the radio off) and a
button ("It is still asking nicely"); no name needed; the cars run side-on through the centre third, so
it crops to 9:16. Back-up short: 2 (the address), which also stands alone.

**Midpoint (end of Part One, at 12:10).** Chichikov's offer ends on "We supply her water." He
vanishes; his contract is on the table; the Baron, who wants it, turns it round, picks up the pen and holds
it out to Jax, handle first; everyone looks at Jax, who does not take it yet. Sayable question: **"Will Jax sell the Baron?"** Tag question: **"Why is Jax
nobody?"**

**Opening rule.** Motion and a problem in the first frame (the bank's drone carrying the engine past the
window, 0:00–0:05; "The rest goes at six" by 0:05). First laugh about 0:10 ("The one going past the
window", with the engine drifting across Jax's face), then "It was financed separately" at about 0:25.
The air is named as the cost by about 0:35 (Temi). The goal (the parcel, by six, for exactly the debt) by
1:10. Title at about 1:26, after the laughs. The spectacle place (the address going out over the real south
pole) at 2:10. The guest is on screen at 3:03 (the tiny figure on the cargo scale behind Mira, from the
first frame of 3) and met at 4:05. The clock is six o'clock.

**Re-hook cadence** (something new every 2–3 minutes; no two static talk scenes in a row): see the
running clock in `revision-notes-v4.md`. The old Act 3 run of three talk scenes is now a moving rover
(11), a side-on walk (12), and the rim with a drawn tale (13).

**Standing rules for picture and performance**
- **Reaction faces (engine 8), one per regular, always the same pose, held 1.5 s on a clean background:**
  **JAX — THE SALESMAN'S FREEZE** (the grin stays on; only the eyes move) · **MIRA — THE LOUPE DROP**
  (she lets the loupe fall on its chain and looks with her own eye) · **LUCÍA — THE ARM-FOLD** (slow, both
  arms, chin down) · **PIP — THE DOT** (the amber lens shrinks to a pinpoint) · **BARON — THE DROOP** (one
  side of the moustache sags, the other stays proud). Each is written below as a TAKE line.
- **The Baron's tales are DRAWN** (engine 26) as short cutaways in our own engraving-like style: black
  cross-hatched line on warm cream, taken in spirit from the public-domain 1862 Doré plates, never traced
  from a plate and never from any film. Flat, few moving parts, reusable in E10.
- **Thinning rule.** The Baron's coat and moustache, and the colour washes in his cutaways, lose about 10%
  saturation per act: Act 1 (scenes 3–5) full; Act 2 (6–10) 90%; Act 3 (11–14) 80%; Act 4 (15–21) 70%.
  Colour returns only in 14, only as colour, and drains again. Never make him younger.
- **PIP's one tale** (the eels, 5) is drawn in a second style: a Victorian ledger print, sepia ink on ruled
  paper, copperplate captions.
- **Chichikov is the only character drawn unfinished:** a fainter, broken line, colour washed toward grey,
  **no shadow**, and whenever anyone looks straight at him the framing drifts off him by itself, as if the
  camera lost interest. He is perfectly lip-synced and pleasant. In the tag his line and colour snap in at
  full strength for the first time, and he casts a shadow. **That is a one-shot:** from Episode 2 he is
  drawn by the faint rule again.
- **PIP** is a brass-and-cream desk machine bolted to the bridge (the locked prop): the amber lens brightens
  when he speaks and swivels to look; the rubber-stamp arm is his punctuation. **He never leaves the ship in
  this episode.** Off the ship he talks the way draft 3 had it: as a call from the ship on Jax's ordinary
  wrist comm (a plain strap screen, the caller line reading THE UNSCHEDULED), his voice filtered like radio.
  The comm has **no amber lens and no PIP icon**: the brass wrist relay is new in Episode 2. His reaction face
  (THE DOT) is therefore played on the bridge (20). **Every report gets an INSERT of its number:** #1,204 (0), #1,205 (2), #1,206 (10),
  #1,207 (20).
- **Noakes's board** (RIM PORT CUSTOMS — DAYS WITHOUT INCIDENT) reads **1,094** in every shot until the tag.
  The clamp console stands under it, so it is in shot for free.
- **Exteriors:** clear-domed helmets, faces lit inside; suit radio carries the dialogue; no sound outside
  except what comes through a suit (score, breath, radio). One-sixth gravity in every jump, step and fall:
  hang a beat longer than feels right.
- **The LOT 14 trunk** rides on the post-chaise's roof rack in every wide from 6 on.
- **Bright by default.** Darkness is a story event (the inner wall, 17). Most of the episode is in hard white
  lunar sun, the warm inn, and the cream-and-brass hall.

**Voice direction**
- Parentheses under a cue are **performance notes** for the acting voices: emotion, volume, pace, laughs,
  breaths, overlaps, beats. They are directions to play, not descriptions. "(OVERLAPPING)" means the line
  starts under the previous one. "(beat)" inside a speech is a half-second stop.
- Jax: quick, warm, selling; he speeds up when cornered and goes very plain when it's true. Mira: dry, exact,
  low, never louder than she needs. Lucía: loud, blunt, warm; laughs from the chest. PIP: precise, British,
  slightly band-limited, no emotion except when there is. The Baron: big, rolling, theatrical; when his
  stories fail he goes small and plain. Chichikov: unhurried, courteous, soft, no comic accent. Noakes: quick,
  anxious, regional English. Temi: bright call-centre warmth. Mamani: kind, tired, radio.
- Radio colours: cab dry; suit radio narrow; open customs channel wider with hiss.
- Say it: Oczakow "OCH-a-kov" · post-chaise "post-SHAYZ" · Hieronymus "heer-ON-ih-mus" · Chichikov
  "CHEE-chi-kof" (Jax's "Chich… Chicha…" is a hand-built read) · N-O-A-K-E-S spelled out · 4471-C
  "four-four-seven-one-C" · "H. K. F." as letters.

**Speaking roles (9):** JAX VALE (captain, narrator) · DR MIRA SEN (customs officer, struck-off
cartographer) · PIP (ship's computer) · LUCÍA "LUCHA" ORTEGA (engineer) · BARON MUNCHAUSEN (guest) · PAVEL
CHICHIKOV (buyer for Voss) · SUPERINTENDENT NOAKES · TEMI ADEYEMI (bank, Lagos) · OFFICER INTI MAMANI (Rim
Port control). Looks as locked in `canon/CHARACTER-LOCKS.md`, redrawn for the cartoon rig.

**How to read the scene headers.** Number · place · thread (A = the job and the Baron · B = Mira, Lucía and
Jax · C = Chichikov, Voss, Jax's past) · target seconds (by the model) · engines (playbook numbers) ·
animation cost (A cheap / B medium / C expensive) · the real NASA video it wants (a kind of plate, not an
asset id; every plate must be logged in `assets/` before use) · Picture-only time.

### 0 · THE UNSCHEDULED — BRIDGE, IN LOW LUNAR ORBIT
**Thread:** A/C · **Target:** 90 s · **Engines:** Polite Escalation [1], Physics is the comedian (light-delay) [16], Status flip [5] · **Cost:** B (drone and engine moving in the window plate) · **NASA video:** real Moon surface sliding past from low orbit (LRO or Artemis-mission window-type footage), bright, close · **Picture-only:** 4 s
**STAGING:** First frame is motion and a problem: through the big bridge window, the real Moon slides
past, and a cheerful little bank tow-drone (smiling logo, one waving arm) undoes the last bolt on the
ship's left engine pod. The pod drifts slowly across the glass, turning. Jax flies one-handed with a
sandwich in the other. Temi is animated in a call window, lip-synced, smiling throughout, a second and a
third behind everything. The repossession escalates on screen, one item per line.

```
FADE IN.

INT. THE UNSCHEDULED — BRIDGE — DAY

Through the window, the real Moon, grey and enormous, sliding
past. In front of it, a little bank drone with a painted smile
unscrews the last bolt on our left engine pod. The pod floats
free and drifts slowly across the glass, turning over.

PIP's amber lens swivels to follow it.

                         PIP
                    (calm, like a weather report)
          Captain. The bank has taken the
          left engine. The rest goes at six.
          Including the air.

JAX VALE, burnt-orange jacket, sandwich in one hand, flies with
the other. He doesn't look up.

                         JAX
                    (through a mouthful, unbothered)
          Which one's the left?

                         PIP
          The one going past the window.

The engine drifts past Jax's face. His head turns with it.

TAKE: JAX — THE SALESMAN'S FREEZE. The grin stays. The eyes
follow the engine all the way out of frame.

                         PIP (CONT'D)
                    (one item per breath)
          And the navigation lights.
                    (they blink off)
          And the heating.
                    (frost feathers across the glass)
          And the coffee machine.

INSERT: the coffee machine's little green light goes out with a
sad descending blip.

                         JAX
                    (standing up, genuinely wounded)
          They can't take the coffee machine.

                         PIP
                    (flat)
          It was financed separately.

A chime. A call window pops open: TEMI ADEYEMI, headset,
dazzling smile, a bank logo smiling behind her.

                         TEMI
                    (bright, rehearsed, sunny)
          Good morning! This is Temi in
          Recoveries. Your vessel is being
          recovered. Full recovery at six
          tonight, lunar time.

                         JAX
                    (big sell smile, over her)
          Temi! Hi! I'm about to make a
          payment—

Temi keeps smiling straight through him for a full second. She
can't hear him yet. Then:

                         TEMI
                    (reset to the top, delighted)
          Hello! You're speaking to Temi in
          Recoveries!

                         JAX
                    (side of the mouth, to PIP)
          Why does she keep doing that?

                         PIP
          She is in Lagos. You are arguing with
          the past.

                         JAX
                    (pleased with himself)
          And I'm winning.

A different chime: soft and old, like a bell on a shop door.
PIP's lens swivels to it.

                         PIP
          A job, posted to us directly. One
          parcel, sealed, prepaid. Shackleton

                    (instantly)

                         PIP
          You have not read the—

                         JAX
                    (hands together, faster each time)
          Take it take it take it take it—

                    (dry)

INSERT: PIP's screen. REPORT #1,204 — RE: CAPTAIN. "TAKE IT" (x5).

acceptance takes 1.3 s to reach Earth and the order 1.3 s to come
back). Outside, the drone stops,

coffee.

the corners, thick with labels pasted one over another.

                    (suspicious, for one second)
          Who sends a parcel to a crater?

                         PIP
          Someone who knew precisely how much
          we owed.

Jax looks at the parcel a moment longer than he'd like. Then the
grin comes back.

                         JAX
          Lucky.

                         PIP
          Filed under "arithmetic".

SMASH TO: MAIN TITLE.
```

### 1 · CARGO HOLD
**Thread:** B/A · **Target:** 25 s · **Engines:** Object that carries it [11] · **Cost:** A · **NASA video:** none (interior; the Moon in the porthole is the same orbit plate as 0)

```
INT. THE UNSCHEDULED — CARGO HOLD — DAY

A hand comes out of the reactor housing, palm up. A red ribbon
is tied round the wrist.

                         LUCÍA (O.S.)
                    (muffled, from inside the machine)
          Eleven.

Jax slaps a spanner into it. The hand goes in, comes straight
back out, and taps the number stamped on the spanner. Twice.

                         LUCÍA (O.S.) (CONT'D)
          That's a nine.

                         JAX
                    (offended on its behalf)
          It's a nine that believes in itself.

LUCÍA ORTEGA shoots out on her creeper and stops at his boots,
looking up. Grease to the elbows.

                         LUCÍA
                    (flat, not moving)
          Eight months, Jax. Eight months unpaid
          is called volunteering.

                         JAX
                    (hand on heart)
          Today. Off the top. Before fuel,
          before food, before me.

Getting up, she puts a hand on the parcel to steady herself, and
stops. Her face changes.

                         LUCÍA
                    (soft, surprised)
          It's warm.

                         PIP (V.O.)
          Twenty-one degrees. According to its
          stamps, it has been twenty-one degrees
          for twenty years.

She takes her hand away. Puts it back.

                         LUCÍA
                    (half a laugh)
          Something in here is alive. Or very
          well wrapped.
```

### 3 · RIM PORT — CUSTOMS HALL — THE DESK
**Thread:** B/C · **Target:** 60 s · **Engines:** Status flip two-hander [5], Repetition plant [25] ("section twelve"), Intrusion into dignity [7] (the Baron, far off) · **Cost:** A–B · **NASA video:** the hall's huge window looks into Shackleton: permanently-shadowed-region visualisation (LRO/ShadowCam-type), black floor, white rim

empty benches, the board on the far wall. Mira sits high behind her counter, Jax low in front of it (the

### 4 · CUSTOMS HALL — HOLDING BAY
