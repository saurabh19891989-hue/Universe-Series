# SECRET UNIVERSE
## Episode 1 — "The Parcel Nobody Ordered"
### Shooting script, draft 3 (critic fixes and showrunner rulings on the punch-up draft 2)

Running time: about 35:55 honest (scene targets add up to 35:45 including "What was real" at 0:40, plus the 0:10 title; model: dialogue at 155 wpm, 0.4 s per speaker change, plus each scene's stated silences, holds and insert-only action). 4,443 spoken words (counted by script: dialogue lines only, including "What was real"; cues, parentheticals and action excluded). 31 units (cold open, 29 scenes with 18 and 19 merged, tag), plus "What was real". Scene numbers are kept from draft 2 so that the outline's plant references still work.
Visual style: photoreal characters and sets composited with real NASA footage, staged mostly
as cinematic stills with camera moves and limited motion. Lip movement is scarce or absent.

**How to read the headers.** Each unit lists its number, setting, thread (A = the job and the
Baron · B = Mira and Lucía · C = Chichikov, Voss, Jax's past), honest target seconds, visual class
(REAL FOOTAGE WIDE / PHOTOREAL SET STILL / PHOTOREAL CLOSE-UP / INSERT / MONTAGE /
DATA VISUAL), and a STAGING note saying how it plays without lip-sync.

**Standing staging rules (they apply everywhere unless a scene says otherwise)**
- **The Baron's moustache covers his mouth completely.** He can talk all day on a still face.
  Play him on eyes, eyebrows and hat.
- **The Baron thins on screen.** His coat and moustache lose about 10% saturation per act
  (Act 1 full, Act 4 at about 60%). The colour returns only in 22, and only as colour: never
  make him look younger.
- **Chichikov is never in sharp focus** until the tag. He sits in soft focus behind others,
  or appears as a reflection, or as his hands on his little inlaid box. That serves the
  forgettable-man gag and needs no lip-sync.
- **PIP speaks with his amber lens**, which brightens when he talks. The rubber-stamp arm is
  his punctuation. **Every report he files gets an INSERT of its number on his screen**
  (#1,204 in the cold open, #1,205 in 2, #1,206 in 16, #1,207 in 30).
- **Lucía is very often half inside a machine**, behind a welding visor, or seen from behind
  driving, with the lucky ribbon on her wrist as her signature insert.
- **Mira is often seen through glass or behind the brass loupe.** A reflection still shows a
  mouth, so **reflections are for silent beats only**. Her spoken lines play on her hands, the
  stamp, the back of her head, or the listener.
- **Noakes's board.** On the customs-hall wall: RIM PORT CUSTOMS — DAYS WITHOUT INCIDENT, a
  flip-number board. It reads 1,094 in every shot until the tag. The clamp console (25, 28)
  stands under it in the main hall, so the board is in shot for free.
- **Exteriors are always in helmets**, with dialogue on suit radio over real footage.
- **When someone important speaks, hold on the listener's reaction.** That is the default cut.

**Voice and TTS notes**
- PIP has a processed, slightly band-limited timbre. Noakes is older, with a regional English
  register. Chichikov is unhurried, with precise, slightly old-fashioned English and **no comic
  accent**. Noakes is anxious and quick.
- Radio colours: the cab is dry; suit radio is narrow; the console and open channel are wider
  with hiss. Mira on the radio is always filtered; Lucía in the cab is always dry.
- Say it: Oczakow "OCH-a-kov" · post-chaise "post-SHAYZ" · Hieronymus "heer-ON-ih-mus" ·
  Chichikov "CHEE-chi-kof" (Jax's "Chich… Chicha…" is a hand-built read) · N-O-A-K-E-S spelled
  out · 4471-C read "four-four-seven-one-C" · "H. K. F." as letters.

### 0 · BRIDGE OF *THE UNSCHEDULED*
**Thread:** A/C · **Target:** 85 s · **Visual:** PHOTOREAL SET STILL / PHOTOREAL CLOSE-UP / INSERT / REAL FOOTAGE WIDE (window)

### 3 · RIM PORT — CUSTOMS HALL — THE DESK
**Thread:** B/C · **Target:** 75 s · **Visual:** PHOTOREAL SET STILL (the vast empty hall) / PHOTOREAL CLOSE-UP / INSERT

(ink-stained, stamping), on the back of her head over which we see Jax, and on Jax listening.
Her reflection in the glass is used once, silently, for the look that lasts one beat too
long. The licence record is an INSERT. The Baron is a tiny distant figure on a cargo scale
far down the hall, and his one line is shouted from a long way off.

### 6 · CUSTOMS HALL — HOLDING BAY
**Thread:** A/C · **Target:** 140 s · **Visual:** PHOTOREAL SET STILL / PHOTOREAL CLOSE-UP / INSERT
**STAGING:** The Baron's moustache does the work: he can deliver every line on a still
portrait, playing on eyebrows and the tilt of the tricorn. Mira is in profile or behind the

the trunk he sits on, and its LOT 14 tag: it rides on his car's roof from 8 onward.

```
INT. CUSTOMS HALL — HOLDING BAY — DAY

A wire-mesh cage at the far end of the hall. Inside, everything

the leather.

                         JAX
          Can I point?

          At last. You may approach.

          The swamp guy.

INSERT: he taps it. Nothing.
```
