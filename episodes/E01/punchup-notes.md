## Five jokes cut, and why
1. "It's the eleven of nines." It's a pun on nothing, and the replacement sells the way Jax does.
2. "Quiet suits you." / "Nothing suits me, sir. I just wear it." It muddles Mira's voice (she deflates; she doesn't do pathos about herself), and the plaque does Noakes better.
3. Noakes's weather story in 25 ("it was the same weather"). It was soft and didn't use his want; the saved-hours run does.
4. "First time for everything." A stock phrase, the kind of joke any show could use.
5. "Nobody. Unbelievable." (tag). It was funny, but it spent the word just before the screen needed it.
(Also cut: "Madre de Dios" as a reflex exclamation, "I'll fit right in", "Mm.", "Great.")

## Lines I was unsure of on taste
- **"I'm not mad."** (Noakes, 25): British for "I'm not crazy". It's mild, but flag it if the series wants no mental-health shorthand. Alternative: "I'm not a lunatic" is worse; "I want a pension" is safe.
- **"prisoner of the Sultan"** (22): Raspe has the Baron enslaved, keeping the Sultan's bees. I softened it so a laugh line doesn't carry the word "slave". Revert if fidelity matters more.
- **The crawler nose-down in the gully** (24): staged so the officer is clearly fine (he is still holding up his card), so the joke is no one being hurt. Keep it that way in the edit.
- **"That's my dad at Christmas"** and **"a grandad with paperwork"**: they are affectionate and stay. Flagged only because they are the episode's family jokes about a Mexican family; they are about appetite and bluntness, not about culture.
