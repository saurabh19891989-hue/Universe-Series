# SECRET UNIVERSE
## Episode 1 — "The Parcel Nobody Ordered"
### Shooting script, draft 1 (from the approved outline v1 and the showrunner's ruling)

Running time: about 35:40 (scene targets add up to 35:00, plus the 0:10 title and the 0:30 "What was real"). 4,818 spoken words. 32 units (cold open, 30 scenes, tag), plus "What was real".
Visual style: photoreal characters and sets composited with real NASA footage, staged mostly
as cinematic stills with camera moves and limited motion. Lip movement is scarce or absent.

**How to read the headers.** Each unit lists its number, setting, thread (A = the job and the
Baron · B = Mira and Lucía · C = Chichikov, Voss, Jax's past), target seconds, visual class
(REAL FOOTAGE WIDE / PHOTOREAL SET STILL / PHOTOREAL CLOSE-UP / INSERT / MONTAGE /
DATA VISUAL), and a STAGING note saying how it plays without lip-sync.

**Standing staging rules (they apply everywhere unless a scene says otherwise)**
- **The Baron's moustache covers his mouth completely.** He can talk all day on a still face.
  Play him on eyes, eyebrows and hat.
- **Chichikov is never in sharp focus** until the tag. He sits in soft focus behind others,
  or appears as a reflection, or as his hands on his little inlaid box. That serves the
  forgettable-man gag and needs no lip-sync.
- **PIP speaks with his amber lens**, which brightens when he talks. The rubber-stamp arm is
  his punctuation.
- **Lucía is very often half inside a machine**, behind a welding visor, or seen from behind
  driving, with the lucky ribbon on her wrist as her signature insert.
- **Mira is often seen through glass**, as a reflection in her customs screen, or with the
  brass loupe to her eye (a close-up of a magnified eye, no mouth needed).
- **Exteriors are always in helmets**, with dialogue on suit radio over real footage.
- **When someone important speaks, hold on the listener's reaction.** That is the default cut.

**Speaking roles (9)**
| Role | Note |
|---|---|
| JAX VALE | Captain, narrator. Late 30s. Mediterranean looks: olive skin, dark curls with early grey, a sailor's squint, burnt-orange courier jacket. American voice. Sells. |
| DR MIRA SEN | Customs officer, struck-off cartographer. Bengali. Dark braid, ink-stained fingers, brass loupe on a chain. Dry and exact. Deflates. |
| PIP | Ship's computer. A brass-and-cream postal-audit unit, half postal scale and half desk lamp. British, literal. |
| LUCÍA "LUCHA" ORTEGA | Engineer. Belt-born, Mexican family, former low-gravity wrestler. Broad shoulders, a ribbon from her old mask on her wrist. Blunt. |
| BARON MUNCHAUSEN | In his seventies. Enormous white moustache, tricorn, moth-eaten cavalry coat over a patched pressure suit. Magnificent and wounded. |
| PAVEL CHICHIKOV | Buyer for Voss. Plump, polite, grey, neither handsome nor plain. Courteous. |
| SUPERINTENDENT NOAKES | Head of customs at Rim Port. Wants nothing to happen. |
| TEMI ADEYEMI | "Recoveries", the bank's agent, speaking from Lagos on Earth. Bright, professional, 1.3 seconds behind. Voice only. |
| OFFICER INTI MAMANI | Rim Port control. Andean, kind, tired. Voice only. |

Jax V.O. lines: six in total (scenes 8, 9, 22, and three in 30), each skipping time or
lying against the picture.

# COLD OPEN

### 0 · BRIDGE OF *THE UNSCHEDULED*
**Thread:** A/C · **Target:** 90 s · **Visual:** PHOTOREAL SET STILL / PHOTOREAL CLOSE-UP / INSERT / REAL FOOTAGE WIDE (window)
**STAGING:** Open on PIP's lens in black, so the first line is a light, not a mouth. Jax is
mostly a silhouette or profile against the real Moon in the window. The bank is voice only.
Lucía is off-screen. The repossession plays as INSERTS: screens dying one by one, the coffee
machine's light going out.

```
BLACK. Silence. One amber light blinks on — PIP's lens.

                         PIP
          Captain. The bank has taken the
          left engine.

INT. THE UNSCHEDULED — BRIDGE — CONTINUOUS

A cramped courier cockpit: tape, cable ties, a dashboard of
mismatched screens. Through the window, filling it, the real
Moon — grey, close, enormous.

JAX VALE (late 30s, olive skin, dark curls going grey early,
burnt-orange jacket with too many pockets) flies with one
hand. The other holds a sandwich.

                         JAX
          Which one's the left?

                         PIP
          The one that has stopped.

INSERT: a row of engine readouts drops to zero.

                         PIP (CONT'D)
          And the navigation lights. And the
          cabin heating. And the coffee
          machine.

INSERT: the coffee machine's little green light goes out.

                         JAX
          They can't take the coffee machine.

                         PIP
          It was financed separately.

A cheerful chime. A generic bank logo smiles from a screen.

                         TEMI (V.O.)
          Good morning! This is Temi in
          Recoveries. Your vessel is being
          recovered. Please remain seated
          while—

                         JAX
          Temi! Hi. Hello. You sound like a
          person. I'm about to make a payment—

Nothing. A full second of nothing.

                         TEMI (V.O.)
          —we disable your—

                         JAX
          A big payment, a really—

                         TEMI (V.O.)
          Hello! You're speaking to Temi in
          Recoveries.

                         JAX
                    (to PIP)
          Why does she keep doing that?

                         PIP
          She is in Lagos. Your words take one
          point three seconds to reach her, and
          hers take one point three to come
          back. You are arguing with the past.

                         LUCÍA (O.S.)
                    (from below, muffled)
          Jax! The heater's gone!

                         JAX
          Put a jumper on!

                         LUCÍA (O.S.)
          The heater for the REACTOR!

A different chime: soft and old-fashioned, like a bell on a
shop door. PIP's lens swivels toward it.

                         PIP
          A job has been posted to us.
          Directly. One parcel, sealed,
          prepaid. Delivery to Shackleton
          crater, lunar south pole, by six
          o'clock tonight. The fee is—

He stops.

                         JAX
          The fee is?

                         PIP
          Exactly what we owe. To the cent.

                         JAX
          Take it.

                         PIP
          You have not read the—

                         JAX
          Take it take it take it take it—

INSERT: PIP's rubber-stamp arm comes down. THUNK.

                         PIP
          Accepted. I am also filing a report.
          Once is binding.

One point three seconds. Then the left engine coughs back to
life. The lights. The heating. Not the coffee.

                         TEMI (V.O.)
          Recovery suspended! Payment is due by
          six o'clock tonight, lunar time. After
          that we take the rest of the vessel,
          including the air. Thank you for
          banking with us!

INSERT: the cargo screen. A brown-paper parcel, soft at the
corners, thick with stamps and labels pasted one over another.

                         JAX
          Who sends a parcel to a crater?

                         PIP
          Someone who knew precisely how much
          we owed.

Jax looks at the parcel a moment longer than he'd like.

                         JAX
          Lucky.

                         PIP
          Filing that under "arithmetic".

SMASH TO: MAIN TITLE.
```

# ACT ONE — "Withdrawn"

### 1 · CARGO HOLD

and a hand; her voice comes from the machine. Jax is seen from behind, handing in tools.
When she slides out on the creeper, her line about the parcel plays on her hand flat on the
brown paper (INSERT), not on her face.

```
INT. THE UNSCHEDULED — CARGO HOLD — DAY

The parcel sits strapped in the middle of an empty hold, like
a patient. Beyond it, a pair of work boots sticks out of the
reactor housing. A hand emerges, palm up. A red ribbon is tied
round the wrist.

                         LUCÍA (O.S.)
                    (from inside the machine)
          Eleven.

Jax puts a spanner in the hand. The hand withdraws, and
returns with the spanner.

                         LUCÍA (O.S.) (CONT'D)
          That's a nine.

                         JAX
          It's the eleven of nines.

                         LUCÍA (O.S.)
          Eight months, Jax.

                         JAX
          I know.

                         LUCÍA (O.S.)
          Eight months without pay isn't a job.
          It's volunteering.

                         JAX
          Today. Off the top. Before fuel,
          before food, before me.

                         LUCÍA (O.S.)
          Before you?

                         JAX
          Before me.

                         LUCÍA (O.S.)
          Say it to PIP. So it's on record.

                         PIP (V.O.)
                    (hold speaker)
          Recorded. I have also recorded that
          he said exactly the same thing in
          March.

LUCÍA ORTEGA slides out on a creeper: broad shoulders, grease
to the elbows. Getting up, she puts a hand on the parcel to
steady herself, and stops.

INSERT: her palm on the brown paper.

                         LUCÍA
          It's warm.

                         PIP (V.O.)
          Twenty-one degrees. According to its
          stamps, it has been twenty-one
          degrees for twenty years.

                         LUCÍA
          Something in here is alive. Or very
          well wrapped.

                         JAX
          Don't shake it.

                         LUCÍA
          I wasn't going to shake it.

                         JAX
          You had a shaking face.

                         LUCÍA
          This is my face.

                         JAX
          I know. It's very expressive.
```

### 2 · BRIDGE — ON APPROACH

screen, a DATA VISUAL in which the address dims letter by letter. Jax and PIP are a
silhouette and a lens against the window. Officer Mamani is radio only. Lucía is on the
intercom.

```
EXT. LUNAR SOUTH POLE — DAY (REAL FOOTAGE)

Earth.

                         PIP (V.O.)
          Shackleton crater. Destination in
          eleven minutes. We will be four hours
          early. I have never been early.

INT. THE UNSCHEDULED — BRIDGE — CONTINUOUS

                         JAX
          Enjoy it.

DATA VISUAL: the Atlas display. A glowing label on the black
crater floor: THE SILVER HATCHET — SHACKLETON FLOOR.

The letters begin to dim, one at a time, from the end.

                         PIP
          Captain.

                         JAX
          Mm.

                         PIP
          The destination is no longer
          anywhere.

                         JAX
          It's a crater, PIP. Craters don't
          leave.

                         PIP

DATA VISUAL: the label is gone. Then the little street names

                         JAX
          Did we do that?

                         JAX
          Officer Mamani, hi, Jax. Small thing.
          My client's expecting—

                         MAMANI (V.O.)
          Your client is no longer anywhere,

                         PIP
          I am filing a report on the Atlas.
          Withdrawn without notice to the
          carrier.

                         JAX
          Who reads those?

                         PIP
          That is not the point of them.

                         LUCÍA (V.O.)
                    (intercom)
          Jax. Why are we landing at customs?

                         JAX
          We're being thorough.

                         LUCÍA (V.O.)
          We've never been thorough.

                         JAX
          It's a new direction.

                         LUCÍA (V.O.)
```

### 3 · RIM PORT — CUSTOMS HALL — THE DESK
**Thread:** B/C · **Target:** 85 s · **Visual:** PHOTOREAL SET STILL (the vast empty hall) / PHOTOREAL CLOSE-UP / INSERT
**STAGING:** Mira sits behind a glass customs screen. We play her as a reflection in the
glass with Jax's face beyond it, and over Jax's shoulder. Her hands (ink-stained, stamping)
carry the action. The licence record is an INSERT. The Baron is a tiny distant figure on a
cargo scale far down the hall, and his one line is shouted from a long way off.

```
INT. RIM PORT — CUSTOMS HALL — DAY

A hall built for two thousand people and staffed for three.
Rows of empty benches. One desk lit. Behind its glass screen,
DR MIRA SEN — dark braid, ink-stained fingers, a brass loupe on
a chain round her neck.

                         MIRA
          Next.

Jax is the only person in the hall.

                         JAX
          There's no one else here.

                         MIRA
          It's procedure. Next.

He steps up.

                         MIRA (CONT'D)
          Parcel, one. Sealed. Addressed to a
          district withdrawn at fourteen-oh-two.
          Seized at fourteen-oh-nine. You were
          very quick.

                         JAX
          Thank you.

                         MIRA
          It wasn't a compliment. It was a
          time.

                         JAX
          Doctor Sen. Doctor of what?

                         MIRA
          Cartography.

                         JAX
          A mapmaker. Working customs.

                         MIRA
          A cartographer working customs. A
          mapmaker draws. I measure.

                         JAX
          Here's my situation. My aunt—

                         MIRA
          You don't have an aunt.

                         JAX
          You don't know that.

                         MIRA
          Your form says next of kin: none.

                         JAX
          She's very distant. Look — I can tell
          you're someone who appreciates
          flexibility.

                         MIRA
          I'm a cartographer. I appreciate where
          things actually are.

                         JAX
          Then you'll appreciate the Lunar
          Courier Exemption. Section twelve.

                         MIRA
          There are nine sections.

                         JAX
          Which is why twelve is so exclusive.

                         MIRA
          Is there anything true you'd like to
          tell me, Captain? Before I stamp it.

                         JAX
          I'm very good at my job.

                         MIRA
          You're four hours from losing your
          ship.

                         JAX
          I'm good at it in bursts.

She runs his licence.

INSERT: her screen. VALE, JAX. MASTER, COURIER CLASS. The record
begins seven years ago. Above that line, the fields are empty.
Not redacted. Empty.

                         MIRA
          Your record starts seven years ago.

                         JAX
          Late bloomer.

                         MIRA
          Your record isn't late, Captain. It's
          blank.

In the glass, her reflection looks at him one beat longer than
it needs to.

                         JAX
          Is that a problem?

                         MIRA
          Not for customs.

INSERT: her stamp. THUNK. SEIZED.

                         JAX
          Can I at least see it?

                         MIRA
          Holding bay. Lot fourteen. You can
          look.

Far down the hall, a tiny figure in a tricorn hat stands on a
cargo scale while a customs drone reads his weight.

                         BARON (O.S.)
                    (distant, outraged)
          I used to be taller!
```

### 4 · CUSTOMS HALL — MIRA'S DESK
**Thread:** B · **Target:** 50 s · **Visual:** INSERT / PHOTOREAL CLOSE-UP
**STAGING:** Almost all hands and paper: the deletion record, the notebook, page after page
of entries with the same empty field. Noakes is first a voice behind her, then a reflection in
her dark monitor, never a speaking face. Mira is lit from below by the desk lamp, and her
lines are short enough to land on the reflection.

```
INT. CUSTOMS HALL — MIRA'S DESK — CONTINUOUS

INSERT: the deletion record. THE SILVER HATCHET. STATUS:
WITHDRAWN AT THE REQUEST OF THE OWNER. OWNER: —

                         MIRA
                    (to herself)
          Which owner?

She takes a battered paper notebook from her drawer and opens
it.

INSERT: page after page of addresses in her small exact hand.
Every entry ends the same way. OWNER: —. OWNER: —. OWNER: —.

She writes: 41. THE SILVER HATCHET. OWNER: —

                         NOAKES (O.S.)
          Dr Sen.

In her dark monitor: SUPERINTENDENT NOAKES, behind her. She does
not close the notebook fast enough.

                         NOAKES (CONT'D)
          We discussed the notebook.

                         MIRA
          We discussed it being on my desk.
          It's in my hand.

                         NOAKES
          How many pages now?

                         MIRA
          Forty-one entries. All withdrawn at
          the owner's request. Not one of them
          has an owner.

                         NOAKES
          You were struck off for saying the
          map was wrong. Please don't get
          struck off again for saying it
          louder.

                         MIRA
          It is wrong.

                         NOAKES
          It's official. Those aren't opposites,
          Dr Sen. They're departments.

A pause.

                         NOAKES (CONT'D)
          One more complaint and your probation
          ends. And then you have nowhere to
          stand.

                         MIRA
          I'll be quiet.

                         NOAKES
          Thank you. Truly. Quiet suits you.

                         MIRA
          Nothing suits me, sir. I just wear it.

His reflection leaves. She looks at the page. She doesn't close
it.
```

### 5 · CUSTOMS HALL — A BENCH

at the soft-focus end of the bench in a two-shot. We hold on Jax's face while Chichikov
speaks. INSERTS: the inlaid box on Chichikov's knees, his teacup, the card. The empty bench is
the punchline, and it costs nothing.

```

          Options. One: we bribe her.

                         PIP (V.O.)

          Two: we charm her.

          People warm up.

in grey, neither handsome nor plain, neither fat nor thin. He
has a small inlaid travelling box on his knees and a teacup in
his hand. He seems to have been there for a while.

This is PAVEL CHICHIKOV.

                         CHICHIKOV
          Please. Don't stop on my account.

                         JAX
          How long have you been there?

                         CHICHIKOV
          Long enough to be sorry about your

          I buy what the register has
```

### 6 · CUSTOMS HALL — HOLDING BAY
**Thread:** A/C · **Target:** 120 s · **Visual:** PHOTOREAL SET STILL / PHOTOREAL CLOSE-UP / INSERT
**STAGING:** The Baron's moustache does the work: he can deliver every line on a still
portrait, playing on eyebrows and the tilt of the tricorn. Mira is in profile or behind the
loupe. Jax is over the shoulder. INSERTS: the layered labels, two fingers on the padlock, the
stopped clock, Mira's wrist message. The failed lock is pure stillness, which is ideal.

```
INT. CUSTOMS HALL — HOLDING BAY — DAY

A wire-mesh cage at the far end of the hall. Inside, everything
from the deleted district, each piece with a brown luggage tag:
a grandfather clock that has stopped, a stuffed bear in a
hussar's jacket, half a saddle with a green shoot growing out of
the leather.

And on a trunk, with a tag on his top button: THE BARON. An
enormous white moustache. A tricorn. A moth-eaten cavalry coat
over a patched pressure suit. On his knee, with both hands
resting on it like a cat: Jax's parcel.

MIRA lets herself and JAX in, and locks the gate behind them.

                         MIRA
          Lot fourteen. You can look. You can't
          touch.

                         JAX
          Can I point?

                         MIRA
          Sparingly.

The Baron rises. It takes a while. He makes it look deliberate.

                         BARON
          At last. You may approach.

                         JAX
          Sorry — we're just here for the box.

                         BARON
          Everyone is just here for the box.
          Then they meet me.
                    (a bow, magnificent)
          Hieronymus Karl Friedrich, Baron von
          Munchausen. Hussar. Ambassador. Twice
          to the Moon — once on purpose. You
          have heard of me.

                         JAX
          ...The swamp guy?

A pause. The Baron takes it in the chest, like a soldier.

                         BARON
          The swamp guy.

                         JAX
          Pulled yourself out by your own hair.

                         BARON
          By my pigtail. And my horse with me,
          gripped between my knees. Earth always
          leaves out the horse. Earth has no
          respect for horses.

                         MIRA
                    (reading his tag)
          The registry has you as "Residue.
          Delisted. Formerly of the Silver
          Hatchet, Shackleton Floor."

                         BARON
          Residue. Madam, I have been called a
          liar by an empress, a scoundrel by a
          sultan, and delicious by a fish. I
          have never been called residue.

                         MIRA
          It's not personal. It's a category.

                         BARON
          All the worst things are.

                         JAX
                    (nodding at the parcel)
          That's mine, by the way.

                         BARON
          It is mine. Look.

INSERT: he tilts it to the light. Labels on labels — twenty years
of handwriting. On top, in looping brown ink: c/o the Baron von
Munchausen, The Silver Hatchet.

                         BARON (CONT'D)
          It comes to me. I send it on. It
          comes back. Twenty years.

                         JAX
          Who's it for?

Something private crosses the Baron's eyes, and goes.

                         BARON
          Not me. It never was. I am only the
          last door anyone could find.

Mira's eye, huge behind the brass loupe, counting edges.

                         MIRA
          There are thirty-one labels on this.

                         BARON
          Thirty-two. One fell off in the
          spring. I was inconsolable.

                         JAX
          Great. So you sign for it, I get paid,
          everyone goes home—

                         MIRA
          He can't sign for seized goods. No one
          can.

                         BARON
          Then let us un-seize ourselves.
                    (turning to the gate, grand)
          This lock was made in Riga by a
          locksmith who owed me his life. I
          pulled him from the river by his ears.
          Whenever I speak to one of his locks,
          it remembers him — and it opens.

INSERT: two old fingers laid on the padlock. Waiting.

Nothing.

INSERT: he taps it. Nothing.

INSERT: behind him, the clock does not tick.

Jax and Mira are careful not to look at each other.

                         BARON (CONT'D)
                    (quiet, to the lock)
          You used to.

A beat. Then the moustache lifts again.

                         BARON (CONT'D)
          It is shy. Locks are shy in front of
          ladies.

                         MIRA
          It's a customs lock. I have the key.

                         BARON
          Then it is loyal. I respect that.

INSERT: Mira's wrist chimes. LOT 14 — PUBLIC SALE.

                         MIRA
          Lot fourteen is listed for sale. "One
          parcel, sealed. One gentleman,
          delisted. Eighteen hundred hours."

                         BARON
          I have been sold before. Never as a
          set.

                         JAX
          Six o'clock.

                         MIRA
          Is that a problem?

                         JAX
          It's when my bank takes my ship.

The Baron sits back down on his trunk and settles the parcel on
his knee.

                         BARON
          Then we are both for sale at six. How
          companionable.
```

### 7 · CUSTOMS HALL — NOAKES'S OFFICE
**Thread:** A/B · **Target:** 65 s · **Visual:** PHOTOREAL SET STILL / INSERT
**STAGING:** Jax lays his wrist on Noakes's desk like evidence, and PIP's amber light on the
desk is the speaker. Noakes is a silhouette against his window onto the crater, back mostly
to us. The regulation text scrolls as an INSERT. Mira speaks from the doorway, framed small
and far away, so her lines land on Noakes turning.

```
INT. CUSTOMS HALL — NOAKES'S OFFICE — DAY

A window onto the black crater. NOAKES in silhouette against it.
Jax unstraps his wrist comm and lays it on the desk, lens up.

                         JAX
          Superintendent. My computer would like
          a word.

                         PIP (V.O.)
                    (from the desk)
          Several. Postal Regulation forty-one,
          sub-section c. Eighteen-forty. Never
          repealed.

INSERT: an old regulation scrolls on Noakes's screen, in a
copperplate font.

                         PIP (V.O.) (CONT'D)
          Goods may not be declared abandoned
          until the carrier has attempted
          delivery at the physical address, in
          the presence of a customs witness.

                         NOAKES
          The address doesn't exist.

                         PIP (V.O.)
          The address does not. The place does.
          Regulation forty-one concerns places.
          It was written before addresses were
          fashionable.

                         NOAKES
          Eighteen-forty.

                         PIP (V.O.)
          A very good year for regulations.

                         NOAKES
          Has anyone ever actually invoked it?

                         PIP (V.O.)
          Once. In eighteen forty-one. A man
          walked a parcel of eels to a
          demolished house in Bristol and
          knocked for an hour.

                         NOAKES
          And?

                         PIP (V.O.)
          No one answered. It was a triumph.

                         JAX
          Basically, nothing's undeliverable
          till—

                         PIP (V.O.)
          —until somebody has knocked. Captain,
          please don't paraphrase me. I was
```
