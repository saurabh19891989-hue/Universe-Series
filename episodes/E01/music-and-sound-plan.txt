## 4. Vacuum rule (exteriors)

No effects at all on any exterior shot: no engines, no dust, no impacts, no grapple, no landing, no knocks. Score only, or nothing. Sound on exteriors comes only from inside something: suit radio (narrow), the cab (dry), felt thumps inside the rover (wheels landing, being hooked). Exterior shots: `E01-S02-001`, `E01-S08-001/002/011`, scene 9, `E01-S17-001`, scene 21, `E01-S24-001/002/003/006/007/014`, `E01-S26-001/003/005`, scene 29, the real-footage shots in 30 and What was real.

## 5. Voice treatment reminders (from the script)

Cab dry; suit radio narrow; console and open channel wider with hiss; Mira on radio always filtered; Lucía in the cab always dry; PIP band-limited; Temi on a call line with 1.3 s lag shown as freezes; Noakes's line in 25 read fast with no pauses. Distant Baron in 3 with long hall reverb.

Voices the TTS cannot do well, to be built and checked by ear: Jax's real laugh (21), Lucía's shocked laugh (15), the Baron's huge laugh (22), the Baron's wordless effort breaths (14), "Chich… Chicha…" (hand-built read). Build from Kokoro syllables with processing; send to the owner as part of the listening reel.
