# SECRET UNIVERSE — Episode 1: "The Parcel Nobody Ordered"

**Outline v1, head writer. Waiting on the showrunner's ruling. No script until then.**
Genre: caper / chase. Music: main theme, plus a harpsichord that has gone slightly wrong.
Running time: about 35:30 (cold open 1:30 · title 0:10 · Act 1 8:30 · Act 2 8:50 · Act 3 8:10 · Act 4 7:00 · tag 1:00 · "what was real" 0:30).
Scenes: 30, plus the cold open and the tag. Speaking roles: 8. Settings: 7.

**Source note.** I worked from Raspe's text, *The Surprising Adventures of Baron Munchausen*
(Project Gutenberg #3154, opened 27 Nov through the gutenberg.pglaf.org mirror, because the
main site was down). The hatchet thrown "by an unlucky turn of my arm" that flies to the Moon,
the bean climbed to "one of the moon's horns", the straw rope, the post horn whose "tunes were
frozen up in the horn", the horse on the steeple, the cannonballs meeting in mid-air at
Gibraltar, the King of the Moon, who is thirty-six feet tall and eats once a month — all of
these are Raspe's own. **The cannonball ride and the pigtail-and-swamp story are not in
Raspe's English text.** They come from G. A. Bürger's expanded German edition of 1786. That is
also public domain, but it is a different book (see Question 1). No film was used.

## 1. Logline and why you keep watching

**Logline:** A broke courier lands a parcel at the Moon's south pole just as its address is
deleted from the map. He then has until six o'clock to choose between the fee that saves his
ship and the magnificent, fading old man who swears he is Baron Munchausen. A polite company
man is offering to buy them both.

**Why you keep watching:** The funniest man in the episode is disappearing, and he is the
only one who has noticed. Every choice Jax makes costs something you can count (a ship, a
job, nine months' wages), and by the end the crew owns nothing but each other and a parcel
that refuses to be delivered. The episode's last word is the first clue to who the narrator
really is, and it has been in plain sight since minute four.

## 2. Cast for this episode (8 speaking roles)

| Character | Wants, this episode |
|---|---|
| **Jax Vale**, captain, narrator | To pay off the lien by six and keep his ship, without having to care about anything he carries. |
| **Dr Mira Sen**, customs officer (and a struck-off cartographer) | To keep her last job one more day without lying about the map. Underneath that, for one person to look at what she has found. |
| **PIP**, ship's computer | To get the parcel delivered correctly, with every form in order. It has been trying for twenty years, and he takes that personally. |
| **Lucía "Lucha" Ortega**, engineer | Eight months of wages, today, and to stop being the only adult on board. |
| **Baron Munchausen**, guest | To be heard to the end of one story, and to stay himself while he fades. |
| **Pavel Chichikov**, buyer for Voss | Both signatures by six, the courier's and the Baron's, with no fuss. |
| **Superintendent Noakes**, head of customs at Rim Port | For nothing to happen on his shift. |
| **Recoveries**, the bank (voice only, on Earth) | Payment, with a smile, 1.3 seconds late. |

**Settings (7):** (1) bridge of *The Unscheduled* · (2) cargo hold · (3) Rim Port customs
hall, used for the desk, the holding bay and the back office, all corners of one set ·
(4) inside the "post-chaise", the Baron's old sealed rover · (5) the rim outside: road, dock
apron, the view of Earth · (6) the Silver Hatchet, the Baron's inn, with its parlour and cold
store · (7) the floor of Shackleton crater, which is permanently in shadow.

**The world rule we show without explaining:** a place listed in the Atlas is somewhere. Delete
the listing and the rock stays, but the place is nowhere, and a legend who lives there starts
to *thin*. The Baron's new stories no longer come true, his signature won't stay on paper, and
his colours drain a little more each act. That is cheap to animate: a palette shift and a
moustache that droops a few degrees.

### COLD OPEN (0:00–1:30)
**0. Bridge** · Jax, PIP, Lucía (off-screen), Recoveries · A/C · 90 s
The bank repossesses the ship by remote control while Jax is still flying it, one system at
a time. Arguing with Earth comes with a 1.3-second delay, so Jax keeps interrupting someone
who hasn't been interrupted yet. A job arrives addressed directly to them: a sealed,
prepaid parcel for Shackleton crater, due by six, and the fee is exactly what they owe. He
takes it without reading it, and the systems come back on, except the coffee.
**Turns:** goal and clock are set. Deliver by six or lose the ship, the air included.
**Line:** PIP: "She is on Earth. You are arguing with the past."
**Moon:** light delay to Earth, about 1.3 s each way [gen]. · **Visual:** close-ups on the
illustrated bridge, with the real Moon filling the window.

### ACT ONE: "Withdrawn" (1:40–10:10)

**1. Cargo hold** · Lucía, Jax, PIP (speaker) · B/A · 50 s
Lucía, half inside the reactor housing, gives Jax her figure: eight months unpaid. The parcel
is strapped in the middle of the hold, brown paper soft with age, and it is warm. PIP reports
that, according to its stamps, it has been twenty-one degrees for twenty years. Jax promises
her wages come out of the fee first.
**Turns:** Lucía gets a promise out of him, and we see how much his promises are worth to her.
**Line:** LUCÍA: "Eight months without pay isn't a job, Jax. It's volunteering." / LUCÍA (hand
on the parcel): "Something in here is alive, or very well wrapped."
**Moon:** none. · **Visual:** illustrated interior.

**2. Bridge, on approach** · Jax, PIP, Lucía (comms) · A · 80 s
The south pole comes up in the window: a rim burning white, the crater a pit of total black,
and Earth sitting low on the horizon. On the navigation screen the Atlas shows the
destination, *The Silver Hatchet, Shackleton Floor*, and as they watch the entry dims letter by
letter. Then the street names around it go out one after another. Port control: all goods
for the withdrawn district are held by customs, and the ship is to land and be inspected.
**Turns:** the delivery has become legally impossible, and the clock hasn't moved.
**Line:** JAX: "It's a crater, PIP. Craters don't leave." PIP: "The crater is still there,
Captain. It has merely stopped being anywhere." (PIP files report #1,205 on the Atlas:
"withdrawn without notice to the carrier".)
**Moon:** a sunlit rim above a permanently shadowed floor [file]; Earth low on the horizon
[gen]. · **Visual:** real footage wide (LRO/LOLA flyover plate), cutting to close-ups on the bridge.

**3. Customs hall** · Mira, Jax · B/C · 90 s
The hall was built for two thousand people and is staffed for three. In the background, a
ridiculous old man in a tricorn is being weighed on a cargo scale. Mira processes the seizure
with perfect courtesy and gives nothing. Jax sells: a sick aunt, a regulation he has just
made up, then charm. She corrects each lie with the real number. She runs his licence, and the
record starts seven years ago; before that there is nothing. She looks at him one beat longer
than she needs to.
**Turns:** the parcel is seized, and for the first time Jax's charm has failed on someone. He
is interested.
**Line:** JAX: "You strike me as someone who appreciates flexibility." MIRA: "I'm a cartographer.
I appreciate where things actually are." / MIRA: "Your record starts seven years ago." JAX:
"Late bloomer." MIRA: "Your record isn't late. It's blank."
**Moon:** none. · **Visual:** close-up dialogue, with one wide of the empty hall.

**4. Mira's desk** · Mira, Noakes · B · 50 s
Alone, Mira opens the deletion record: "Withdrawn at the request of the owner." The owner field
is empty. She takes out a battered paper notebook of deletions copied out in her own hand,
forty pages, and every one has the same empty field. Noakes appears at her shoulder: one more
complaint and her probation ends.
**Turns:** we learn Mira has been right for years, and that today is her last chance to keep
quiet about it.
**Line:** NOAKES: "You were struck off for saying the map was wrong. Please don't get struck off
again for saying it louder." MIRA: "It is wrong." NOAKES: "It's official. Those aren't
opposites, Dr Sen. They're departments."
**Moon:** none. · **Visual:** close-up dialogue, plus an insert of the notebook.

**5. Customs hall, bench** · Jax, Chichikov, PIP (wrist) · C · 60 s
Jax is on his wrist to PIP, planning out loud. A plump, polite man in grey is sitting beside
him, neither handsome nor plain, neither fat nor thin, and seems to have been there a while.
Chichikov offers his sympathy. He knows the size of the lien, the ship's name and Lucía's
name, and he buys "anything that has stopped being anywhere". He leaves a card. When Jax looks
up the bench is empty, and he can't remember the man's face.
**Turns:** someone with money knew this parcel would be stranded here before it was.
**Line:** CHICHIKOV: "I buy what the register has forgotten, Captain. You would be amazed how
much of it still works." / JAX (reading the card): "Chich… Chicha…" PIP: "I have logged him
as 'man, average'."
**Moon:** none. · **Visual:** close-up dialogue. Chichikov is only ever in two-shots, never
alone in the frame, until the tag.

**6. Customs holding bay: first meeting with the Baron** · Baron, Jax, Mira · A/C · 110 s
Inside a cage of everything from the deleted district sits the Baron, with a luggage tag on
his top button and Jax's parcel on his knee like a cat. He introduces himself in full. He is
wounded to be known as "the swamp guy", and corrects Earth's version of the story. The parcel's
top label is in his own hand: it comes to him and he forwards it, and it has been coming back
for twenty years. He orders the cage lock to open, the way it always has, and nothing happens.
**Turns:** the parcel and the Baron are one lot, for public sale at six, the same minute the
bank takes the ship.
**Line:** BARON: "I have been called a liar by an empress, a scoundrel by a sultan, and
delicious by a fish. I have never been called residue."
**Moon:** none. · **Visual:** illustrated interior, close-ups. *(Written out in full in section 4.)*

**7. Noakes's office (PIP on Jax's wrist)** · Jax, Mira, Noakes, PIP · A/B · 65 s
PIP, who has read every postal regulation written since 1840, finds one: goods may not be
declared abandoned until the carrier has tried to deliver at the *physical* address, with a
customs witness present. The address is gone, but the place isn't. Noakes cannot refuse a
regulation. Mira volunteers as the witness. Noakes's condition: parcel and gentleman back by
six for the sale.
**Turns (END OF ACT ONE):** the job becomes a road trip into a deleted place, chaperoned by a
woman whose job is to stamp them both "abandoned".
**Line:** PIP: "Regulation 41(c). Nothing is undeliverable until somebody has knocked." /
NOAKES (to Mira): "Why on earth would you volunteer?" MIRA: "Someone should write down what's
there before it's sold."
**Moon:** none. · **Visual:** close-up dialogue.

### ACT TWO: "The Silver Hatchet" (10:10–19:00)

**8. Inside the post-chaise, on the rim road** · Lucía (driving), Jax, Mira, Baron · A/B · 65 s
The Baron's rover has a carved horse's head on the bonnet. The Baron gives a guided tour of a
Moon nobody else can see. Mira deflates every claim; Lucía adores him at once. Outside, the Sun
rolls along the horizon instead of rising, the rim is white and the slope below is ink.
**Turns:** Lucía learns the old man is part of the sale and picks a side out loud. Mira, for the
first time, doesn't correct him; she writes something down.
**Line:** BARON: "The King of the Moon is thirty-six feet tall. He eats once a month: opens his
left side, puts in the whole meal, and closes it again." LUCÍA: "That's my dad at Christmas."
MIRA: "There is no king." BARON: "Not any more."
**Moon:** the rim sits in near-constant sunlight with the Sun circling low, because the Moon's
tilt is tiny [file: the 1.6° tilt; the rim illumination is gen]. · **Visual:** illustrated
interior, with the real terrain plate in the windows.

**9. Rim road, wide** · (Jax V.O.) · A · 25 s
The rover crawls along the bright rim and down to the edge of the shadow. The Silver Hatchet's
one lamp is the only light in a sea of black. Outside there is total silence; only the score.
The inn's painted sign is flaking, letter by letter.
**Turns:** time skips, and Jax, who never listens, admits he listened.
**Line:** JAX (V.O.): "Forty minutes. He talked for all of them. I've never been so sorry when
a drive ended."
**Moon:** no air, so no sound [gen]; the border between light and permanent shadow [file].
· **Visual:** real footage wide (terrain-derived plate) with the illustrated rover.

**10. Silver Hatchet, parlour** · All four, plus the Baron · A · 60 s
The low inn is cut into the crater wall, half in sunlight and half in permanent dark. The
light crosses the room like the hand of a very slow clock. Every surface holds something
impossible. The Baron grandly commands the stove to light, "as it always has", and nothing
happens. Lucía lights it with a spanner and says nothing.
**Turns:** his magic has gone, and he knows they have all seen it.
**Line:** BARON: "The Sun comes round this room once a month. On the fourteenth it reaches the
sofa. I always sit there on the fourteenth. One keeps one's appointments."
**Moon:** the lunar day lasts about a month, and shadows sweep slowly across the ground [file].
· **Visual:** illustrated interior, with the light rendered as a slow wipe.

**11. Parlour: the signature** · Jax, Baron, Mira, PIP (wrist) · A · 55 s
Jax produces the delivery slip. The Baron signs with an enormous flourish. The ink holds for one
second and then fades to nothing. They try again and it fades faster.
**Turns:** the loophole has failed. A man who isn't listed can't receive anything, so the fee
can't be earned.
**Line:** PIP: "The addressee has signed. The signature has declined to remain."
**Moon:** none. · **Visual:** close-up, plus an insert of the paper.

**12. Customs back office** · Chichikov, Noakes · C · 45 s
Chichikov places a reserve bid on Lot 14 with perfect courtesy and opens his little inlaid
travelling box of contracts. Noakes, uneasy, asks what Voss wants with an old liar. Chichikov
is right about one thing: nobody listens to the Baron any more, and Voss will.
**Turns:** the sale is already decided, and the buyer is Voss.
**Line:** CHICHIKOV: "You think we are buying a man, Superintendent. We are buying him an
audience. Who else is offering?" / NOAKES (signing, later): "Mr… Chichester." CHICHIKOV: "Close enough."
**Moon:** none. · **Visual:** close-up dialogue.

**13. MONTAGE: the inventory** · Mira, Baron, Lucía, Jax · A/B · 50 s
This is the episode's one music-led montage, on the harpsichord that has gone wrong. Mira tags
each object, the Baron narrates its legend, and the pictures politely contradict him. There is
half a saddle with a laurel growing out of it, and the straw rope "on which I climbed down from
the Moon", which is three metres long. Last comes the silver hatchet. Jax picks it up idly and
weighs it, and it sits in his hand as if it has been waiting for him. The Baron notices.
**Turns:** Mira's tags stop saying "item" and start using the Baron's names for things.
**Line:** BARON (V.O.): "This hatchet I threw at two bears in the Sultan's garden, and by an
unlucky turn of my arm it flew upward and did not stop until it reached the Moon." MIRA
(writing): "Hatchet. Silver. Dented." BARON: "Moon-dented."
**Moon:** none. · **Visual:** montage (stills and limited animation suit it).

**14. Parlour** · Lucía, Baron, Jax, Mira · A/B · 55 s
Lucía asks bluntly for proof of one story, any story: if he's real, she isn't driving him back
to be sold. He tries the famous one. He takes hold of his own pigtail and heaves, and his boots
don't leave the floor, in one-sixth gravity, where a child could jump to the ceiling. Silence.
He tells them plainly that his stories have stopped coming true, and why.
**Turns:** the comedy breaks open on the sadness. Lucía now feels guilty, and guilt makes her
dangerous.
**Line:** BARON: "In one-sixth gravity. A child could do it. I once did it with a horse." /
BARON: "Do you know what a lie is, Señorita? A story nobody believed in time."
**Moon:** one-sixth gravity, so the failure is crueller [gen]. · **Visual:** close-ups, plus one
held wide of him alone.

**15. The cold store** · Baron, Mira, Jax, Lucía, PIP (wrist) · A/B · 85 s
To make up for it, he takes them into the back room. It opens onto ground that has never seen
the Sun; it is the coldest place any of them has stood, with ice on the walls older than any
language. He lifts out a brass post horn white with frost: long ago a postilion played tunes
into it down here, and they froze inside. He holds it to the stove. It thaws, and from a horn
nobody is blowing comes a bright, slightly out-of-tune eighteenth-century march. Mira checks it
with her loupe and there is no mechanism, and for the first time she has no number.
**Turns:** one impossible thing is true (the season outline's "the Baron proves one impossible
thing"). Mira's certainty cracks, and Lucía is fully on his side.
**Line:** MIRA: "There's nothing in it." BARON: "There was a postilion in it. Long ago. He
played very well, and it was very cold." / PIP (quiet, moved): "That is a post horn. That is…
the *post*." / BARON: "I won it at cards from an English knight. He had a better one."
**Moon:** permanently shadowed ground, among the coldest places in the solar system and likely
to hold ice [file]. · **Visual:** illustrated interior and close-ups. The effect is sound design,
so no animation is needed.

**16. Parlour** · Chichikov, everyone · A/B/C · 85 s
Chichikov is in the corner chair. Nobody knows how long he has been there. For the parcel,
unopened, he offers more than the ship is worth. For the Baron, a Voss contract: relisted, and
his stories true again. Then, to Lucía, he reads out her mother's water-account number on
Ceres, and says he can make it zero. The condition is both signatures, courier's and Baron's,
by six, or neither.
**Turns (END OF ACT TWO):** the villain can cure the Baron, and the crew splits down the middle.
**Line:** CHICHIKOV: "Forgive me, I let myself in. The door is no longer anywhere, so technically
there wasn't one." / CHICHIKOV (gently, to Lucía): "Account 4471-C. Ortega, Carmen. I could make
that number zero before you are home." (PIP files report #1,206: "entry without a door".)
**Moon:** none. · **Visual:** close-up dialogue.

### ACT THREE: "Both or Neither" (19:00–27:10)

**17. Post-chaise, climbing back** · All, plus the Baron · B/C · 70 s
A shouting match in a small sealed box. Lucía says take the money, and every word of her case
is fair. Jax says nothing, which frightens everyone more. Mira reads Chichikov's contract
through her loupe: it was drawn up three days before the Silver Hatchet was deleted.
**Turns:** Mira has proof that Voss bought the Baron before anyone deleted him. The deletion
*was* the purchase.
**Line:** LUCÍA: "He's a lovely old man and I'd die for him. I'd just rather not do it for free."
/ MIRA: "This is dated the twenty-fourth. He was deleted on the twenty-seventh. They bought the
wreck before they sank the ship."
**Moon:** the climb back into blinding sunlight [file: shadow border]. · **Visual:** illustrated interior.

**18. Customs desk** · Mira, Noakes · B · 55 s
Mira puts the contract on Noakes's desk. He reads it, goes pale, and is completely reasonable.
If she files it, the complaint goes to the Atlas office, the Atlas office is three floors from
Voss, and she is gone by morning. If she stamps Lot 14 "abandoned" at six, she keeps the job and
the notebook. He hands her the stamp.
**Turns:** her choice is now a physical object in her hand.
**Line:** NOAKES: "Filing that would be courageous." MIRA: "Is that a no?" NOAKES: "It's the
polite word for one."
**Moon:** none. · **Visual:** close-up dialogue, plus an insert of the stamp.

**19. Customs corridor** · Mira, Jax · B/C · 60 s
Each of them tries to make the other be brave. He pitches her to file the contract; she points
out that he wants her to be brave so he can be rich. It is all friction, and for the first time
each of them sees the other clearly. She asks what happened eight years ago, and he gives her
the only honest answer he has.
**Turns:** a crack of truth between them, and the second plant of the blank record.
**Line:** MIRA: "You want me to be brave so you can be rich." JAX: "I want everyone to be brave.
I'm just not in a position to lead by example." / MIRA: "What happened eight years ago?" JAX
(a real beat): "I don't know." MIRA: "That's the first true thing you've said to me."
**Moon:** none. · **Visual:** close-up dialogue, a walk-and-talk done as alternating singles.

**20. Cargo hold** · Lucía, Jax, PIP · B · 65 s
Lucía is packing her bag. If Jax doesn't sign she walks, and the Baron himself told her to. Jax
starts to sell her, and for once she lets him finish before saying no. PIP gives his one moral
opinion: "Deliver it. It has been trying for twenty years." Jax says he'll sign.
**Turns:** Jax decides to take the money, and the audience believes him.
**Line:** LUCÍA: "You promise like it's money, Jax. It isn't money. I've checked." / JAX: "I'll
sign." LUCÍA: "…Good." (She doesn't look as if it is.)
**Moon:** none. · **Visual:** illustrated interior. A loud scene, placed against the quiet one that follows.

**21. The rim outside the port, Earth on the horizon** · Jax, Baron · A/C · 120 s
This is the quiet scene. The two of them sit outside in pressure suits on a private radio
channel. Earth doesn't rise here; it waits, low on the horizon. The Baron asks Jax to sign:
being relisted and tidy is better than fading to nobody. Jax, who never listens to anything,
asks for one story and listens to all of it: the cannonball. "I rode one out. Halfway I had
doubts, and I am not ashamed of doubts, so I changed to one coming the other way." The Baron
makes his promise and gives Jax the silver hatchet. Then he looks at him properly.
**Turns:** the Baron has asked to be sold, and Jax, for the first time, has heard something to
the end. (This plants the episode 10 cavalry and Jax's past.)
**Line:** BARON: "From here it never rises. It just waits, low, like a dog at a door." / BARON:
"If ever you need me, truly, I will come. By cannonball. It is undignified and very fast."
JAX: "That's not a real promise." BARON: "Young man, it is the only kind I make." / BARON: "You
lie like a man who once had to lie for his life."
**Moon:** Earth low on the horizon at the pole [gen]; vacuum, so radio only, and the silence
around their voices [gen]. · **Visual:** real footage wide (Earth over a lunar limb, from the
Artemis/Orion assets in location 02, re-framed as a horizon plate; this needs a check in the
footage audit), then close-ups through visors.

**22. Customs hall: the signing** · Jax, Baron, Chichikov, Mira, Noakes, Lucía, PIP · A/B/C · 120 s
It is 17:45 and everyone is around one desk. The Baron signs the Voss contract and the ink
*stays*. His coat warms back to its real colour, so the villain was right. Chichikov,
pleasantly: "Tell the Captain how you came to the Moon." The Baron opens his mouth for the
beanstalk and what comes out is "I took a scheduled shuttle." He tries again, but the contract
won't let the real story out. Jax's pen is touching the paper. He puts it down. "Both or
neither." The relisting is void, the Baron's colour drains away, and he has never looked happier.
**Turns (END OF ACT THREE):** Jax gives up the fee. Chichikov: "Then at six I buy him for the
price of a sandwich." Jax: "It's quarter to." He grabs the Baron and the parcel, and Noakes says
"Seal the port."
**Line:** BARON (tidy, appalled at himself): "I took a scheduled shuttle. Economy. It was
perfectly pleasant."
**Moon:** none. · **Visual:** close-up dialogue, with one composed wide of the whole table.

### ACT FOUR: "Between the Light and the Dark" (27:10–34:10)

**23. Corridor to the airlock** · Lucía, Baron, Jax · A · 35 s
Lucía lifts the Baron over her head, wrestling-style, and carries him down the corridor in
one-sixth gravity, three metres a stride, with Jax and the parcel behind.
**Turns:** they are out of the hall.
**Line:** LUCÍA: "I wrestled in a third of a g. This is a holiday." BARON (aloft): "Madam, I have
been carried from battlefields by worse."
**Moon:** one-sixth gravity [gen]. · **Visual:** illustrated. A single held hero pose, cutting
between leaps; no fluid run cycle needed.

**24. Rim road, the chase** · Jax, Lucía, Baron (in the post-chaise) · A · 55 s
The rover tears along the white rim with two customs crawlers behind. Outside everything is
silent, so the first crawler draws alongside without a sound. The Baron delivers a magnificent
battle speech to the pursuers with his radio switched off.
**Turns:** a third crawler blocks the road ahead.
**Line:** LUCÍA: "Your radio's off." BARON: "I know. They are not worthy of the words. But I
needed to say them."
**Moon:** no sound in a vacuum [gen]; the sunlit rim [gen/file]. · **Visual:** real footage wide
plus illustrated vehicles. Vehicles on terrain are the cheapest action we can do.

**25. Customs hall** · Noakes, Mira, Chichikov (silent) · B/C · 40 s
To lock the docking clamp on *The Unscheduled*, Noakes needs authority from the Atlas office on
Earth, and every exchange costs 2.6 seconds of nothing. Mira watches the clock with the stamp
in her hand. The clamp needs two keys, and one of them is hers.
**Turns:** Mira sees that the escape depends on her.
**Line:** NOAKES (into the void): "…Yes. … … No. … … Yes, I'll hold."
**Moon:** light delay, which comes back as a weapon [gen]. · **Visual:** close-up dialogue.

**26. The inner edge of the rim** · Jax, Lucía, Baron · A · 35 s
Boxed in, Jax steers off the sunlit road and over the inner lip, down into shadow that has never
been lit. It goes black at once.
**Turns:** they are blind in the coldest place on the Moon, and the heater begins to fail.
**Line:** JAX: "Everyone hold on to something." BARON: "I am holding on to my opinion that this
is a mistake."
**Moon:** the edge of permanent shadow [file]. · **Visual:** real footage wide, then a hard cut to black.

**27. Shackleton floor** · Jax, Lucía, Baron, plus Mira on the radio · A/B · 80 s
The sensors are useless and frost creeps over the windscreen. The headlamps show three metres
of grey and then nothing. Then Mira's voice comes over the open customs channel. Nobody has ever
photographed this floor, but it has been mapped by laser from orbit, and she knows the map by
heart. She talks Jax through the dark, boulder by boulder, to the ramp road up to the dock, and
Noakes is listening to every word.
**Turns:** Mira has chosen being right, in public.
**Line:** MIRA: "Left eleven degrees. Stop. Four metres to your right the ground drops three
hundred metres. I'd like you not to find out." JAX: "How sure are you?" MIRA: "I've been sure
for six years. You're the first one who's had to listen."

laser altimetry (LOLA) [file]; extreme cold [file]. · **Visual:** mostly black, faces lit by the

## 6. Running-gag candidates

1. **PIP's violation reports.** It starts small: in the cold open he files on Jax for saying
   "take it" five times when once is binding. In 2 he files on the Atlas itself ("withdrawn
   without notice to the carrier"), and in 16 on Chichikov ("entry without a door"). In 30 he
   files on himself and recommends his own dismissal. The targets grow each time, and the last
   one gets *read*. Over the season it moves from joke to plot (ep 4 to 10).
2. **Nobody can hold on to Chichikov.** In 5 Jax can't read the name on his card and PIP logs
   him as "man, average". In 12 Noakes calls him "Mr Chichester" and is told "Close enough". In
   16 he has been sitting in the room for an unknown length of time. In the tag he finally has a
   frame to himself. Later: his description on warrants reads "medium", and he is in the
   background of shots for weeks before anyone notices. The payoff comes when someone *does*
   remember him exactly (Veda, who memorises everything), and it is chilling.
3. **The Baron corrects Earth's version.** In 6: "my pigtail, and the horse, Earth has no respect
   for horses". In 8, the King of the Moon. In 13: "Moon-dented". In 21, the cannonball: "Earth
   says I rode it there and back. I rode it there. Back, I took a different cannonball." It turns
   painful in 22, when the contract forces the tidy version out of his own mouth. Later it flips:
   in ep 2 Kaguya corrects *his* version of *her*.

(The reserve, if one of these fails: Lucía's wage count, "eight months" becoming "nine months"
and then "don't make it ten", carried into a running tally across the season.)

## 7. The five things I'm least sure about

1. **The source of the cannonball and pigtail stories.** Both come from Bürger's 1786 German
   edition, not from Raspe's English text; I checked the Gutenberg Raspe. Bürger is public
   domain too. Can we treat "the Munchausen books" as a whole, or should the Baron stick to Raspe
   only (hatchet, horn, Gibraltar, King of the Moon)? If it's Raspe only, the ep 10 cavalry
   becomes Raspe's two cannonballs meeting in mid-air, and the swamp joke in scene 6 needs
   replacing.
2. **Does Jax see "nobody" in the tag?** As written, he reads it and says "Leave it." That gives
   him a flicker of knowing, which may be too early. The alternative: he laughs and orders a
   correction, PIP files it, and the correction comes back reading *nobody* again, which only
   the audience sees.
3. **Superintendent Noakes, or Auditor Pell?** Noakes is a new, disposable customs officer.
   Using Pell instead would save a role, seed Seo's arrival in ep 2 (Pell has met the crew), and
   put the "that would be courageous" idiom in the right mouth. But it spends Pell's first
   appearance on a customs desk.
4. **Lucía's mother's account in Chichikov's offer.** It is the strongest temptation in the
   episode, but it opens Voss's water debt six episodes before ep 7. Is the early plant welcome,
   or would you rather I offered her something smaller in ep 1 (a Voss engineer's contract,
   paid on time)?
5. **How much of the parcel to show.** The warmth, the twenty years, the 32 labels and the Baron
   as "the last door", plus the fake-out where they leave the Baron on the Moon before the
   stowaway reveal. Is that too much of a signpost toward the olive wood, and does the fake-out
   cost us a good goodbye, or earn a better tag?

### Production notes for the ruling
- **Real footage carries** 2, 9, 21, 24, 26 and 29, plus the bridge windows. Everything at
  ground level is illustration over terrain-derived plates, as the location file says it must
  be. The crater floor (27) is mostly black plus a real laser-terrain visualisation, so it is
  our cheapest set and our most striking.
- **No crowds** (the hall is empty on purpose, and that is the point). **No hand-to-hand
  fighting**: Lucía's lift and Jax's throw are single held poses. The action is vehicles on
  terrain.
- **Voiceover** is three short lines (9, 30, plus none in the cold open), well under the 12% limit.
- **Things the science check must confirm** (all marked [gen] in section 3): the 1.3 s delay,
  one-sixth gravity, near-constant sunlight on the rim, Earth low on the horizon at the pole,
  and whether orbital Earthrise footage can be re-framed honestly as a horizon plate.
