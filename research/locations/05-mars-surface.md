## Three physical facts that could drive a story problem

- Curiosity captured Mars's first colour sunset sequence over seven minutes on 15 April 2015 and showed the sky near the Sun turns blue at sunset. Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/11875/
- Mars is a dusty, cold desert world with a very thin atmosphere. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/
- InSight recorded the sound of a meteoroid striking Mars (NASA library video). Source: https://images.nasa.gov/details/JPL-20220919-INSIGHf-0001-InSight%20Captures%20Sound%20of%20a%20Meteoroid%20Striking%20Mars
