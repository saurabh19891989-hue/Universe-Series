## Three physical facts that could drive a story problem

- Jupiter has the shortest day in the solar system, about 9.9 hours. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/jupiter/facts/
- The Great Red Spot is a storm bigger than Earth that has raged for hundreds of years, and NASA found it is shrinking while growing taller and deepening in colour. Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/12878/
- Hubble found winds in the outermost lane of the Great Red Spot are accelerating. Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/13939/
