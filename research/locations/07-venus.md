## Three physical facts that could drive a story problem

- Venus surface temperature is about 467 C (872 F), hot enough to melt lead, under crushing pressure and corrosive acid clouds. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/venus/facts/
- Magellan radar imaged more than 98 percent of the surface of Venus (1990-1994). Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/30358/
- Maat Mons is a large, currently dormant volcano seen in the Magellan terrain tour; Maxwell Montes is the highest range. Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/3728/
