## Three physical facts that could drive a story problem

- Saturn's rings are thought to be pieces of comets, asteroids or shattered moons torn apart by gravity, and Goddard found they are disappearing through 'ring rain'. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/saturn/facts/
- Titan has clouds, rain, rivers, lakes and seas of liquid methane and ethane. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/saturn/moons/titan/
- Enceladus jets spew water vapour and ice; plume organic density was about 20 times what scientists expected. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/saturn/moons/enceladus/
