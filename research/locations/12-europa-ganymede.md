## Three physical facts that could drive a story problem

- Ganymede is the only moon known with its own magnetic field, which causes auroras around its poles. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/jupiter/moons/ganymede/
- Europa may be one of the most promising places to find present-day environments suitable for life; Europa Clipper launched 14 Oct 2024. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/moons/europa/
- Hubble found evidence of water vapour in Ganymede's atmosphere. Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/13892/
