## Three physical facts that could drive a story problem

- Earth's atmosphere is 78% nitrogen and 21% oxygen, and air molecules glowing under solar particles cause the aurorae. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/facts/
- Earth rotates once every 23.9 hours and its axis is tilted 23.4 degrees, driving day-night sweeps of city lights. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/facts/
- The SVS Earth-at-night composite was assembled from nine days of Suomi NPP data in April 2012 and thirteen days in October 2012: night lights are a patchwork across weeks, not one instant. Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/30082/
