## Gaps (must be illustrated, not shown for real)

- Real camera imagery of the inside of Shackleton: the floor is in permanent shadow, so there are no ordinary photographs; only LRO/LROC low-light and LOLA laser-derived views exist.
- Any surface-level view of the south pole (no rover has operated there), so characters standing on the rim are illustration over terrain-derived plates.

## Three physical facts that could drive a story problem

- Permanently shadowed regions near the lunar poles have not seen the Sun in millions or even billions of years because the Moon's tilt is only 1.6 degrees, making them some of the coldest, darkest places in the solar system, and likely water traps. Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4043/
- Over two lunar days (about two Earth months) the shadows near the south pole sweep across the terrain, so light and dark patches move over weeks. Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4893/
- The Moon completes one orbit and one rotation in the same time (27 Earth days), so a lunar day is about a month long. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/
