## Three physical facts that could drive a story problem

- Io is the most volcanically active world in the solar system, with hundreds of volcanoes and lava fountains dozens of miles high. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/jupiter/moons/io/
- Io has lakes of molten silicate lava, and its volcanoes are sometimes bright enough to be seen with large telescopes on Earth. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/jupiter/moons/io/
- Voyager 1 spotted the first signs of Io's volcanism in 1979. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/jupiter/moons/io/
