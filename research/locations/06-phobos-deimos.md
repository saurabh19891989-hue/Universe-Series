## Three physical facts that could drive a story problem

- Phobos is about 27 x 22 x 18 km, was nearly shattered by a giant impact (Stickney crater), and is covered in gouges. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/mars/moons/phobos/
- Solar wind interaction with Phobos creates a complex electrical environment that could affect future exploration. Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/20252/
- Phobos photobombed a Hubble picture of Mars (it moves visibly in Hubble time-lapses, about 10 s clips). Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/11946/
