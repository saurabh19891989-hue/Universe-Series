## Three physical facts that could drive a story problem

- The Moon rotates at the same rate it orbits (27 Earth days), so we only ever see one face: the far side is permanently hidden from Earth. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/
- Artemis II's crew trajectory swings around the Moon's far side (simulated in the SVS visualisation of the April 6, 2026 flyby). Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5633/
- Artemis II saw impact flashes on the far side of the Moon (SVS page title; real events in the data). Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/15103/
