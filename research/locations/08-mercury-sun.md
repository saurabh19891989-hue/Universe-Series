## Three physical facts that could drive a story problem

- Mercury day temperatures reach 430 C and drop to -180 C at night because there is no atmosphere to retain heat. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/mercury/facts/
- The Sun's core is about 15 million C and its diameter about 1.4 million km. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/sun/facts/
- Parker Solar Probe has flown through the Sun's corona and sampled particles and magnetic fields: a spacecraft has touched the Sun. Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/14045/
