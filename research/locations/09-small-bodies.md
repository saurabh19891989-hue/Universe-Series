## Gaps (must be illustrated, not shown for real)

- Ceres, Vesta, Ryugu and 67P as high-resolution video: not found in verified sources (ESA Rosetta items are on esa.int with non-commercial default terms; JAXA Hayabusa2 terms need permission).
- The asteroid belt itself is empty space; any busy belt image is artist concept.

## Three physical facts that could drive a story problem

- Ceres makes up about 25% of the asteroid belt's mass yet Pluto is still 14 times more massive; Ceres surface salts are magnesium sulfate-type minerals. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/dwarf-planets/ceres/facts/
- OSIRIS-REx found boulders on Bennu that originated from asteroid Vesta, evidence that Bennu is a rubble pile. Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/13707/
- Dawn's arrival at Ceres in 2015 made it the first dwarf planet visited by a spacecraft. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/dwarf-planets/ceres/facts/
