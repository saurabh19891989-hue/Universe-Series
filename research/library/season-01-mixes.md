# Season 1 — mix recipes from the Story Library

Research draft, 27 Nov 2026. Each recipe names what is taken from each source (mechanism, image or trait), how the pieces combine into something in none of them, and — clearly marked — **PROPOSAL FOR SHOWRUNNER** where the reading suggests a better idea than the current outline in `canon/season-01.md`. Card numbers refer to `research/library/sources/`. Nothing is adapted scene by scene; every plot below is ours.

## 1. The Parcel Nobody Ordered — caper/chase, Moon south pole, guest: Baron Munchausen

**Sources (4 cultures):** Raspe/Bürger, German-English (01) · Gogol, Russian (02) · Thousand and One Nights, Arabic (08) · Homer, Greek (15) · Popol Vuh, K'iche' Maya (18, gag only).

| From | Taken |
|---|---|
| 01 Munchausen | **Mechanism:** the post-horn whose tunes freeze in the cold and play by themselves when thawed. **Image:** the silver hatchet found on the Moon in a heap of chaff. **Trait:** narrates to a table, dares doubters to leave the room. |
| 02 Dead Souls | **Mechanism:** buy what the register says exists; frame it as *relief* ("I take the burden off you"). **Trait:** Chichikov's euphemism ladder — he never says "delisted", only "non-existent". **Backstory:** he was the state's most zealous customs officer. |
| 08 Sindbad, voyage 1 | **Mechanism:** the man declared dead finds bales marked with his name being sold "for his family", and must prove he is himself by telling his story. |
| 15 Odyssey IX | **Mechanism:** the curse specifies "home in another man's ship" — the cold-open repossession. |
| 18 Popol Vuh | **Gag:** the message relayed by nested carriers (louse in toad in snake in bird) — the parcel's routing history. |

**How they combine:** Shackleton's floor is in permanent shadow, and the permanently shadowed craters near the lunar south pole are among the coldest places ever measured in the Solar System (check exact figures against `research/locations/01-moon-south-pole.md`). The Baron's stories haven't stopped coming true — *they have frozen*. Everything he has claimed since he arrived is stored, silent, in the dark. Chichikov arrives at the customs desk offering to pay the lien in exchange for "relieving" the crew of a parcel that is legally non-existent (its address no longer exists). Mira, at the desk, recognises a customs man's courtesy — the way he searches without seeming to. In the crater-rim chase the ship crosses into a peak of near-permanent sunlight, and one of the Baron's frozen boasts thaws mid-air and comes true at the worst moment. That is "the one impossible thing" — late, and not the one anyone wanted.

**PROPOSAL FOR SHOWRUNNER (1a):** make the Baron's "proof" a *delayed* truth, not a demonstration: something he said in act 1 in the shadow happens in act 4 in the light. It gives the episode a ticking device rooted in a real lunar property (shadow vs sunlit rim), and sets the rule for all his later lies: they come true *when they thaw*.

**PROPOSAL FOR SHOWRUNNER (1b):** the cold-open repossession should be explicit that the ship is now legally someone else's — the Odyssey's "another man's ship" planted in minute one. Jax flies home in a ship that is not his for the whole season.

**PROPOSAL (1c):** the cannonball promise. In the German original, the Baron rides a cannonball, has second thoughts halfway, and *jumps to a ball flying back the other way*. So in ep 1 the Baron should promise not "I'll come on a cannonball" but "I'll come back on the return shot" — and in ep 10 the pay-off is a mid-air swap (he arrives, and someone leaves on the return). Truer to the source and more useful than a simple cavalry entrance.

## 2. The Princess of the Far Side — siege, far side / L2, guest: Kaguya

**Sources (5 cultures):** Bamboo Cutter, Japanese (04) · Ariosto, Italian (03) · Pu Songling, Chinese (19) · Gessar, Mongolian (20) · Hong Gildong, Korean (17).

| From | Taken |
|---|---|
| 04 Bamboo Cutter | **Mechanism:** the siege that cannot win — two thousand bows, every door opens by itself. The impossible task as a lie detector; the forged branch exposed by unpaid craftsmen. **Image:** the feather robe ("who dons this robe changes his heart"); she finishes writing before it touches her. **Trait:** Kaguya enjoys exposures; she is an examiner, not a victim. |
| 03 Orlando 34–35 | **Image:** the Valley's inventory (shouting bladders of dead empires, labelled flasks of wits). **Mechanism:** name-plates thrown by Time into Lethe; crows grab and drop them; two swans carry a few to a pillar in a temple. **Trait:** Astolfo takes back his own wits without fuss — then loses them again. |
| 19 Pu Songling | **Image:** the moon-lady's song, "too lonely and cold is my home in the moon" — Kaguya's neighbour. |
| 20 Gessar | **Mechanism:** forgetting brought on by comfort after victory (a second, different forgetting than the robe). |
| 17 Hong Gildong | **Name:** Seo's cover papers carry the name of a man the law refused to recognise. |

**How they combine:** Enforcement jams the relay and settles in — the Mikado's guard reversed: this time the besiegers are the state, and Kaguya calmly explains that no siege has ever held her. To decide whether to help, she sets each crew member an impossible task. Jax, the seller, produces a perfect fake. Mid-presentation, Lucía, eight months unpaid, arrives with her own invoice for the parts she machined into it — the craftsmen's petition — and Kaguya laughs for the first time in centuries. Seo, the only one who solves her task honestly, earns Kaguya's trust; the audience knows by the tag that it was the rat who passed the honesty test. In the Valley the crew see the "river" where delisted names fall — a crater of discarded Atlas tags, with Voss salvage drones like Ariosto's crows grabbing names and dropping most. Astolfo is there with a jar; on a back shelf, unnoticed, the jar labelled *Nobody*.

**PROPOSAL (2a):** replace "Seo's flying saves the ship twice" as her only proof with *Kaguya's test*: Seo is the one person who will not fake an impossible thing. The rat is the honest one — a betrayal "between two loves" set up from her first episode.

**PROPOSAL (2b):** use Ariosto's name-plates and swans as the in-world *origin myth* of the Atlas: Kaguya tells it; Oyuna (ep 4) later turns out to come from a "swan" family. It gives the delisting world a poetic rule the show never has to explain (rule 5: nobody explains the Storied).

**PROPOSAL (2c):** the robe's price. Kaguya offers the robe as the way out of the siege — whoever wears it can walk through the blockade because they no longer want anything the jammers can track (a desire-free signature). Jax reaches for it; Kaguya stops him: "you have worn one before." First clue, straight from the text's "who dons this robe changes his heart".

## 3. The Knight of the Red Planet — western/road movie, Valles Marineris to Olympus Mons, guest: Don Quixote and Sancho

**Sources (4 cultures):** Cervantes, Spanish (05) · Munchausen *Sequel*, English (01) · Sindbad voyage 7, Arabic (08) · Homer, Greek (15).

| From | Taken |
|---|---|
| 05 Don Quixote | **Mechanisms:** cure by defeat (the Knight of the White Moon); sanity as a terminal illness; Sancho's inverted lie (he points at plain reality and swears it is enchanted, and the madman sees the truth); Sancho's offer to falsify the record ("I girthed Rocinante badly"); the sworn affidavit by a character from a *fake* version. **Image:** sails beginning to turn in a sudden wind as he charges. **Trait:** Quixote's courtesy — "not saying you lie, but that you are not correct". |
| 01 Munchausen *Sequel* | **Mechanism:** a 1792 text in which Quixote charges another legend's procession — precedent for Quixote attacking the Voss survey convoy. |
| 08 Sindbad 7 | **Trait (for Idris):** his claimed ancestor ended his career as a reluctant courier who shot elephants with a bow and had his stories written in gold. |
| 15 Odyssey XVII | **Image:** Argos on the dung heap by the stable — the dog's fixed spot. |

**How they combine:** Voss surveyors already used Cervantes' cure on Quixote: a polite "Knight" beat him in a staged duel and made him promise to stay home; he has been sane, dying and filing tax returns ever since. The crew cannot simply re-madden him (Mira objects). Instead Sancho and Idris compete in lies — Sancho's inverted method: they don't show Quixote a giant, they show him the plain truth (a dust devil) and insist it is enchanted, and he — the realist now — refuses to believe *them*, gets angry, and to prove them wrong charges it. On the charge he sees giants again. The second half turns on paperwork: Voss has licensed a tidy "Heritage Quixote" (Avellaneda's fake sequel, in our terms). The crew get the actor who plays him to swear before a Martian magistrate that the old man is the real one — Álvaro Tarfe's affidavit — and that sworn statement is what lets La Mancha Station be re-entered in the Atlas.

**PROPOSAL (3a):** the "first legend un-thinned" should be done *legally*, through the affidavit from the fake, not only emotionally through the charge. It establishes a season rule (a listing can be restored by testimony) that ep 10 can then abuse or honour.

**PROPOSAL (3b):** give the dog an exact Argos beat: it lies by the hydroponics drain on the station, ignores everyone, and stands only when Jax walks past. Jax wipes his eye when nobody is looking and asks about "that fine animal" as a stranger would.

## 4. The Phobos Exception — farce, Phobos and Deimos, guest: Nasreddin

**Sources (5 cultures):** Nasreddin (Turkish chapbook, 06) · Journey to the West, Chinese (12) · Pu Songling, Chinese (19) · Gogol, Russian (16) · Gessar, Mongolian (20) · Anansi formula, Akan (11).

| From | Taken |
|---|---|
| 06 Nasreddin | **Mechanisms:** the cauldron that gave birth and died (hold the other side to its own absurd rule); "the dead came out for a walk"; the two-doored house; the Cadi who tests the rule on himself; the burden of proof shifted ("come and count"). **Lines:** "I neither buy nor sell the moon"; old moons become stars. |
| 12 Journey to the West | **Mechanism:** the gate that cannot admit you until you hold an official position; the job with "no rank — the position before any rank". |
| 19 Pu Songling | **Mechanism:** the humane furlough found by checking the Book of Fate; a substitute serves in your place. |
| 16 The Nose | **Mechanism:** the office that will not record your loss. |
| 11 Anansi | **Formula:** "My hand has touched it; what remains still remains" — the stamp clerk's receipt. |
| 20 Gessar | **Repertoire:** what Oyuna sings — a hero restored by a delivered message. |

**How they combine:** Phobos's real property is ridiculous gravity — escape speed around 11 m/s. You can leave Phobos *by jumping*. The ancient exemption means there is no law that lets you leave, so leaving is not *allowed*, though it is physically trivial. The farce: everyone could walk off the moon and nobody may. Nasreddin's lesson is the cauldron: if Phobos is exempt from every law, it is also exempt from the law that lets anyone *hold* you — so Oyuna has never legally been detained, and the form for her release cannot exist. PIP, who loves forms, falls for the waiting-room's registrar; their courtship is a duel of receipts ("my hand has touched it; what remains still remains"). The report nobody can refuse: PIP files Oyuna as deceased — the dead have different rules ("I am one of the buried, out for a walk") — and Pell's office cannot refuse a death notice from a postal-audit unit.

**PROPOSAL (4a):** Nasreddin has not stayed eleven years "out of spite" but because he is somebody's *substitute* (Pu Songling's furlough): he took a post on Phobos so that someone else could go home and look after a dying mother, and the relief never came. Spite is the cover; kindness is the truth. It gives him a want that collides with the job (if Oyuna leaves, the post needs a new substitute).

**PROPOSAL (4b):** use Phobos's real fate (it is spiralling in and will break into a ring) as Nasreddin's running joke: when asked what happens to old moons he says they make forty stars out of each. Act 4 can end with him choosing to stay on a moon that is, literally, falling.

## 5. A Gentleman on Venus — heist/romance, Venus cloud tops, guests: Arsène Lupin, Scheherazade

**Sources (4 cultures):** Leblanc, French (07) · Thousand and One Nights, Arabic/Persian (08) · Journey to the West, Chinese (12) · Verne, French-Indian (14).

| From | Taken |
|---|---|
| 07 Lupin | **Mechanisms:** the theft announced by letter, with a postscript identifying the fakes; the hired detective who is the thief's man; the narrator who is the culprit; the accomplice who chooses (the Kodak dropped between hull and pier). **Image:** the white rose taken and left. |
| 08 Nights frame | **Mechanism:** the planted confederate who asks the question on cue; story as ransom, nested. **Trait:** Scheherazade as scholar with a library, not a seductress. |
| 12 Journey to the West | **Image/irony:** the envoy of the planet Venus who flatters a guest into an empty title. |
| 14 Nemo | **Mechanism:** the collector who lives off what empires lost (Vigo galleons) — mirror for Voss's collection. |

**How they combine:** Lupin's calling card arrives at Voss's gala before anyone else does — a polite letter listing what he will take, with a postscript saying which piece in the collection is a copy. That postscript is the only reliable map to the real fragment. Scheherazade owns the room by a two-person technique: someone in the crowd always asks her the right question at the right time. The crew must work out who her cue-giver is. Idris and Seo, lying to each other all night, are each the other's cue. Voss hires a "detective" for the gala who sleeps through the theft — Lupin's man, as in "Arsène Lupin in Prison". The double-cross that is itself a double: the one person who could expose Lupin (a woman holding the evidence) drops it into the cloud-deck — the Kodak beat, given to Mira about Jax.

**PROPOSAL (5a):** let Lupin narrate the cold open as a fellow guest, unidentified, and reveal at the end that the narrator was Lupin ("The Arrest" structure). The canon allows guest-narrated episodes; this is the purest source for one.

**PROPOSAL (5b):** Voss's line "Follow the parcel" lands harder if Voss has just identified, like Lupin's postscript, which of the crew's claims are genuine and which are copies — the villain is right about one thing: he is the best appraiser in the room.

## 6. Too Close to the Sun — survival, Mercury and the Sun, guests: Icarus and Sampati

**Sources (4 cultures):** Ovid, Roman (09) · Valmiki Ramayana, Indian (10) · Shahnama, Persian (21) · Thousand and One Nights, Arabic (08) · Popol Vuh, Maya (18, image).

| From | Taken |
|---|---|
| 09 Ovid | **Mechanism:** the middle course with *two* failure modes (too low, too high). **Trait:** Icarus flew higher for joy, not defiance. **Secret:** Daedalus killed his nephew Perdix; the partridge that "dreads the higher regions". |
| 10 Sampati | **Mechanisms:** the shield that burns the shielder; a century of grounded waiting; wings regrow when he tells what he has seen. **Trait:** first speaks of eating the strangers — hunger mistaken for menace. **Map:** the stacked layers of the sky. |
| 21 Simurgh | **Mechanism:** the feather to burn when in trouble — a one-use summoning token. |
| 08 Nights | **Mechanism inverted:** a story told not to delay death at dawn but to keep everyone awake through heat stress. |

**How they combine:** The slingshot is a real "middle course": dive too shallow and you miss the speed you need, too deep and the heat kills you. Icarus wants to go in steep — for joy, and to prove the old story wrong. Sampati wants to go in shallow — he once shielded his brother and has been waiting a hundred years for a reason to fly again; his wings (the tender's heat shield) will "grow back" only when he tells what he saw. What he saw from that orbit, with his hundred-league eyes, is who sabotaged the gate. He tells only one person — Seo — and that is the moment his repairs work. Oyuna's all-night story is the Scheherazade engine turned to survival. Lucía and Mira's fix is the partridge's lesson: fly low.

**PROPOSAL (6a):** the quarrel between the two old flyers should be about *who survived*: Icarus (in our canon alive) has always been ashamed that the story says he died, and Sampati that the story forgets he chose to burn. Each thinks the other's legend is the easier one. Both are annoyed at Earth — in opposite directions.

**PROPOSAL (6b):** Sampati's far sight is how Seo learns she has been seen (not Holmes first in ep 9). He says nothing to the crew because his tradition makes the waiter wait. This seeds the ep 9 Holmes line.

**PROPOSAL (6c):** the Daedalus secret (Perdix) belongs to whoever designed the sabotaged gate — a brilliant engineer who once pushed a rival off a tower and called it an accident. Possibly a Voss engineer in S2.

## 7. The Spider's Share — con/sting, Ceres, Vesta, Bennu, guest: Anansi

**Sources (4 cultures):** Akan (11) · Popol Vuh, Maya (18) · Gogol, Russian (02) · Conan Doyle, British (13, image).

| From | Taken |
|---|---|
| 11 Anansi (Rattray) | **Mechanisms:** an impossible price paid in instalments with receipts; staged argument overheard; fake weather; the sticky effigy; the unrequested bonus — he throws in his own mother; the real planner (his wife Aso) uncredited. **Formulas:** "We do not really mean that what we say is true" / "take some elsewhere, and let some come back to me." |
| 11 Anansi (Barker) | **Mechanism:** the pot of wisdom broken by hoarding; "Anansi and Nothing" — winning by wearing another's clothes. |
| 18 Popol Vuh | **Mechanism:** the kill-and-revive show; the lords *ask* for the trick and are not revived; mannequin lords revealed by pricking. |
| 02 Dead Souls | **Mechanism:** financing on assets that exist only on paper (Chichikov's plan to mortgage the dead). |
| 13 Final Problem | **Image:** the spider at the centre of a web feeling every quiver — Voss as the other spider. |

**How they combine:** Anansi's con is the Popol Vuh show staged as finance: he sells the Voss water company a product that "kills" a debt and revives it as an asset (Chichikov's dead souls on a balance sheet). The directors volunteer to try it on their own holdings — and are not revived. The traps are Ananse's, on the asteroids' real terms: Bennu's surface is so loose that a spacecraft's touch sank in like a ball pit — the sticky doll becomes a landing pad that swallows anyone who "slaps" it. The turn ("Anansi's real game"): the price he quietly adds to the deal is Lucía's mother's debt — the bonus mother — so that he walks away with the debt as his own story-asset. Lucía chooses the town over the score.

**PROPOSAL (7a):** make the uncredited planner a running joke and a reveal: every trap of Anansi's was designed by someone else (in our version, Lucía's mother, who has been running small cons against Voss for years). Anansi credits himself; Lucía finds out who really outwitted the company. True to Rattray, where Aso plans everything.

**PROPOSAL (7b):** use the Akan opening and closing formulas as the episode's bookends, spoken by Anansi to camera only in the tag — the one time the show lets a guest frame the story.

## 8. Havoc Under the Mountain — prison break, Jupiter and Io, guest: Sun Wukong

**Sources (4 cultures):** Journey to the West, Chinese (12) · Hong Gildong, Korean (17) · Gessar, Mongolian (20) · Homer, Greek (15) · Pu Songling, Chinese (19) · Popol Vuh (18).

| From | Taken |
|---|---|
| 12 Journey to the West | **Mechanisms:** the Register struck out by its subject (and all his named kin); the post with no rank; the empty title; the palm he never left, proved by his own graffiti on the pillars; the mountain dropped on him. |
| 17 Hong Gildong | **Mechanism:** eight identical copies, each with the same scar; demand the rank you were denied; the trap set for when you bow. |
| 20 Gessar | **Mechanism:** the horse that remembers when the hero doesn't. |
| 15 Odyssey XXI | **Image:** a bow strung like a lyre, its string singing like a swallow. |
| 19 Pu Songling | **Mechanism:** the power that fails the moment you brag. |
| 18 Popol Vuh | **Structure:** houses of ordeal, each with a trick solution. |

**How they combine:** Wukong signed a Voss contract for "Great Sage" — a title "with no rank". The prison under Io's volcano is a set of Popol Vuh houses (heat, radiation, dark, cold vacuum). The breakout runs on Hong Gildong decoys: eight copies of the prisoner, each with his marks, flood Arakawa's sensors. The twist from the palm: after the Red Spot chase, they find Wukong's own scrawl on a "pillar" — they are still inside Jupiter's magnetosphere, Voss's jurisdiction; they never left the palm. The way out is not speed but the Register: Wukong tells them a listing can be struck by its owner, and does it to himself — the first hint that Jax did the same. Jax strings a bow in the escape like a man who has done it a thousand times.

**PROPOSAL (8a):** "the cost of the escape" = one of the decoys is not a decoy. A crew member is badly hurt because they were mistaken for a copy. The Hong Gildong logic turned to pain.

**PROPOSAL (8b):** Wukong's tag line could be sharpened by the source: he struck out *every named monkey*, not only himself. "You've forgotten more than a name, Captain. When I did it, I took my whole family off the list. Ask the archivist who else is missing." It opens a S2 question: who else did Jax delist with himself?

## 9. Murder on the Nautilus — locked room, Europa, guests: Holmes and Nemo

**Sources (4 cultures):** Conan Doyle, British (13) · Verne, French-Indian (14) · Leblanc, French (07) · Valmiki (10) · Homer (15).

| From | Taken |
|---|---|
| 13 Doyle | **Mechanisms:** the locked room as a delivery route (dummy bell-rope, ventilator, bed clamped down); the misread dying word; the friend who reads the scene with the master's method; deliberate forgetting (the brain attic; "Astronomy — Nil"). |
| 14 Verne | **Mechanisms:** identity established by separate testimony while pretending not to understand; "I am the law, I am the judge, I am the oppressed". **Fact:** the English translation removed Dakkar's murdered family and inverted his cause. |
| 07 Lupin | **Mechanism:** the observer whose look feels like being photographed; Holmes deducing *how* the thief learned the secret (it was said aloud at dinner). |
| 10 Sampati | **Mechanism:** the far-seer who tells only one person. |
| 15 Odyssey XIX | **Plant:** the scar recognised by touch, the man who turns from the firelight. |

**How they combine:** Under the ice the auditor is found dead in a sealed cabin. The room was sealed; the route was not: a ventilation line to the next cabin and a bunk bolted to the deck so that whatever came down the line had to reach him (Speckled Band logic, translated to a submarine). Holmes is thinning and knows it: his famous attic is emptying without his consent — he can't remember which planet they are orbiting, which once he would have forgotten on purpose. Mira lends him her method — Watson at Reichenbach. Nemo already knows who did it; he let each passenger tell their story separately and compared them, as he did with Aronnax. The solution nobody wanted: the auditor carried Nemo's Directorate file, and the file is a *translation* that deletes the reason for his war — exactly what happened to Verne's English text. The killer is someone who could not stand to see the lie filed again.

**PROPOSAL (9a):** make the falsified file the motive (straight from the Verne finding). It is serious, it dignifies Nemo (per canon), and it ties the murder to the series theme: who edits a legend's record, and what it costs.

**PROPOSAL (9b):** Holmes's private line to Seo already exists; the medical bay gives a second, quieter beat: Holmes notices Jax turn his leg away from the scanner light, and says nothing — a scar he files for later.

## 10. A Seat at the Table — dynasty drama/finale, Saturn and Titan, the Voss family

**Sources (6 cultures):** Homer, Greek (15) · Ariosto, Italian (03) · Gogol, Russian (16 and 02) · Munchausen/Bürger, German-English (01) · Gessar, Mongolian (20) · Cervantes, Spanish (05) · Thousand and One Nights (08).

| From | Taken |
|---|---|
| 15 Odyssey XXIII, IX, I | **Mechanisms:** the bed test by lie (Penelope claims the bed was moved); the olive can't be moved unless cut at the roots; Calypso is Atlas's daughter; the curse "find trouble in his house". |
| 03 Ariosto 35 | **Argument (Voss's):** poets made heroes great because heirs paid them; read Homer "by contraries" — Penelope unfaithful. The villain is right about one thing: legends are bought. |
| 16 The Nose | **Mechanism:** your own part, in a better uniform, travelling on papers in another name, outranking you, refusing to reattach. |
| 02 Dead Souls | **Trait:** Chichikov at the table, still polite. |
| 01 Bürger | **Mechanism:** the cannonball swap mid-air (see 1c). |
| 20 Gessar | **Mechanism:** the message that arrives and lifts years of forgetting. |
| 05 Don Quixote | **Mechanism:** Sancho's offer to falsify the record for love — Seo's choice. |
| 08 Sindbad 7 | **Image:** a gift-bed carried as diplomatic cargo; stories written in gold and locked in the treasury (Voss's archive). |

**How they combine:** Dinner is Ariosto's argument made by Soren Voss: every legend at the table is what someone paid for, and he simply pays better; Earth's Homer was a contractor. Seo's exposure and choice is Sancho's: she can save the crew by falsifying the record she has kept on them — choosing one love over another. When the parcel is opened, the living olive wood carries a terrible implication only Mira works out: if this is part of the bed, someone *cut the tree at its roots*. The bed can no longer pass its own test. Voss owns the rights to "whoever Jax was" the way the nose owned a better rank: his story is out there in a uniform, outranking him. The Baron arrives on the cannonball — and at the swap point someone leaves on the return shot.

**PROPOSAL (10a):** keep Mira as the one who understands the olive: she is the scientist, and the line is a fact, not a revelation. "You don't post a piece of a bed built round a living tree unless somebody cut the tree." It hurts in two ways at once (for Penelope; for Mira).

**PROPOSAL (10b):** the tag ("a woman at a loom stops weaving and looks up") gains a source-true edge: Penelope has been tested by lying stories for twenty years and has a test ready. Let her look up because a piece of her bed has stopped being where it was. She knows before anyone tells her.

**PROPOSAL (10c):** Voss's archive of "stories written in letters of gold and laid in the treasury" (Sindbad 7) is where the rights to Jax are kept — a visual for S2's heist: steal back a man's own story.

## Cross-season motifs the reading surfaced
- **Nobody / Noman / Nemo / "Nothing":** Homer's Noman, Verne's Nemo ("no one"), and Barker's Akan tale of a rich man called Nothing whose clothes Anansi wears and whose place he takes. Three cultures, one dangerous name. Ep 9 (Nobody meets Nemo) and ep 7 (Anansi knew a Nothing once) can both touch it lightly.
- **Scars as identity proof, and their failure:** Odysseus (above the knee), Lupin (right forearm), Hong Gildong (left thigh, shared by eight copies).
- **Delisting myths:** Ariosto's name-plates in Lethe; Wukong's Register; Pu Songling's Book of Fate furlough; Nemo's "forget that you had ever existed".
- **Forgetting by gift:** Kaguya's robe, Gessar's victory drink, Calypso's blandishments — three versions of the kind of forgetting Jax may have chosen.
