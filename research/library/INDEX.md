## Most useful findings for the showrunner
1. The Munchausen cannonball ride is Bürger's German addition, and in it the Baron **turns back halfway**.
2. The English *Mysterious Island* (Gutenberg #1268) deletes the killing of Nemo's family and recasts 1857 as an "atrocious mutiny"; the French makes him Tipu Sultan's nephew and a hero of independence.
3. Ariosto's Moon contains a ready-made delisting myth (name-plates thrown into Lethe; swans save a few) and an argument that Homer lied about Penelope.
4. Sindbad's seventh voyage (Galland/Lang) is a reluctant courier job delivering a royal gift that includes a bed, ending with him as an archer.
5. Hong Gildong defeats identification by scar with eight straw copies.
6. Barker's Akan "Anansi and Nothing": a rich man named Nothing whose clothes and place Anansi takes.
7. Calypso, who keeps Odysseus forgetting home, is the daughter of Atlas.
