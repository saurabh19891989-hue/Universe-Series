# SECRET UNIVERSE - Guest Legend Character Bank

Research draft, 27 November 2026. Companion data file: `character-bank.json` (same entries, all fields).

**This is not legal advice.** Rights readings below apply the general rules as of late 2026: in the **USA**, works first published before 1 January 1931 are public domain; in **EU/UK** (life+70), works by authors who died in 1955 or earlier are public domain; in **India** (life+60), authors who died in 1965 or earlier. Anonymous folk and ancient works have no author copyright anywhere, **but every modern translation, retelling, illustration, film, game and TV look is a separate protected work**, and trademarks can outlive copyright. A media lawyer should clear any guest before broadcast, starting with the "medium" and "low" confidence entries.

How to read the space locations: each one is a real place or object with a real property that rhymes with the legend. They are story hooks, not claims that the legend "explains" the science.
